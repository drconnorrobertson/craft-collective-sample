Nails

Nails

LIZ

​

Classic Manicure
This is a manicure for those who prefer a regular nail polish or no polish. The cuticles are removed, nails are filed and polished, and hands are massaged with Aveda lotion. (30 minutes)

 

Gel Manicure
This manicure includes removal of previous gel that was applied at this salon, cuticle care, a clean gel application and a hand massage with Aveda hand lotion. (45-60 minutes)

 

Structure Gel Manicure
This Manicure is for those who would like to grow their natural nails or healthily maintain their long
natural nails. Previous polish is removed, nails are filed to desired length, cuticles are removed and nails are filled and sculpted. (75 minutes)

 

Classic Pedicure 
This pedicure includes trimming and shaping of nail, cuticle care, filing of calluses and dead skin, sugar scrub, massage and polish. (45-60 minutes)

 

Spa Pedicure
This pedicure includes trimming and shaping of nail, cuticle care, filing of calluses and dead skin, sugar scrub, warm foot mask, hot towels and a 10 minute massage. (60-75 minutes)

 

Callus Removal Pedicure
This pedicure includes trimming and shaping of nail, cuticle care, application of enzyme for deep dead skin removal, filing of calluses and dead skin, sugar scrub, warm foot mask, hot towels and a 10 minute massage. (90 minutes)

 

Callus Removal Add On
Add a callus removal treatment to your classic pedicure.

 

Gel Polish Change on Toes
This includes removal of previous polish on toes, trimming and shaping of nails and gel polish.

 

Gel Add On Pedicure
Add Gel Polish to your pedicure.

 

French Add On
Add a French manicure design to your manicure or pedicure. (only for gel)

 

Gel X Manicure
This manicure will add length with the Apres Gel Extension system. Gel Extensions are applied over the natural nail to your desired length. They are fitted and filed to make them look like they are your natural nails.
 

Nail Art
This service will provide a simple nail design on one or two fingers per hand. For more detailed designs please consult with your nail technician prior to making the appointment.

​Acrylic Full Set
This service includes cuticle care, acrylic application, basic gel polish and can add length to, orjust overlay your natural nails.
 

Acrylic Fill

This service is to rebalance a previous acrylic service, including cuticle care and a
basic gel polish.
 

Hard Gel Full Set

This service is good for clients who just want an overlay to grow out their
natural nails and make their manicures last longer. This includes cuticle care, hard gel
application and a basic gel polish.
 

Hard Gel Fill

 This service is to rebalance a previous hard gel manicure. This includes
rebalancing your previous manicure, cuticle care, hard gel fill in and a basic gel polish.
