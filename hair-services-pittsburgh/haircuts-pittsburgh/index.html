Craft Collective Salon Group Services

Craft Collective Salon Group provides a complete range of modern hair services designed for guests who want elevated results, healthier hair, and a customized experience. Our salon focuses on advanced education, precision technique, and a personalized consultation for every appointment. As one of the top hair salons in the North Hills and greater Pittsburgh area, we are known for natural color, blonding, dimensional highlights, extensions, smoothing treatments, and detail focused haircuts.

​

Haircuts and Shaping

Every haircut is tailored to your face shape, lifestyle, and styling routine. Our stylists create personalized looks with soft movement, seamless texture, and precision lines. We offer women’s cuts, men’s cuts, long layer shaping, short hair transformations, and maintenance trims designed to grow out beautifully between visits.

​

Color Services

Our colorists specialize in natural dimensional color and modern color techniques. Whether you want subtle brightness or a dramatic change, we create results that enhance your features while keeping your hair healthy. Services include full color, root coverage, glossing, toning, highlights, lowlights, balayage, lived in color, and complete color transformations.

​

Highlights and Lowlights

Customized foil placements designed to brighten your look, blend regrowth, or add contrast. Our stylists use strategic sectioning and careful placement for consistent, long lasting dimension.

 

Balayage and Hand Painted Color

Balayage offers a soft, sunlit finish with seamless blending from roots to ends. This technique creates low maintenance, natural dimension that grows out softly and maintains tone longer between visits.

​

Lived In Color

Lived in color combines highlights, lowlights, smudging, and toning to create a natural blended finish that requires less frequent maintenance. It is ideal for guests who want dimension without a high upkeep schedule.

​

Full Color and Root Touch Ups

From coverage to rich all over tone, we create consistent results that match your desired shade. Root touch ups are recommended for guests needing regular coverage or smooth blending.

​

Glossing and Toning

These shine boosting services refine tone, correct brassiness, add smoothness, and refresh your color between major appointments.

 

Blonding Services

Craft Collective Salon Group specializes in blonding for all hair types. This includes full blonding, partial blonding, lived in blonde, platinum, bright foils, or soft blended highlights. Every blonding service includes a custom toning process to achieve the ideal shade for your skin tone.

​

Hair Extensions

Our extension specialists offer multiple methods customized to your goals, hair density, and maintenance preferences. Extensions can add fullness, length, volume, or color accents without damage when applied correctly. Consultations are required for color matching, planning, and ordering custom hair.

​

Keratin and Smoothing Treatments

Smoothing treatments reduce frizz, improve manageability, and add long lasting shine. These services are ideal for guests who want softer texture, faster blow drying, and smoother hair in humid conditions.

​

Styling and Blowouts

Our blowouts are customized for volume, smoothness, curls, or waves. This service is perfect for photoshoots, events, or weekly maintenance that keeps your hair looking polished and healthy.

​

Special Occasion Styling

We provide event styling for weddings, formals, photoshoots, and special occasions. This includes updos, half up looks, braids, and soft glam styles. Trials are recommended to plan the perfect style.

​

Hair Repair and Treatment Services

Our salon offers deep conditioning, bond rebuilders, scalp treatments, moisture repair, and strengthening services. These treatments support long term hair health and enhance shine, softness, and resilience.

​

Men’s Hair Services

Precision cuts, clean edging, beard shaping, and easy to maintain styles designed for men who want a sharp look with simple home upkeep.

​

Kids Cuts

Gentle, age appropriate cuts for young guests that include simple styling and a comfortable experience.

​

Why Guests Choose Craft Collective Salon Group

Craft Collective Salon Group is known for advanced education, customized techniques, and a warm guest experience. Our team focuses on healthy hair, long lasting color, and styles that look great in real life. Guests across the North Hills and Pittsburgh area choose our salon for balayage, highlights, blonding, treatments, extensions, haircuts, and complete hair transformations.
