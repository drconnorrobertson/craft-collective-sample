SKIN CARE

Custom Facial….$100

(60 min)

Tailored to your skin's unique needs, this facial includes a double cleanse, exfoliation, relaxing massage, customized mask, and finishing products based on a thorough consultation and skin analysis. You will leave with your skin feeling refreshed and balanced.

 

Anti-Aging Facial….$150

(90 min)

Designed to target fine lines and hyperpigmentation, this extended facial includes a double cleanse, advanced exfoliation, relaxing massage, and our firming Faux-Tox mask to lift and tighten. A rejuvenating experience that leaves your skin feeling youthful and restored.

 

Teen Facial….$75

(45 min)

A customized treatment designed for teenage skin, addressing breakouts, oiliness, dryness, or sensitivity. This facial includes a deep cleanse, gentle exfoliation, and a customized mask to promote a clear, healthy complexion while providing expert skincare guidance.

 

HydroDiamond Facial….$200

(60 min)

A non-invasive treatment that removes the buildup of dirt, oil, and impurities while nourishing the skin with an infusion of antioxidants, hyaluronic acid, and peptides for a deep hydration. This treatment will leave your complexion refined and hydrated with a luminous glow.

 

HydroDiamond Deluxe Facial….$250 

(90 min)

An advanced non-invasive treatment that includes all the benefits of the HydroDiamond facial with added radio frequency to stimulate collagen and elastin, plus our firming Faux-Tox mask for a lifted appearance. This treatment leaves your skin smoother, tighter, and radiant.

 

Lift & Sculpt Facial….$180

(60 min)

A transformative treatment designed to reduce puffiness, smooth fine lines, define facial contours, and ease muscle tension. This facial combines lymphatic drainage with sculpting massage techniques to promote circulation and enhance skin firmness for a refreshed and lifted complexion.

 

Lift & Sculpt Facial + Buccal…. $200

(75 min)

Enhancing all the benefits of the Lift & Sculpt facial, this treatment includes an intraoral buccal massage to further relieve jaw and facial tension boosting results and relaxation.
