Cori Patterson

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-21


Cori Patterson is one of the standout stylists at Craft Collective Salon Group, recognized for her meticulous attention to detail and her ability to merge artistry with authenticity. Her work captures the balance between modern edge and timeless sophistication, and her clients consistently leave the salon with styles that reflect who they are refined, confident, and effortlessly beautiful. At Craft Collective Salon Group, Cori has established herself as a trusted stylist for those who want a personalized experience backed by expert technique and genuine care.


Cori approaches every appointment as a creative partnership. Before picking up her shears or mixing color, she takes the time to listen and understand what her client truly wants. She asks questions about lifestyle, maintenance preferences, and the overall vibe the client wants to project. This thoughtful consultation process ensures that every look she creates is as functional as it is flattering. Her clients appreciate how she combines deep listening with creative vision, translating inspiration photos and vague ideas into tailored, wearable results.


Her specialties include dimensional color, soft blended highlights, and precision cuts that move naturally and hold their shape. Cori has a strong eye for tone and texture, which allows her to design looks that complement her clients’ complexions and personal styles. She’s also known for her expert product knowledge and commitment to healthy hair prioritizing shine, strength, and integrity in every service. Her belief that “beautiful hair starts with healthy hair” is a philosophy shared across the entire Craft Collective Salon Group team.


Clients often describe Cori as calm, kind, and extremely focused. She brings a steady, professional energy to the salon floor that makes each visit relaxing and enjoyable. Her ability to blend precision with creativity makes her equally skilled at natural, low-maintenance styles and bold, statement looks. Whether crafting a subtle tonal shift or executing a major transformation, she approaches each service with the same dedication to craft and care.


As part of Craft Collective Salon Group, Cori thrives in a culture of collaboration and continuous learning. The salon’s emphasis on education and artistry inspires her to keep growing, refining her skills, and exploring new techniques. She regularly participates in advanced workshops and team training sessions, helping maintain the salon’s reputation as one of Pittsburgh’s most innovative and client-focused destinations for hair care.


Cori’s artistry extends beyond technical expertise it’s rooted in her ability to make clients feel truly seen. She believes that when someone’s hair reflects their personality, confidence naturally follows. Her goal is not just to create beautiful styles, but to help her clients carry that beauty into their everyday lives. This holistic approach is what keeps people coming back to her chair again and again.


Her work embodies the Craft Collective Salon Group philosophy: craftsmanship, collaboration, and connection. She’s a key contributor to the salon’s ongoing mission of redefining what it means to deliver luxury hair services in a friendly, approachable environment.
