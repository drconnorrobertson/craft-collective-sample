Scalp Health Treatments in North Hills: Balancing Oil, Reducing Build-Up, and Supporting Hair Growth

Craft Collective Team · 2025-12-12


Beautiful hair starts at the source, the scalp. If your scalp is itchy, dry, oily, tight, flaky, irritated, or producing less-than-ideal oil flow, your hair will struggle to thrive. At Craft Collective Salon Group in North Hills, our scalp health treatments focus on detoxifying, balancing, hydrating, and supporting long-term hair health.


Whether you’re dealing with product buildup, dryness from color services, oil imbalance, or discomfort from wearing extensions, our customized scalp therapies create a healthier foundation for growth and shine.


Why Scalp Health Matters

Most clients focus on conditioning their hair, but the scalp is where every strand begins. When it’s out of balance, you may experience:


– Excess oil or greasy roots

– Dryness or flaking

– Itching or irritation

– Slow or uneven hair growth

– Dull, flat, or limp hair

– Breakage near the root

– Poor volume at the crown

– Faster color fade


Scalp treatments restore the natural environment your hair needs to grow strong, smooth, and resilient.


The Craft Collective Scalp Health Program

Every service is tailored to your specific scalp condition, whether dry, oily, sensitive, or congested.


1. Scalp Detox & Clarifying Treatment

For clients struggling with buildup, flakes, or dullness. This detox gently exfoliates the scalp to remove:


– Hard-water minerals

– Dry shampoo residue

– Heavy styling products

– Environmental pollution

– Sweat buildup

– Silicone and oil layers


Ideal for:


– Oily scalps

– Clients who wear extensions

– Frequent dry-shampoo users

– Hard-water households

– Anyone with dull or flat roots


Pairs perfectly with: Hydration & Moisture Treatments for soft, balanced results.


2. Hydrating Scalp Therapy

For dry, tight, flaky, or itchy scalps. We use lightweight, soothing hydration formulas that restore moisture without clogging follicles.


Benefits include:


– Reduced tightness

– Calmer skin

– Less flaking

– More comfort under extensions

– Better long-term moisture balance


Great for clients who frequently lighten their hair or experience seasonal dryness.


3. Oil-Balance Scalp Correction

For clients with overactive sebaceous glands. This treatment resets the scalp’s natural oil production to prevent greasy roots and mid-length limpness.


Results include:


– Less daily washing

– More root volume

– More consistent style longevity

– Balanced moisture

– Reduced scalp odor or heaviness


4. Scalp Exfoliation Polish

This gentle but effective exfoliation removes dead skin cells and unclogs follicles. It helps improve circulation, oxygenation, and hair growth potential.


5. Soothing Sensitive-Scalp Treatment

Formulated for clients who:


– React to products easily

– Experience redness

– Have dry skin conditions

– Experience irritation from extensions or color


This hypoallergenic service calms, cools, and comforts the scalp without added fragrance or harsh detergents.


Scalp Treatments for Extension Wearers

Extensions create extra weight and coverage, which can sometimes lead to buildup or dryness. Scalp treatments help:


– Remove residue around beads or tapes

– Restore moisture to exposed areas

– Reduce itching during grow-out

– Improve comfort and longevity of the install


Pair with: Extension Care & Maintenance for best results.


Scalp Care for Color-Treated Clients

Lightener, permanent color, and frequent glossing can disrupt the scalp’s natural balance. Coloring clients may experience:


– Temporary dryness

– Sensitivity

– Flakiness or tightness

– Faster oil production


A scalp treatment before your next color appointment ensures:


– Better color adhesion

– More even lift

– Longer-lasting tone

– Healthier overall results


Pair beautifully with: Gloss & Shine Enhancements for full-spectrum hair and scalp renewal.


At-Home Scalp Health Routine

Your stylist will recommend products based on your needs from the Product Education & Hair Care series.


General at-home tips:


– Use clarifying shampoo once every 2 to 3 weeks

– Apply hydrating scalp oils sparingly and only when needed

– Avoid heavy silicones on the scalp

– Don’t scratch or pick at flakes

– Brush the scalp gently to stimulate circulation

– Rinse thoroughly after shampoo and conditioner

– Blow-dry the scalp area fully to prevent dampness


A healthy scalp = longer-lasting styles, better volume, and stronger hair.


Sustainable Scalp Care

All products used during scalp services at Craft Collective align with the Sustainable & Eco-Friendly Salon initiative.


We prioritize:


– Vegan formulas

– Biodegradable surfactants

– Low-fragrance or fragrance-free options for sensitive clients

– Recyclable packaging

– Environmentally safe exfoliation particles


Your scalp benefits, and so does the planet.
