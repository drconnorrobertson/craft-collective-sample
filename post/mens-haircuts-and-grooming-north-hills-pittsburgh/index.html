Men’s Haircuts & Grooming in North Hills Pittsburgh | Craft Collective Salon Group

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-26

Men’s grooming has evolved — and so have we. At Craft Collective Salon Group, located in North Hills Pittsburgh, we believe a great haircut does more than clean up your look — it defines your style, boosts confidence, and sets the tone for how you present yourself to the world. Our men’s haircut and grooming services are built on precision, consistency, and modern technique, tailored for the lifestyle of today’s Pittsburgh professional.


Gone are the days when men’s cuts were one-style-fits-all. Every man who sits in our chair gets a customized service designed around face shape, texture, and lifestyle. Whether it’s a skin fade, textured crop, classic side part, or long, layered flow, our stylists deliver clean lines, balanced proportions, and easy upkeep between visits.


Our approach is rooted in technical mastery. Each stylist at Craft Collective Salon Group is trained in both salon and barbering techniques, allowing them to create structured yet wearable looks. We combine the refined artistry of salon precision with the crisp finish of a traditional barbershop. The result: sharp, modern styles that look just as good on day 30 as they do on day one.


Precision Meets Personalization

Every appointment begins with a personalized consultation. We talk through what’s working, what’s not, and how you want your hair to feel — not just look. This helps us tailor the service to your lifestyle, whether you’re someone who styles every morning or prefers a low-maintenance routine. Our North Hills clients love that each visit feels consistent, reliable, and easy to maintain.


We analyze growth patterns, density, and cowlicks to determine the ideal cutting approach. A haircut should work with your hair’s natural direction, not against it. This ensures that styling feels effortless at home, even with minimal product.


We use R+Co products for styling — their flexible holds, matte pastes, and moisture-balancing cleansers are favorites among our male clients. For a refined finish, we elevate select looks with R+Co Bleu — a premium, sustainable grooming line that enhances shine and texture without weight.


Men’s Haircut Styles That Dominate Pittsburgh Trends

Our stylists are always watching and refining the latest trends in men’s hair. Right now, these are some of the most popular styles requested at our North Hills salon:

  • The Classic Fade: Clean, tapered sides with customizable height. Perfect for professionals who want a polished look that still feels modern.

  • The Textured Crop: A contemporary favorite that adds movement and edge without sacrificing sophistication.

  • The Executive Cut: A timeless, versatile shape that transitions seamlessly from the office to the weekend.

  • The Long Layered Flow: Perfect for men growing out their hair — structured to move naturally with shape and purpose.

  • The Undercut Revival: Bold contrast between short sides and longer top, styled with R+Co Pomade for a sleek, controlled finish.


Each of these styles can be customized to match your features, job requirements, and personal preferences. Our stylists are experts in adapting trends to look timeless — because the goal is not just a haircut, but a look that feels like you.


Fade Perfection and Beard Detailing

At Craft Collective, fades are treated like art. Each one is blended seamlessly with meticulous attention to line and contour. We work with precision clipper techniques and scissor-over-comb detailing to achieve transitions so smooth they look airbrushed. Clients often comment that their fade grows out more naturally than anywhere else — that’s because we cut with the regrowth cycle in mind, not just the day of.


We also offer beard shaping and line detailing for men who want a cohesive, polished appearance. Whether you’re maintaining a full beard or a short stubble edge, our stylists sculpt and define with the same precision as your haircut. We use conditioning oils from R+Co Bleu that soften facial hair and nourish the skin underneath.


The Modern Grooming Experience

Men’s grooming today is about more than just the cut. It’s about the experience. Our salon environment is calm, elevated, and designed for comfort. We provide scalp massages, warm towel finishes, and styling lessons that make you look forward to every visit. Our clients in North Hills appreciate that they can relax in a premium environment while still getting efficient, professional service.


Every grooming session includes a wash with R+Co or Wella Professionals shampoos tailored to your scalp and hair type. We follow with hydrating conditioners and finish with lightweight, residue-free styling products. These systems are designed to enhance scalp health and prevent buildup, extending the life of your haircut and keeping your hair looking sharp between visits.


Scalp Health & Hair Longevity

Healthy hair starts at the root. Many men experience dryness, itchiness, or thinning due to product buildup and Pittsburgh’s fluctuating climate. That’s why we incorporate scalp treatments into our grooming program. These detoxifying sessions cleanse impurities, remove residue, and stimulate circulation for stronger growth.


For clients noticing early thinning, we provide custom treatment recommendations using bond-repairing technology from Wella Professionals and nutrient-rich formulas from R+Co Bleu. These advanced systems help strengthen follicles, promote density, and restore balance to the scalp.


Product Knowledge for Men Who Care About Results

One of the most common questions we hear is, “What should I use at home?” We keep it simple and effective.

  • Styling Creams: Lightweight and pliable, perfect for natural hold without stiffness.

  • Matte Pastes: For texture and separation with a lived-in, masculine finish.

  • Pomades: For sleek control and high shine — ideal for formal or executive styles.

  • Leave-In Conditioners: Keep hair hydrated without greasiness.


We carry a curated selection of R+Co and R+Co Bleu products designed to work with Pittsburgh’s humidity and cold-weather static. Every product we use in the salon is available for at-home purchase, so you can maintain professional results long after your visit.

Booking & Maintenance

We recommend scheduling men’s haircut appointments every 3–5 weeks, depending on your style and growth rate. Regular trims maintain structure and keep your hairline clean. For fades, every two to three weeks is ideal for maintaining a sharp, consistent appearance.

Our North Hills salon offers online booking for convenience, and repeat clients enjoy priority scheduling with their preferred stylist.
