Best Blowouts and Finishing Styles in Sewickley | Craft Collective Salon Group

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-22

In Sewickley, style is timeless, and at Craft Collective Salon Group, our blowouts deliver that perfect mix of polish and ease. Every detail—from the brushwork to the product selection—is intentional, creating hair that looks just as beautiful on day three as it does when you leave the chair.


The Craft Collective Difference

A professional blowout should do more than add shine—it should bring out the natural structure of your haircut, protect your hair’s health, and help your look last through the week.

Our stylists tailor every service to your texture, density, and personal style.


Each appointment begins with a consultation and a lightweight prep using R+Co Bleu Essential Hair Tonic to condition and protect against heat. The result is smooth, frizz-free hair that feels healthy and looks radiant.


Techniques Designed for Longevity

Our stylists are trained in advanced blowout and finishing techniques that blend structure with softness.

  • Volume building: Lift and body at the root using directional drying.

  • Polishing: Controlled tension through the brush to seal the cuticle for ultimate smoothness.

  • Finishing: Strategic cooling and lightweight misting to hold style without stiffness.


Every blowout is finished with Wella EIMI Perfect Me or Aveda Air Control Spray for flexible, touchable hold.


For more details on long-lasting style, visit Best Blowouts and Event Styles in Allison Park.


Perfect for Any Occasion

Our Sewickley clients book blowouts for a variety of reasons:

  • Work presentations and corporate headshots

  • Nights out or weekend events

  • Weddings, engagements, or special celebrations

  • Every day, confidence boosts


Each style is custom-finished to your preferences—sleek, bouncy, or texturized with effortless waves.


Sustainable Styling Without Compromise

True luxury today means sustainability. Every Sewickley blowout uses products and tools that align with our eco-conscious values:

  • Energy-efficient dryers that cut drying time and lower power usage.

  • Biodegradable towels that reduce waste.

  • Refillable styling products that minimize packaging.


These small steps make a real difference while maintaining our signature salon performance.

Learn how our environmental choices connect across all services in Sewickley’s Luxury Salon Experience: Sustainable and Sophisticated.


How to Extend Your Blowout at Home

To keep your hair fresh between visits:

  • Use R+Co Spiritualized Dry Shampoo Mist midweek.

  • Sleep on a silk or satin pillowcase.

  • Wrap hair loosely with a scrunchie before bed.

  • Avoid excess moisture or humidity when possible.


Paired with professional products, these habits can stretch your blowout up to five days.
