The Role of Sustainable Salons in Pittsburgh’s Beauty Industry | Craft Collective Salon Group

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-26

Pittsburgh’s beauty industry is evolving, and at the center of that evolution is sustainability. As clients demand transparency and cleaner practices, salons like Craft Collective Salon Group are redefining what luxury looks like—proof that high-end beauty can also be ethical and environmentally conscious.


Why Sustainability Matters in the Salon World

Traditional salon systems often generate excess waste through plastic packaging, high energy use, and chemical runoff. Craft Collective’s mission is to rewrite that model, creating eco-luxury environments that deliver stunning results while reducing environmental impact.


Our salons in Wexford, Allison Park, and Ross Township are setting a new standard for Pittsburgh: beautiful hair, clean ingredients, and responsible business.


Our Sustainable Commitments

  1. Refillable Product Systems – Clients refill R+Co Bleu and Aveda bottles directly in-salon, reducing single-use plastics.

  2. Low-Energy Operations – LED lighting, efficient dryers, and optimized scheduling lower our energy footprint.

  3. Water Stewardship – Smart sinks and water-saving rinse systems conserve thousands of gallons annually.

  4. Zero Waste Philosophy – Biodegradable towels and foils minimize landfill impact.


Learn how these practices manifest locally in Sustainable Beauty in Wexford: Clean, Conscious, and Confident.


Empowering Clients Through Education

Education is a core pillar of sustainability. We teach our Pittsburgh-area clients how to care for their hair with clean, effective routines that extend salon results. Whether it’s switching to sulfate-free shampoos or using heat protection correctly, our stylists make every recommendation purposeful.


Read our at-home care guide: How Wexford Clients Are Building a Clean Hair Routine.


Collaborating for a Cleaner Future

Craft Collective partners with clean beauty innovators to stay ahead of environmental standards. Our relationship with Aveda, R+Co Bleu, and Wella Professionals ensures our salons remain leaders in both artistry and accountability.


Together, we’re part of a growing movement redefining Pittsburgh’s beauty culture—one rooted in awareness, integrity, and innovation.


The Impact on Local Communities

Sustainable salons don’t just transform hair—they transform neighborhoods. Our North Hills locations employ local talent, source community partnerships, and reinvest in education to help Pittsburgh thrive through purpose-driven business.


Join the Movement

When you choose Craft Collective Salon Group, you’re supporting a vision of beauty that values both people and planet. We’re proud to represent Pittsburgh’s new standard of clean, sustainable, and sophisticated salon care.
