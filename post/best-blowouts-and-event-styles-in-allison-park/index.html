Best Blowouts and Event Styles in Allison Park | Craft Collective Salon Group

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-24

At Craft Collective Salon Group, we believe a great blowout can change your entire day. For Allison Park professionals, families, and brides-to-be, our signature blowouts combine long-lasting hold, lightweight movement, and clean, sustainable products that keep hair soft and healthy.


The Art of the Perfect Blowout

Every blowout begins with the right foundation. Our stylists use precision sectioning, ionic dryers, and round-brush sculpting to create smoothness without sacrificing volume. Whether you want sleek polish or effortless waves, we customize every step to your texture and occasion.


Our process blends technique with product synergy — each formula chosen to maximize shine, humidity resistance, and touchable finish.


The Products That Power the Style

We only use products that enhance health and performance:

  • R+Co Bleu Essential Hair Tonic for heat protection and hydration

  • Wella EIMI Perfect Me for lightweight smoothing and shine

  • Aveda Air Control Spray for flexible, lasting hold


These eco-luxury formulas create movement that lasts — even in Pittsburgh humidity.

For a deeper look at sustainability in styling, see How Allison Park Clients Are Redefining Sustainable Beauty.


Event Styling That Lasts

From weddings to galas, our Allison Park stylists specialize in updos, soft curls, and modern textured styles that photograph beautifully. We understand that event hair should look perfect in every light — and stay comfortable through every celebration.


We prep each style with R+Co Bleu’s weightless heat protectants and finish with Aveda’s humidity-resistant hold, giving you confidence from morning photos to evening dancing.

See how we handle special occasion services in Best Blowouts and Finishing Styles in Sewickley.


Sustainability Meets Performance

Every blowout at Craft Collective Salon Group reflects our eco-conscious commitment. We use biodegradable towels, low-energy dryers, and refillable products to keep our environmental footprint as light as our styling touch.


Learn more about our eco-luxury standards on the Sustainable Beauty Philosophy page.


At-Home Care for Lasting Results

After your salon visit, keep your style fresh with a few simple habits:

  • Sleep on a silk pillowcase to reduce friction.

  • Use R+Co Spiritualized Dry Shampoo Mist for a midweek refresh.

  • Avoid overwashing — extending your blowout helps preserve tone and texture.


Find shine-care tips in The Allison Park Gloss Revival Routine for Healthy Shine.

Our stylists understand how Pittsburgh’s humidity, seasons, and water quality affect your style, and tailor every blowout to your needs.
