The Future of Hair Color: Clean, Sustainable, and Customized | Craft Collective Salon Group

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-26

Hair color is entering a new era—one defined by clean science, personalized artistry, and environmental responsibility. At Craft Collective Salon Group, we’re leading that transformation across our Wexford, Allison Park, and Ross Township locations with the next generation of professional color systems.


Why Clean Color Matters

Traditional color formulas relied on harsh chemicals that compromised the integrity of the hair. Today’s clients want color that performs powerfully yet gently, preserving shine and structure while delivering stunning tone.


We use Wella Professionals Shinefinity, Aveda Full Spectrum Vegan Color, and R+Co Bleu Toning Systems to create vibrant, sustainable color with zero compromise.

Each line we carry is vegan, cruelty-free, and carbon-neutral—reflecting our ongoing commitment to cleaner beauty.


Learn how this aligns with our broader values in Sustainable Beauty in Wexford: Clean, Conscious, and Confident.


Technology Meets Artistry

Our stylists blend years of experience with cutting-edge color technology to craft fully customized shades. With digital shade mapping and precision tone formulation, every client receives color that complements their skin tone, lifestyle, and texture.

Our smart glossing system refines tone in real time, ensuring maximum dimension and minimal fading.


Longevity Through Care

Sustainability extends beyond the color bowl. We pair each service with bond-repair glosses, heat protection, and moisture-balancing treatments that keep hair vibrant and healthy long after your visit.


At-home care is just as crucial—maintain your results with:

  • R+Co Bleu Primary Color Conditioner

  • Aveda Botanical Repair Mask

  • Wella Invigo Brilliance Shampoo


See our guide for home maintenance in How Allison Park Clients Maintain Shine Between Appointments.


Sustainable Systems, Local Impact

Each Craft Collective salon participates in sustainable color recycling initiatives, ensuring all unused materials and foils are properly repurposed. This closed-loop approach allows us to serve clients beautifully while minimizing environmental impact.


The Future Is Personal

The next phase of hair color is personalization—every tone, gloss, and placement tailored to the individual. At Craft Collective, we combine artistic expertise with modern science to make that future real today.


Book Your Sustainable Color Service

Experience the future of professional color—customized, clean, and consciously crafted. Book your appointment at Craft Collective Salon Group in Wexford, Allison Park, or Ross Township.
