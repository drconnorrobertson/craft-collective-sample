Amanda Melvin

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-21


Amanda Melvin is known at Craft Collective Salon Group for her grounded, client-centered approach and her eye for detail that turns every style into something both polished and practical. She represents the kind of professionalism and warmth that defines the salon’s reputation across Pittsburgh. Every client who sits in Amanda’s chair leaves not just with exceptional hair, but with a deeper sense of confidence and ease an experience that reflects the very heart of Craft Collective’s mission.


Amanda specializes in precision cutting, soft color transitions, and styles that balance everyday wearability with artistic flair. Her work demonstrates a deep understanding of shape, tone, and texture, ensuring each look feels natural yet refined. Whether a client wants a subtle enhancement or a complete transformation, Amanda takes the time to listen, assess, and execute with purpose. She’s especially skilled at helping people identify what will suit their lifestyle, face shape, and hair type, so the end result is something that feels authentic and timeless.


Her calm, methodical approach has made her one of the most trusted stylists in the salon. She is known for explaining each step in the process from consultation through styling so clients always feel comfortable and included in their beauty journey. Many of Amanda’s guests return year after year, drawn to her consistency and care. In an industry that thrives on trends, her focus on sustainable, healthy hair makes her work stand out.


At Craft Collective Salon Group, Amanda thrives in an environment that celebrates collaboration and growth. The salon’s culture of shared learning keeps her inspired and sharp, with ongoing education sessions that help her explore the latest in coloring techniques, haircutting methods, and product innovation. She’s particularly passionate about using professional-grade products that protect and nourish the hair, a practice aligned with Craft Collective’s commitment to long-term hair health.


Amanda’s clients appreciate how she bridges technical excellence with a warm, approachable personality. She makes everyone feel seen and valued, and her precision-based work is always balanced with an effortless finish that never feels overdone. From expertly blended highlights to sculpted short cuts and rich, dimensional tones, her portfolio showcases the best of modern salon artistry.


As part of one of Pittsburgh’s most dynamic beauty teams, Amanda helps maintain Craft Collective Salon Group’s standing as a leader in the region’s hair community. Her consistency, craftsmanship, and genuine connection to her clients make her a key part of the team’s success story. Every appointment with Amanda reflects what the salon is all about modern design, meaningful connection, and a deep respect for the craft.
