Modern Men’s Cuts Popular in North Hills | Craft Collective Salon Group

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-25

For men throughout North Hills Pittsburgh, hair has become a statement of personal style and confidence. At Craft Collective Salon Group in Ross Township, our stylists offer a refined approach to men’s grooming — combining classic techniques with modern texture and precision.


Our male clients come from every background — entrepreneurs, students, and professionals — and they all want the same thing: a cut that looks sharp, feels effortless, and grows out cleanly. That’s why our men’s cuts balance design, structure, and wearability to fit each client’s routine and style.


The Rise of Modern Men’s Hair in North Hills

In the last few years, Ross Township and Wexford have seen a major shift in men’s hair trends. More men are embracing texture, longer lengths, and tailored fade work. The goal is a cut that can transition from a professional environment to a casual weekend with minimal styling time.


Our stylists specialize in:

  • Textured fades that grow out soft and blend naturally.

  • Long layered cuts for men with curl or wave.

  • Scissor-over-comb techniques for precision without harsh lines.

  • Classic taper cuts revived with a modern finish.


We use lightweight styling creams from R+Co and bond-protective products from Wella Professionals to keep hair healthy through every cut and color refresh.


Explore our approach to clean, sustainable style in the Hair Health & Maintenance guide.


Clean Fades and Tapers That Fit Your Routine

The fade remains a staple for Pittsburgh men because it’s versatile and low-maintenance. At Craft Collective, our fades are customized to each head shape and hair texture — never cookie-cutter.


We incorporate R+Co texture pastes to add definition and separation without stickiness. Our stylists also recommend Wella’s EIMI Matte Putty for clients who prefer a more structured look that still moves naturally.


For fade inspiration and transitional color ideas, read Best Balayage Near Ross Park Mall. Even if you’re not adding color, understanding tone and contrast helps build dimension into your cut.


Longer Men’s Hair and Textured Movement

Longer styles are making a strong comeback throughout North Hills. Whether shoulder-length or a refined medium cut, the key is maintaining healthy ends and structured layers.


Our team uses moisture-rich care from R+Co Bleu — a line that’s vegan, cruelty-free, and designed for sustainability. These formulas preserve shine and prevent breakage during winter months when Pittsburgh’s air is especially dry.


Check out How to Maintain Your Color Through Pittsburgh Winters for conditioning techniques that also benefit natural hair.


Sustainable Grooming for Modern Men

Men’s grooming has evolved beyond barbering into self-care and sustainability. At Craft Collective Salon Group, we align our men’s services with our eco-conscious philosophy.

From water-efficient stations to biodegradable towels and recyclable product packaging, our Ross Township space represents modern luxury with integrity.


Every appointment includes styling education — our stylists show you how to recreate your look using a few products and minutes of daily effort.


Meet the team behind the chair on the Meet the Stylists page to see who specializes in men’s cuts and texture design.


Beard Trims and Detail Work

For clients who want a polished finish, our stylists offer precision beard trims and detailing services. We use gentle, hydrating formulas from R+Co and Aveda to soothe the skin and refine edges.


Aveda’s men’s aromas and plant-based aftershave creams balance oil production and reduce irritation — ideal for Pittsburgh’s cold, dry winters.


For seasonal care advice, see How to Maintain Your Color Through Pittsburgh Winters, which also covers humidity and hydration strategies.


Consistency and Education

Men’s hair grows fast — and so does your schedule. We recommend booking every 4–6 weeks to keep your shape clean and fade lines crisp. If you’re growing length, our stylists adjust weight distribution at each visit to avoid bulk or flat spots.


Education is central to Craft Collective’s culture. Our team trains regularly with global brands like Wella and R+Co to stay ahead of international trends. That commitment ensures North Hills clients get precision cuts and modern styling insight every visit.


Learn how we apply education and sustainability in every service through the Ross Township Salon Page.


Why North Hills Men Choose Craft Collective

From McCandless to Wexford, men choose Craft Collective because we value craftsmanship as much as comfort. Every appointment combines artistry and efficiency so you leave looking refreshed and ready for anything.


Whether you prefer a clean fade or a textured medium cut, our stylists deliver refined, low-maintenance results built for Pittsburgh’s climate and lifestyle.


Book your next men’s cut at Craft Collective Salon Group and experience why our North Hills team has become the go-to for modern men’s hair design.
