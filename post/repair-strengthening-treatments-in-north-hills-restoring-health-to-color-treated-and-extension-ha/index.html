Repair & Strengthening Treatments in North Hills: Restoring Health to Color-Treated and Extension Hair

Craft Collective Team · 2025-12-12


Healthy hair is the foundation of every beautiful style, and at Craft Collective Salon Group in North Hills, our repair and strengthening treatments are designed to rebuild, fortify, and protect your strands from the inside out.


Whether you lighten your hair, wear extensions, heat-style regularly, or simply want a stronger, smoother foundation, our professional treatments deliver noticeable results after just one session, with transformational benefits over time.


Why Repair Treatments Matter

Color, heat, weather, and mechanical stress all weaken the internal structure of the hair. When the bonds inside the strand break or weaken, the hair becomes more prone to:


– Breakage

– Frizz

– Split ends

– Rough texture

– Dullness

– Tangling


For clients with extensions, stress points can appear around rows or tape areas if the natural hair isn’t properly reinforced.


Repair and strengthening treatments restore the hair’s integrity, making it smoother, softer, shinier, and more resistant to breakage.


The Craft Collective Repair System

At Craft Collective, we use a combination of professional bond-repair technology, hydration therapies, and strengthening boosters customized to your hair type and goals.


1. Bond-Building Treatments

These treatments rebuild the hair from the inside, repairing broken disulfide bonds caused by bleach, color, heat, or wear and tear. Ideal for:


– Blondes

– Highlighted hair

– Balayage clients

– Clients prepping for extensions

– Clients wearing hand-tied or tape-in extensions


Bond builders also help prepare your hair for future color services, keeping it strong and resilient.


2. Deep Hydration Masks

Hydration is essential for elasticity, shine, and movement. We use masks designed to:


– Restore moisture

– Reduce frizz

– Improve softness

– Increase manageability

– Protect against environmental exposure


Perfect for curly, thick, or coarse textures.


3. Strengthening Protein Infusions

Fine or over-processed hair benefits from targeted protein that reinforces the cuticle and reduces breakage. Your stylist will balance moisture and protein to avoid stiffness or dryness.


4. Extension-Safe Treatments

All treatments in this pillar are safe for:


– Hand-tied extensions

– Tape-in extensions

– Clip-in extensions

– High-end wefts


We keep products away from the ends and focus on mid-length through ends for maximum nourishment.


When You Should Get a Repair Treatment

We recommend professional strengthening treatments if you:


– Lighten or color your hair regularly

– Want balayage or blonde services

– Wear extensions

– Use hot tools more than 3x/week

– Experience shedding, breakage, or rough texture

– Notice dullness or lack of movement

– Can’t grow your hair past a certain length

– Want a gloss or color refresh to look smoother


Pairing a treatment with a Gloss & Shine Enhancement delivers extraordinary smoothness and reflective shine.


Benefits You’ll See Immediately

After one treatment, clients typically notice:


– Softer, smoother hair

– Reduced frizz

– More shine and light reflection

– Easier styling

– More hydration

– Less breakage and shedding

– Better elasticity


After consistent monthly treatments, results compound, making your hair healthier, stronger, and easier to maintain.


Pairing Repair Treatments With Color or Extensions

Before Extensions

Repair treatments prep the hair for Hand-Tied or Tape-In Extensions by strengthening the foundation, reducing breakage, and improving longevity.


During Extension Wear

Hydration and repair maintain softness and prevent matting or dryness at the ends.


After Removing Extensions

A full-strengthening session restores balance, rebuilds bonds, and prepares your hair for your next install.


Before or After Color

Color-treated hair responds dramatically to repair sessions. Treatments reduce porosity, soften texture, and help color take on more evenness.


Pair beautifully with:


– Balayage With Extensions 

– Dimensional Brunette Techniques 

– Blonde Extension Care 


Your At-Home Strength Maintenance

After your in-salon treatment, your stylist will recommend products from our Product Education & Hair Care series, such as:


– Bond-building shampoos/conditioners

– Heat protectant sprays

– Hydrating masks

– Lightweight replenishing oils


These extend the benefits of your salon service and help you maintain long-term health.

Healthy hair, healthy planet.
