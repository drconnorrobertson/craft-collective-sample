Ha Na Ko

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-21



Ha Na Ko is one of Craft Collective Salon Group’s most detail-oriented and creative stylists, known for her calm presence, refined aesthetic, and meticulous approach to design. She brings a sense of grace and intentionality to every service, embodying the balance between technical mastery and artistic intuition that defines the salon’s reputation across Pittsburgh. Clients who sit in Ha Na’s chair consistently remark on her ability to listen deeply, visualize clearly, and deliver results that are both stunning and easy to maintain.


At Craft Collective Salon Group, Ha Na’s work reflects the salon’s mission to combine craft and care in every appointment. She approaches hair as both an art and a science considering structure, tone, texture, and daily wear before crafting a style that feels authentic and effortless. Her specialties include natural, low-maintenance coloring, soft layering, and subtle shaping that enhances facial features and natural movement. Every detail is deliberate, from her sectioning technique to her product choices, ensuring clients leave with hair that looks polished and feels healthy.


Her approach to color work is especially noteworthy. Ha Na has a deep understanding of tone harmony and how light interacts with texture, allowing her to create seamless, luminous finishes that elevate her clients’ natural beauty. Whether it’s a soft balayage, dimensional brunette, or bright blonde refresh, she focuses on keeping color believable and easy to grow out gracefully. Her goal is to help clients achieve modern results that align with their personal aesthetic and lifestyle.


Clients often describe Ha Na as patient, precise, and thoughtful. She takes time to educate them on maintenance routines, home care, and styling tips, ensuring their results last long after the appointment. That transparency builds trust and fosters long-term relationships a hallmark of Craft Collective Salon Group’s approach to service. Her clients know they can expect consistency, honesty, and artistry in equal measure.


Ha Na is also deeply engaged in Craft Collective’s culture of education and collaboration. She regularly participates in advanced training sessions to refine her skills and explore new techniques in color theory, cutting geometry, and product innovation. Her curiosity and professionalism inspire those around her, contributing to the salon’s collective energy and standard of excellence.


Within the team, Ha Na is known as a quiet leader steady, reliable, and always striving for excellence. She supports her fellow stylists, shares knowledge generously, and helps foster the creative environment that keeps Craft Collective at the forefront of Pittsburgh’s beauty industry. Her dedication to her craft and clients perfectly reflects the salon’s vision of elevating artistry through authenticity.


Ha Na’s personal style philosophy is rooted in balance and natural beauty. She believes the best hair looks enhance who someone already is, not mask it. Her subtle transformations and polished finishes speak to that belief, earning her a growing clientele that values her refined, understated approach.
