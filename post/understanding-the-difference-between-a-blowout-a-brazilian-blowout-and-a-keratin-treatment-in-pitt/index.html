Understanding the Difference Between a Blowout, a Brazilian Blowout, and a Keratin Treatment in Pittsburgh

Craft Collective Team · 2026-01-08


Many clients in Pittsburgh want smoother, shinier, easier-to-manage hair, but the number of smoothing treatments available can be confusing. Blowouts, Brazilian blowouts, and keratin treatments all soften frizz and improve styling, yet each works differently, lasts differently, and fits different hair types. Choosing the right service is key to achieving long-lasting results without compromising your hair’s health.


This guide breaks down each option so you understand what they are, who they are for, and how Pittsburgh’s climate influences their effectiveness.


What a Standard Blowout Is

A blowout is a styling service where a stylist uses a round brush and blow dryer to smooth your hair, add volume, or create shape. It does not chemically alter the structure of your hair. Results last until your next wash.


A blowout is best for:

  • Special events

  • Temporary smoothness

  • Adding volume

  • Quick refreshes

  • Trying a new style without commitment


In Pittsburgh’s humid months, blowout longevity depends heavily on the weather. For clients battling frizz or puffiness, this is a temporary solution.


What a Brazilian Blowout Is

A Brazilian blowout is a semi-permanent smoothing treatment that creates a protective layer around each strand. It reduces frizz, improves shine, and softens texture. It does not fully straighten hair, but makes it significantly easier to style.


Brazilian blowouts are ideal for:

  • Clients with frizz

  • Clients with medium to thick hair

  • Clients who want easier daily styling

  • Clients who want smoothness but also movement


Results typically last two to four months, depending on maintenance and hair type.


How a Keratin Treatment Works

A keratin treatment restructures and smooths the hair by infusing keratin proteins and sealing them with heat. This treatment reduces frizz more aggressively than a Brazilian blowout and can slightly relax curl or wave, depending on the formulation.


Keratin is best for:

  • Clients who want long-lasting smoothness

  • Clients with high frizz levels

  • Clients with thick, coarse, or porous hair

  • Clients who want reduced volume


Results can last three to six months with proper care.


The key difference between these treatments and a blowout is longevity and chemical interaction. Blowouts are styling. Brazilian blowouts and keratin treatments are smoothing systems.


How Pittsburgh’s Climate Impacts These Treatments

Humidity increases frizz and makes hair expand. Cold winter air dries hair out and increases breakage. Pittsburgh’s rapid fluctuations can make hair unpredictable.


Smoothing treatments reduce the effect of humidity and help your style last longer. Many clients choose keratin or Brazilian blowouts in spring and summer, then switch to hydration-based routines in fall and winter.


Signs You Should Choose a Keratin Treatment

You may be a good candidate if:

  • Your hair expands in humidity

  • You fight frizz daily

  • Your blowouts never last

  • Your hair feels porous or rough

  • Your hair takes a long time to dry

  • You want smoother, shinier hair long term


Keratin treatments deliver the most dramatic smoothing effect.


Signs You Should Choose a Brazilian Blowout

You may prefer this treatment if:

  • You want frizz control without losing movement

  • Your hair is medium density

  • You want manageable, soft styling

  • You prefer a lighter smoothing effect

  • You want something more customizable


Brazilian blowouts are more flexible for clients who want natural movement.


Signs You Should Choose a Standard Blowout

A regular blowout is perfect for someone who:

  • Only wants temporary smoothing

  • Is getting ready for an event

  • Wants to try a style before committing to a smoothing system

  • Prefers minimal heat or chemical services

  • Does not experience high frizz levels


This option is short-term but easy to maintain.


How to Maintain Smoothing Treatments in Pittsburgh

To protect your results:

  • Use sulfate-free shampoo

  • Avoid washing daily

  • Use hydrating masks weekly

  • Use heat protectant

  • Avoid heavy oils that block moisture

  • Protect your hair from chlorine and hard water


Maintenance determines how long the treatment lasts.
