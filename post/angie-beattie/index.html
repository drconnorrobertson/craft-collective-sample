Angie Beattie

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-21


Angie Beattie is one of the creative cornerstones of Craft Collective Salon Group, known for her confident artistry, warm personality, and dedication to helping clients discover a version of themselves they truly love. With years of experience in the beauty industry, Angie has cultivated a reputation for crafting effortlessly beautiful styles that balance technique and individuality. Her chair has become a trusted space where clients feel listened to, cared for, and inspired to try something new.


At Craft Collective Salon Group, Angie’s work reflects the salon’s core philosophy merging high-level craft with personalized attention. She believes that great hair should feel as natural as it looks, and her ability to customize each look to suit face shape, lifestyle, and personal taste has earned her a loyal following across Pittsburgh. From precision cuts and hand-painted balayage to vibrant color transformations, Angie’s skill lies in her ability to make every detail feel intentional and timeless.


Her approach begins with conversation. Angie takes time to understand what each guest envisions and how they want to feel when they leave the salon. That clarity allows her to deliver results that not only look incredible but are also manageable long after the appointment. She’s known for explaining her process in a way that makes clients feel empowered and educated sharing insights about product use, styling techniques, and healthy maintenance practices that extend the life of their look.


Angie’s artistry is shaped by her passion for continued education and her love of collaboration. She regularly participates in Craft Collective’s in-house workshops and advanced training sessions, where stylists exchange ideas and refine their techniques. Her curiosity and drive keep her on top of modern color placement, precision cutting, and texturizing trends, ensuring that her clients always benefit from the most current industry practices.


Beyond her technical mastery, Angie brings something less tangible but equally powerful an energy that makes every appointment feel welcoming. She’s the kind of stylist who instantly puts people at ease, creating a balance between professional focus and friendly conversation. It’s that combination that transforms a simple haircut or color into a confidence-building experience. Her clients often describe her as intuitive, thoughtful, and genuinely invested in helping them feel their best.


Within the Craft Collective Salon Group team, Angie is respected not only for her work but for her mentorship and collaboration. She embodies the salon’s mission of creative excellence and community connection, and she contributes to the culture of positivity that defines the salon. Her dedication to the craft and her commitment to her clients help uphold Craft Collective’s standing as one of the best hair salons in Pittsburgh.


Angie’s signature style can best be described as natural luxury looks that appear effortlessly beautiful yet are executed with meticulous attention to detail. Whether she’s refining soft layers, crafting a dimensional brunette, or brightening a blonde, her work consistently reflects the salon’s commitment to elevated, modern beauty.
