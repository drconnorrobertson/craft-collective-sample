Curly Haircuts in North Hills: Embracing Natural Texture with Expert Shape and Balance

Craft Collective Team · 2025-12-06


Curls have a life and rhythm of their own. At Craft Collective Salon Group in North Hills, we celebrate individuality by offering specialized curly haircuts designed to shape, define, and enhance your natural texture, never fighting against it.


Our stylists are trained in curl pattern analysis and advanced cutting techniques to ensure every curl falls exactly where it should. Whether you have loose waves, soft spirals, or tight coils, we tailor each cut for volume, movement, and lasting health.


Why Curly Hair Needs Specialized Cutting

Curly hair isn’t uniform; it grows in patterns, behaves differently when wet versus dry, and requires precision to maintain shape. A one-size-fits-all haircut simply doesn’t work.


Our curly cutting philosophy focuses on:


– Preserving curl integrity and definition

– Balancing volume without creating bulk

– Enhancing face shape and proportions

– Supporting long-term hydration and elasticity

– Teaching proper care and styling for your texture


The result: curls that feel free, full, and effortlessly defined.


The Craft Collective Curly Cut Experience

Every curly haircut begins with an in-depth consultation to understand your texture, curl type, and goals. We typically perform our curl shaping cuts on dry hair so we can see how your curls naturally spring and settle.


Step 1: Curl Pattern & Density Analysis

We identify your curl family, from loose wave to corkscrew, and map how your texture behaves throughout the head.


Step 2: Dry Cut & Shape Design

Using curl-by-curl precision, we sculpt your shape to enhance volume, reduce weight, and create balance from crown to perimeter.


Step 3: Cleansing & Hydration Treatment

After cutting, we treat curls with moisture-rich formulas from our Hair Treatments & Health menu to soften, seal, and restore bounce.


Step 4: Education & Home Routine

We finish every appointment with personalized curl-care education, product recommendations, and drying methods to replicate salon results at home, including air-dry and diffuser techniques.


Popular Curly Haircut Styles

1. Rounded Layered Cut

Designed to enhance the natural circular shape of curls and distribute volume evenly throughout.


Ideal for: Tight curls and coils.


Pair with: Hydration Treatments and Bond-Building Services.


2. Deva-Inspired Cut

Cut dry in the natural curl pattern to emphasize each curl’s shape.


Ideal for: Clients who wear their curls naturally every day.


3. Curly Shag

A modern revival that adds softness, fringe, and texture around the face.


Pairs beautifully with: Dimensional Brunette Techniques or Balayage Highlights.


4. Long Curly Layers

For those who love length but want bounce, long layers reduce heaviness and bring life back into the shape.


Maintenance: Trims every 10 to 12 weeks to maintain elasticity and prevent split ends.


5. Curly Bob

Chin-length curls with stacked layers create playful volume and framing.


Best for: Medium-density curls or those transitioning from straight to natural.


Curly Hair Care & Styling Education

Healthy curls require the right home routine. As part of our Product Education & Hair Care series, we teach clients how to maintain hydration, shape, and frizz control using clean, sulfate-free products and minimal manipulation.


Salon-recommended steps:


– Cleanse gently once or twice weekly with moisturizing shampoo

– Deep condition every 5 to 7 days

– Apply curl cream or leave-in on soaking-wet hair

– Use a microfiber towel or cotton t-shirt to blot

– Diffuse with low heat or air-dry for best definition


Sustainable Curl Care

All curl services at Craft Collective align with our Sustainable & Eco-Friendly Salon values. We use cruelty-free, paraben-free, and biodegradable curl care systems, ensuring your beauty choices remain as kind to the planet as they are to your hair.


We also recycle product packaging, color tubes, and foils through Green Circle Salons to minimize waste.
