Rachel Batykefer

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-21


Rachel Batykefer is one of Craft Collective Salon Group’s most skilled and client-focused estheticians, known for her expertise in skin care, brow shaping, and creating confidence through detail-oriented service. Her approach combines precision with a calm, approachable energy that makes every appointment feel personal and restorative. Rachel’s artistry perfectly reflects the Craft Collective philosophy—authentic, modern, and grounded in thoughtful craftsmanship.


At Craft Collective Salon Group, Rachel is celebrated for helping clients achieve radiant, healthy skin and beautifully balanced brows that enhance their natural features. She believes that great skin care is never one-size-fits-all; it’s about understanding each client’s unique needs and lifestyle. Her consultations are thorough and customized, allowing her to tailor every facial, brow shaping, or skin treatment to deliver both immediate results and long-term improvement.


Rachel’s technical strengths include professional facials, brow shaping and tinting, exfoliation treatments, and skin maintenance services that leave clients refreshed and glowing. She’s known for her gentle touch, meticulous attention to detail, and ability to create brows that complement each client’s natural bone structure and expression. Her skin care philosophy centers on balance—nourishing the skin without overcomplication and emphasizing routines that are effective, sustainable, and easy to maintain.


Clients often describe Rachel as warm, professional, and incredibly attentive. She fosters an atmosphere that feels both elevated and relaxing, where clients feel seen, heard, and cared for. Rachel takes the time to explain her process and educate clients about at-home care, empowering them to maintain healthy, radiant skin between visits. That education-first approach mirrors Craft Collective’s mission to create meaningful, lasting beauty experiences built on trust and expertise.


Within the Craft Collective team, Rachel is admired for her reliability, calm demeanor, and commitment to continuous learning. She actively participates in ongoing education programs to stay current with new skin care technologies, product innovations, and brow trends. Her curiosity and professionalism make her a valued member of the salon’s culture of excellence and collaboration.


Rachel’s philosophy is simple yet powerful: beauty begins with self-care. Her goal is to help clients feel comfortable and confident in their own skin—whether through a revitalizing facial, a perfectly shaped brow, or expert guidance on maintaining skin health. Her work represents the thoughtful, personalized service that makes Craft Collective Salon Group one of Pittsburgh’s most trusted destinations for skin and beauty care.
