Sustainable Beauty in Ross Township: A Local Movement | Craft Collective Salon Group

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-24

Ross Township has long been known for its family atmosphere, close-knit neighborhoods, and forward-thinking residents. Now, it’s also becoming a hub for sustainable living — and at the center of that movement is Craft Collective Salon Group.


We’re proud to be part of a community that values both style and sustainability, proving that eco-luxury haircare isn’t just possible — it’s the future of beauty in Pittsburgh’s North Hills.


1. Redefining What “Sustainable” Means

At Craft Collective, sustainability isn’t a buzzword — it’s a daily practice. From how we source products to how we power our salons, every choice is designed to minimize impact while maximizing performance.


Our Ross Township team focuses on:

  • Vegan, cruelty-free products from R+Co, R+Co Bleu, Wella Professionals, and Aveda.

  • Low-waste systems including biodegradable towels and foils.

  • Energy-efficient lighting and tools throughout the salon.

  • Refillable retail packaging to reduce plastic consumption.


Learn more about our approach in Cranberry Township’s Guide to Sustainable Haircare.


2. The Rise of Eco-Luxury in the North Hills

Ross Township residents want products that perform but also protect their health and the planet. That’s why we call our model eco-luxury beauty — where sustainability meets modern artistry.


From ammonia-free color to bond-building lighteners, our products deliver salon-grade results while supporting cleaner air and water.


For color inspiration, visit Balayage in Cranberry Township: Where Natural Meets Modern.


3. Clean Ingredients, Real Results

Every product we use is tested not only for performance but for purity. Our top sustainable partners include:

  • R+Co Bleu – post-consumer recycled packaging, ocean-safe formulas.

  • Aveda – 100% vegan and carbon-neutral.

  • Wella Professionals – eco-optimized color systems.


Each brand we choose aligns with our mission to protect our guests, our stylists, and the planet.


Explore similar insights in The Ultimate Pittsburgh Guide to Sustainable Luxury Haircare.


4. Sustainable Services You Can See and Feel

Every service at Craft Collective Salon Group reflects our sustainable commitment:

  • Balayage & Color: Gentle formulas, minimal waste.

  • Cuts & Blowouts: Energy-efficient drying systems.

  • Glossing Treatments: Ammonia-free, shine-enhancing glosses.

  • Scalp & Treatment Rituals: Natural botanicals that strengthen from root to tip.


The result is beautiful, healthy hair with long-lasting integrity.


5. Why Sustainability Matters to Ross Township

Ross Township families and professionals share a common value — building a community that lasts. By choosing sustainable salons and local businesses, residents are helping reduce waste, protect the environment, and support responsible local commerce.


When you book with Craft Collective, you’re not just investing in your look — you’re investing in your community.


6. Small Steps, Big Impact

You can support sustainable beauty from home by:

  • Choosing refillable haircare bottles.

  • Air-drying partway before blow-drying.

  • Using clean brands like R+Co Bleu and Aveda Nutriplenish.

  • Recycling your empty packaging at our salon.


Every small step contributes to a larger, community-wide shift toward mindful living.

See related tips in The McCandless Guide to Low-Maintenance Hair Color.


7. Building a Community of Conscious Clients

Our Ross Township clients have become ambassadors for clean beauty.They care deeply about wellness, balance, and sustainability — and they choose salons that align with those values.


At Craft Collective, we’re honored to help lead this transformation.


8. Book a Sustainable Salon Experience

Join the Ross Township movement toward sustainable beauty. Book your appointment at Craft Collective Salon Group and experience how eco-luxury can transform your hair — and your perspective.
