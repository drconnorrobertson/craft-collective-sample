Top Hair Treatments in Ross Township for Healthy, Resilient Hair | Craft Collective Salon Group

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-22

Ross Township clients know that healthy hair never happens by accident — it’s built through care, consistency, and custom treatments that strengthen from the inside out. At Craft Collective Salon Group, our stylists specialize in sustainable restorative services that revive shine, repair damage, and protect color.


1. Bond Repair Treatments

Our signature bond-building services use Wella FusionPlex and Aveda Botanical Repair to rebuild weakened disulfide bonds. These treatments strengthen each strand, reduce breakage, and leave hair silky smooth.


Pair this with a Glossing & Shine session for the ultimate post-color recovery.


2. Moisture Infusion Masks

Pittsburgh’s climate dries out hair fast. Our hydration rituals use R+Co Bleu Optical Illusion Oil and Aveda Nutriplenish to deliver plant-powered moisture without weight. Clients love how hair feels revived and manageable immediately after.


Learn more about eco-friendly care in Sustainable Beauty in Ross Township: A Local Movement.


3. Glossing for Shine and Tone

Our stylists finish many treatment appointments with a zero-damage gloss. Using Wella Shinefinity or Aveda Botanical Repair Gloss, we seal the cuticle and restore natural reflection.The result: mirror-like shine that lasts 6–8 weeks.


4. Scalp Health Therapies

Healthy hair starts with a healthy scalp. Our detox and nourishment services use R+Co Crown Scalp Scrub and Aveda Pramasana to remove buildup, balance pH, and stimulate growth. These soothing treatments also extend the life of color and style.


5. Eco-Conscious Care for Lasting Results

All our treatments are performed with biodegradable towels, energy-efficient tools, and refillable product systems. Sustainability is built into every rinse and mask.See our commitment on the Sustainable Beauty Philosophy page.


6. Home Care Tips from Your Stylist

Maintain salon results with:

  • R+Co Gemstone Shampoo & Conditioner for color protection.

  • Aveda Leave-In Treatment twice weekly.

  • Gentle air-drying or low-heat styling with thermal protection.


For shine maintenance between visits, see The Cranberry Glossing & Shine Revival Experience.


7. Book Your Ross Township Treatment

Your hair deserves more than a trim — it deserves therapy. Book your bond repair or hydration treatment at Craft Collective Salon Group Ross Township today and experience the perfect blend of science, artistry, and sustainability.


Schedule online at craftcollectivesalongroup.com.
