Supporting Local Artists and Creators in North Hills

Craft Collective Team · 2025-12-21


Great hair and great art have something in common: they both tell a story. At Craft Collective Salon Group, our passion goes beyond the salon chair. We believe creativity thrives when it’s shared, celebrated, and rooted in community. That’s why we’ve made it part of our mission to support local artists and creators across North Hills and Pittsburgh, turning our salon into a hub for connection, collaboration, and inspiration.


From featuring local artwork on our walls to hosting pop-up showcases and creative partnerships, Craft Collective is more than a salon; it’s a collective of dreamers, doers, and makers shaping a vibrant creative scene right here at home.


1. Why Supporting Local Matters

In a world dominated by mass production and fast trends, local creativity stands out as something real and human.


When you support local artists and makers, you:


  • Strengthen the North Hills creative economy.

  • Keep money circulating within the community.

  • Build a network of collaboration that inspires innovation.

  • Help emerging artists gain exposure and sustainable careers.


Our stylists and artists share the same goal: to make people feel something, whether through a haircut, a painting, or a handcrafted piece of jewelry.


2. Art Meets Beauty: Our Salon as a Creative Space

Step inside Craft Collective, and you’ll notice something different. The walls aren’t filled with generic prints or sterile décor. Instead, they showcase rotating exhibits by local North Hills artists, photographers, painters, and mixed-media creators who bring fresh energy to our salon environment.


Each display transforms our space into a gallery, a living, breathing art experience that evolves with the seasons.


When you sit in our chair for your color session or haircut, you’re surrounded by creativity that reminds you this community is full of talent waiting to be discovered.


Featured collaborations have included:


  • Local fine artists from the Pittsburgh Creative Arts Network.

  • Handmade jewelry designers from nearby boutiques.

  • Photographers who capture the essence of self-expression through portrait work.


(See also: “Why Community Matters in Modern Salons” for more on our connection-first philosophy.)


3. Spotlight: Our Artist Showcase Series

To amplify local talent, we launched the Craft Collective Artist Showcase, a quarterly event highlighting emerging creators from North Hills and beyond.


During these evenings, the salon transforms into a pop-up gallery and event space. Guests sip local coffee, meet the artists, and enjoy a curated lineup of works ranging from digital art to abstract painting, all surrounded by the buzz of conversation and collaboration.


It’s not just an exhibit, it’s an experience where the creative worlds of beauty and art merge.


We’ve also started featuring musicians and live acoustic performers during special events, giving every sense something to connect with.


4. Local Partnerships that Make a Difference

Collaboration is the heartbeat of creativity. We proudly partner with small businesses and creators who share our values.


Some of our favorite collaborations include:


  • Local florists who design seasonal installations for salon events.

  • Independent candle makers whose scents fill our retail shelves.

  • Artisanal jewelry brands featured in limited pop-up displays.


Every partnership reflects the Craft Collective's promise to support talent that makes North Hills more beautiful, one connection at a time.


5. Giving Back Through Creative Events

Beyond collaboration, we host charity events that directly benefit local causes, blending beauty and philanthropy.


Our “Cuts for a Cause” and “Style & Support” events bring together stylists, artists, and clients for a day of creativity that gives back. Proceeds from these events go toward:


  • Local youth art programs

  • Women’s shelters

  • Small nonprofit organizations across the North Hills


(Read more in: “Hosting Charity Events at Craft Collective.”)

These initiatives remind us that creativity isn’t just about making something new; it’s about using your gifts to lift others up.


6. How We Feature Local Creators in Everyday Salon Life

Every corner of Craft Collective tells a story:


  • The art above our shampoo bar? Painted by a Pittsburgh graduate student.

  • Are the ceramics holding our tools? Hand-thrown by a North Hills potter.

  • Even our salon playlists often feature independent Pittsburgh musicians.


We regularly tag and promote these creators on our Instagram and Facebook pages, helping new audiences discover their work.


Because when you support a local artist, you’re not just buying art, you’re believing in someone’s dream.


7. The Intersection of Hair and Art

Hairstyling is an art form. Every color blend, every textured cut, every transformation is a visual story shaped by light, form, and feeling.


Many of our stylists have creative backgrounds in painting, design, or fashion, and that artistry shows in their work. Our stylists often collaborate with local photographers for editorial shoots, creative lookbooks, and community campaigns that highlight both our hair artistry and the local creative ecosystem.


These collaborations not only showcase Craft Collective talent but also create portfolio opportunities for rising local artists.


8. How You Can Support Local Artists in North Hills

Supporting local doesn’t always mean making a purchase, though that helps! It’s also about showing up, sharing, and connecting.


You can:


  • Attend local gallery nights and salon showcases.

  • Follow and engage with artists online.

  • Gift locally made pieces to friends and family.

  • Book collaborative sessions with creative professionals (hairstylists, photographers, designers).


By participating, you’re helping build a more connected, art-driven North Hills — one that inspires the next generation of creators.


9. What’s Next: Craft Collective Creative Residency Program

In 2026, we’re introducing the Craft Collective Creative Residency, offering one local artist per quarter the chance to display, sell, and promote their work in our salon.


Residents will also receive promotional features on our website and social media, along with professional portraits and networking opportunities.


Our goal is to give artists a platform that elevates their careers while enriching the salon experience for our guests.


Join the Craft Collective Movement

At Craft Collective Salon Group, we’re redefining what it means to be a local business, by celebrating the people who make North Hills unique. Every brushstroke, every haircut, every creative spark contributes to a stronger, more connected community.


If you’re a local artist, creator, or maker who’d like to collaborate, we’d love to hear from you. Visit our website or stop by the salon to learn how you can be part of the Craft Collective creative community in North Hills.


Together, we’re not just styling hair, we’re shaping culture.
