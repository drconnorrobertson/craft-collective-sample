Top 2025 Haircare Innovations Coming to Craft Collective Salon Group | Craft Collective Salon Group

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-26

At Craft Collective Salon Group, we believe staying ahead means blending artistry with innovation. As 2025 unfolds, our salons across Wexford, Allison Park, and Ross Township are embracing the newest advancements in sustainable beauty, professional performance, and client experience. These updates are designed to redefine what modern haircare feels like—cleaner, smarter, and more personalized than ever before.


1. Bond-Repair Technology That Works in Minutes

We’re introducing next-generation bond-repair systems from Wella Professionals and Aveda that repair damage in under 10 minutes. This fast-acting innovation strengthens your hair on a molecular level, leaving it smoother, shinier, and more resilient without adding time to your appointment.


Our stylists are trained in these advanced systems to ensure results that are both immediate and long-lasting.


2. AI-Powered Color Formulation

Precision color is an art—and soon, it’s also a science. Craft Collective is implementing digital tools that allow stylists to analyze tone, lighting, and color history using intelligent technology. This ensures perfect consistency in your shade every single visit.


We’ll still rely on our stylists’ creative eye, but the process will now be guided by data-backed precision for a truly customized finish.


3. Sustainable Refill Bars

Starting in 2025, Craft Collective salons will offer refill bars for R+Co Bleu and Aveda products, reducing plastic waste while giving clients access to professional-grade care at home. Bring your reusable bottle, and refill your essentials sustainably—less packaging, same luxury.


Learn more about our eco-luxury model in Sustainable Beauty in Wexford: Clean, Conscious, and Confident.


4. Smart Blowout Systems

Our new energy-efficient dryers feature airflow control that adjusts to your hair’s moisture level, cutting drying time by up to 40%. This technology not only saves energy but also protects hair from heat fatigue. Expect blowouts that last longer, shine brighter, and feel lighter than ever.


For more styling inspiration, visit The Ultimate Blowout and Styling Experience in Wexford.


5. Ingredient Transparency & Clean Certification

All our salons are expanding clean certification partnerships, verifying ingredient integrity across every product line. Transparency will now be standard—clients can easily see sourcing, impact, and recyclability information for every formula used in their service.


The Future of Sustainable Luxury

These innovations are more than trends—they represent our promise to lead the North Hills Pittsburgh salon community toward smarter, cleaner, and more conscious beauty.
