Keratin & Smoothing Treatments in North Hills: Long-Lasting Frizz Control and Sleek, Polished Hair

Craft Collective Team · 2025-12-13


If you struggle with frizz, humidity, or hair that never seems to stay smooth no matter what you do, a keratin or smoothing treatment may be the answer. At Craft Collective Salon Group in North Hills, we offer professional-grade smoothing systems that reduce frizz, add shine, soften texture, and make styling dramatically easier, all while keeping your hair healthy.


These treatments don’t just coat the hair; they transform it into a sleeker, more manageable version of itself for months at a time.


What Is a Keratin or Smoothing Treatment?

Keratin and smoothing systems work by infusing the hair with proteins, amino acids, and smoothing agents that repair the cuticle and create long-lasting frizz control.


They provide:


– Reduced frizz

– Easier styling

– Faster blow-dry time

– Smoother texture

– Enhanced shine

– Humidity resistance

– Softer, more polished movement


Unlike chemical straightening, modern keratin and smoothing treatments preserve your natural hair pattern while reducing unwanted texture.


Who Benefits Most From Keratin & Smoothing Treatments?

These treatments are ideal for clients who have:


– Frizzy or puffy hair

– Humidity-sensitive hair

– Unmanageable curls or waves

– Coarse or dense texture

– Dull or rough-feeling hair

– Difficulty blow-drying

– Hair that swells throughout the day

– Color-treated or lightened hair needing polish


Keratin treatments also help protect hair from the Pittsburgh humidity, especially during the summer and rainy seasons.


Types of Smoothing Systems at Craft Collective

Our stylists customize your smoothing results based on hair type, curl pattern, and desired finish.


1. Keratin Smoothing Treatment

This is the most popular option for long-lasting frizz control (typically 3 to 5 months).It:


– Softens curls and waves

– Smooths the cuticle

– Adds high-gloss shine

– Reduces styling time


Great for clients who want sleekness but still want to retain some natural movement.


2. Amino Acid / Non-Keratin Smoothing

A gentle, formaldehyde-free option ideal for:


– Sensitive clients

– Fine hair

– Pregnant clients (with stylist guidance)

– Clients who want frizz reduction without curl relaxation


This treatment lasts 2 to 3 months and leaves hair soft and natural.


3. Express Smoothing Treatment

Perfect for clients needing a quick refresh before an event, vacation, or the holidays.Lasts 4 to 6 weeks and adds instant shine and smoothness.


4. Curl-Control Softening Treatment

Designed for clients who want to loosen tight curls without fully straightening. Leaves curl bouncily, are defined, and are easier to style.


Smoothing for Color-Treated or Lightened Hair

Color clients benefit tremendously from smoothing treatments because they:


– Seal the cuticle after color

– Reduce porosity

– Prevent dryness

– Eliminate frizz

– Improve tone reflection (especially blondes)

– Make color appear richer and more dimensional


Pair perfectly with: Glossing & Shine Enhancements for even more reflective shine.


Smoothing for Extension Wearers

While keratin is not applied directly on extensions, smoothing treatments can still:


– Reduce frizz in natural hair

– Improve blending

– Make styling dramatically easier

– Prevent puffiness around the root

– Maintain soft, smooth mids and ends

How Long Do Keratin & Smoothing Treatments Last?

Longevity depends on hair type and aftercare, but typically:


– Keratin: 3 to 5 months

– Amino Acid Smoothing: 2 to 3 months

– Express Smoothing: 4 to 6 weeks


To prolong results:


– Use sulfate-free shampoo & conditioner

– Avoid sodium chloride (salt) in hair products

– Reduce frequent washing

– Protect hair when swimming

– Minimize heat without protection

What to Expect During the Service

Your stylist will:


  1. Cleanse the hair thoroughly

  2. Apply the smoothing formula strategically

  3. Process the treatment

  4. Blow-dry

  5. Seal the treatment with a smoothing iron


The result is an instantly sleeker, shinier, softer finish.


Immediate & Long-Term Results

After one treatment, clients notice:


– 80–90% reduction in frizz

– Shinier, smoother, silkier hair

– Better humidity resistance

– More manageable curls

– Faster at-home styling

– Longer-lasting blowouts


Over time, the hair becomes healthier and more polished with each treatment.
