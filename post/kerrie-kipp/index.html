Kerrie Kipp

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-21


Kerrie Kipp is one of Craft Collective Salon Group’s most established and respected stylists, admired for her impeccable technique, seasoned artistry, and commitment to creating styles that exude both polish and personality. With years of experience and a reputation for consistency, Kerrie has become a go-to professional for clients seeking a dependable stylist who can deliver elevated, timeless looks with ease. Her work is a seamless reflection of Craft Collective’s mission to merge creativity, craftsmanship, and connection into every salon experience.


At Craft Collective Salon Group, Kerrie approaches every appointment with quiet confidence and careful attention to detail. She believes the best hair starts with listening, and her consultations are designed to uncover exactly what a client wants, even when they can’t quite put it into words. Her calm, intuitive presence helps clients feel understood and at ease from the moment they sit down. Whether it’s a subtle tone refresh, a layered haircut designed to frame the face, or a full color transformation, Kerrie brings the same level of focus and artistry to every service.


Her expertise lies in dimensional color, precision haircutting, and customized styling for a range of textures and face shapes. Kerrie’s work is marked by its balance never too trendy, never too simple, always perfectly tailored to her clients’ lifestyle and preferences. She has an eye for blending shades that complement skin tone and designing shapes that move naturally, making her one of the most versatile stylists on the Craft Collective team. Her color work is soft and seamless, while her haircuts are known for their longevity and effortless grow-out.


Clients love Kerrie’s professionalism, patience, and honesty. She provides transparent recommendations, explains her process clearly, and ensures that each person leaves feeling confident and cared for. Many of her clients have been with her for years, drawn to her consistency and commitment to quality. Her ability to create looks that remain fresh and wearable long after the salon visit has earned her a loyal following throughout Pittsburgh.


Kerrie is also a strong advocate for continued education and personal growth. She regularly participates in advanced training sessions and workshops through Craft Collective, where she stays current on modern coloring methods, updated cutting techniques, and new styling products that protect hair integrity. Her desire to keep improving after years of experience reflects the Craft Collective ethos no matter how long you’ve been in the industry, there’s always something new to learn.


Within the salon, Kerrie is a mentor and role model. She brings a steady, encouraging presence to the team, often sharing insights and helping newer stylists refine their techniques. Her collaborative spirit and professionalism contribute to the creative, supportive culture that sets Craft Collective Salon Group apart.


Kerrie’s design philosophy is centered on natural enhancement. She believes beauty should feel timeless and approachable, not overdone or unattainable. Every haircut and color she creates is designed to work with, not against, a client’s natural texture and lifestyle. That dedication to creating realistic, beautiful, and low-maintenance results is what keeps her booked and beloved.
