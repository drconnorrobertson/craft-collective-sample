How to Maintain Balayage Between Visits

Balayage is low-maintenance by design, but the right care helps preserve tone, vibrancy, and texture between appointments. Here’s what our stylists recommend:


  • Use a gentle, sulfate-free shampoo — we love R+Co’s “Gemstone” for color-treated hair.

  • Gloss and tone every 6–8 weeks to keep warmth under control.

  • Deep condition weekly to maintain shine and prevent dryness.

  • Schedule a refresh twice a year for brightness and placement updates.


You can learn more about year-round color maintenance in our post, How to Maintain Your Color Through Pittsburgh Winters.
