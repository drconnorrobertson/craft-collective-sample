The Best Haircuts for Round Faces Near Ross Park Mall | Craft Collective Salon Group

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-22

When it comes to haircuts that flatter round faces, balance is everything. At Craft Collective Salon Group near Ross Park Mall, our stylists specialize in designing shapes that elongate features and highlight natural bone structure — without sacrificing softness or movement.


Whether you love a polished lob or a textured bob, these are the cuts our Ross Township clients swear by.


1. The Long Layered Lob

A collarbone-length lob is universally flattering on round faces. It adds vertical lines to slim the cheeks while keeping a light, modern look. We customize each cut with face-framing angles that draw the eye down instead of across.


Our team uses R+Co Bleu’s “Essential Hair Tonic” for weightless shine and Wella Professionals’ bond repair system to protect ends.For more professional style inspiration, see Haircut Trends That Fit Wexford’s Business Lifestyle.


2. The Soft Shag

Modern shags add texture and light volume without bulk at the sides — perfect for round faces. Curtain bangs and piecey layers give movement and an effortless vibe.


We often pair this cut with Aveda’s heat-protecting styling foam for defined, soft waves. It works beautifully with our Balayage & Hair Color Guide for a dimensional finish that frames the face naturally.


3. Long Layers with Side Part

Keeping length while adding angled layers creates a lengthening effect. A side part adds asymmetry to offset roundness and highlight your eyes and jawline.


Our Ross Park Mall clients love combining this cut with glossing treatments from Wella Shinefinity for smooth reflection and manageable texture.Learn how to maintain radiance between appointments in The Secret to Long-Lasting Shine (Without Heavy Oils).


4. Medium Cuts with Invisible Layers

For clients who prefer shoulder length, we add “invisible layers” within the interior to remove bulk without changing the outline. This technique creates movement that flatters round faces while maintaining density at the bottom.


To keep the cuticle smooth and reflective, we finish with R+Co’s “High Dive” and Aveda’s Botanical Repair serum for hydration without weight.For hydration tips, see Best Hydrating Products for Dry Scalp.


5. Short and Textured Pixie

A pixie with tapered sides and extra height on top is a bold yet flattering choice for round faces. It adds dimension and elongates your silhouette.


Our Ross stylists use R+Co’s “Control Flexible Paste” to define texture and finish with R+Co Bleu’s “Optical Illusion Oil” for soft shine.


See related texture concepts in Why McCandless Loves Low-Maintenance Styles.


6. Consultation and Customization

Every face shape and texture deserves a personalized approach. At our Ross Township location, we start each appointment with a consultation to identify your hair goals, maintenance preferences, and styling habits.


We’ll recommend cuts that frame your features naturally and fit your lifestyle — whether you want volume, structure, or ease.


Book your consultation on the Ross Township Salon page.


7. Sustainable Styling in Ross Township

As with all our services, these cuts are backed by Craft Collective’s eco-conscious philosophy. From biodegradable towels to refillable bottles and energy-efficient tools, we make every service sustainable without compromise.


Learn more about our commitment to responsible beauty on the Sustainable Beauty Philosophy page.
