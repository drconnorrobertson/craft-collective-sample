The Role of Trims in Growing Healthy Hair in North Hills Pittsburgh

Craft Collective Team · 2026-09-07

Many people believe skipping trims helps hair grow faster, but regular trims are essential for growing strong, healthy hair. North Hills Pittsburgh clients trust Craft Collective Salon Group to maintain hair length while preventing damage and breakage through professional trimming schedules.


Why Trims Are Important for Hair Growth

Trims support healthy growth by:


  • Removing split and weakened ends

  • Preventing breakage from traveling up the hair shaft

  • Maintaining hair shape and structure

  • Improving overall hair appearance and manageability


Healthy ends allow hair to retain length more effectively over time.


How Often Should You Trim Your Hair?

North Hills Pittsburgh clients benefit from trim schedules based on hair type:


  • Fine or damaged hair: Every 6–8 weeks

  • Medium to thick hair: Every 8–10 weeks

  • Curly or textured hair: Every 10–12 weeks

  • Color-treated hair: More frequent trims to prevent dryness


Craft Collective stylists tailor trim schedules to individual needs and goals.


Combining Trims with Professional Treatments

Craft Collective Salon Group recommends pairing trims with:


  • Deep Conditioning Treatments: Restore moisture

  • Bond-Building Treatments: Strengthen hair structure

  • Gloss Treatments: Protect and enhance shine


This combination supports length retention and overall hair health.


At-Home Care to Support Hair Growth


  • Use gentle brushes and detangle carefully

  • Avoid excessive heat styling

  • Apply leave-in conditioners to protect ends

  • Maintain a balanced scalp care routine


Craft Collective stylists provide personalized at-home care recommendations for long-term success.


Why Clients Trust Craft Collective Salon Group

North Hills Pittsburgh residents rely on Craft Collective for expert trimming, personalized growth plans, and professional hair care guidance. Every client receives solutions designed to protect length and improve hair health.


Regular trims are a key component of growing healthy hair. North Hills Pittsburgh clients who follow Craft Collective Salon Group’s professional trimming schedules enjoy longer, stronger, and healthier hair over time. Healthy growth starts with proper maintenance.
