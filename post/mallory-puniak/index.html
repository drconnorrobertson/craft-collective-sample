Mallory Puniak

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-21


Mallory Puniak is one of Craft Collective Salon Group’s most innovative and creative stylists, admired for her thoughtful precision, calm professionalism, and commitment to helping clients find their unique sense of style. Her work perfectly captures Craft Collective’s guiding values elevated artistry, personalized attention, and the belief that every guest deserves an experience as exceptional as the final result.


At Craft Collective Salon Group, Mallory has become known for her ability to create looks that blend natural beauty with modern sophistication. She approaches each appointment with patience and intention, beginning with a detailed consultation to understand her clients’ personality, preferences, and goals. Whether she’s crafting a dimensional blonde, sculpting a soft layered cut, or refreshing a brunette tone with seamless color placement, Mallory ensures each style feels effortless, flattering, and easy to maintain.


Her color work stands out for its precision and balance. Mallory has mastered the art of creating tones that look luminous and natural under any light, with soft transitions and carefully placed highlights that enhance facial features. She’s equally skilled in haircutting, designing shapes that move naturally and maintain their structure long after the appointment. Her ability to combine technical excellence with creative intuition has made her one of the most requested stylists at Craft Collective.


Clients describe Mallory as kind, intuitive, and deeply professional. Her calm energy makes every visit comfortable, while her clear communication ensures clients know exactly what to expect. She’s especially loved for her ability to combine honesty with encouragement helping guests make confident decisions about their look while maintaining the integrity and health of their hair. Every person who sits in her chair leaves feeling refreshed, empowered, and authentically themselves.


Mallory is also a strong believer in continued growth and collaboration. She regularly participates in Craft Collective Salon Group’s advanced education programs, refining her skills in color theory, advanced cutting, and new product technologies. Her curiosity and passion for learning reflect the salon’s commitment to staying ahead of trends while maintaining a foundation of timeless technique.


Within the Craft Collective team, Mallory is a source of inspiration and support. Her collaborative mindset and positive attitude help foster the creative, inclusive environment that makes the salon one of Pittsburgh’s most respected destinations for professional hair care. She’s admired for her consistency, her artistic discipline, and her dedication to delivering exceptional results for every client, every time.


Mallory’s style philosophy is rooted in simplicity and balance she believes great hair should enhance, not overpower. Her work combines natural movement, harmonious tones, and effortless structure to create styles that are as versatile as they are beautiful. It’s this thoughtful approach that keeps her clients returning year after year.
