Best Balayage Near Ross Park Mall | Craft Collective Salon Group

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-24

When it comes to finding the best balayage near Ross Park Mall, local clients know there’s only one salon that consistently delivers: Craft Collective Salon Group. Nestled just minutes away in North Hills Pittsburgh, our salon has earned a reputation for modern, dimensional color that enhances every client’s natural beauty.


Balayage has become one of the most requested color techniques in Pittsburgh — and for good reason. This freehand, hand-painted approach creates a soft, blended gradient that looks effortlessly sun-kissed and grows out naturally between appointments. At Craft Collective, we take that artistry to another level by customizing every formula and placement to your skin tone, lifestyle, and desired brightness.


Why Our Balayage Stands Out

Balayage isn’t just a technique — it’s an art form. Our stylists receive ongoing training in the latest application methods from global leaders like Wella Professionals and Aveda Color. We don’t believe in cookie-cutter color. Instead, we analyze your base shade, undertones, and face shape to create a result that’s completely individualized.


Every balayage service begins with an in-depth consultation. We discuss the level of lift you want, the tone you’re drawn to (cool, neutral, or warm), and the maintenance plan that fits your schedule. Our goal is to give you hair that looks beautiful the day you leave and continues to look incredible weeks later.


Our stylists also use bond-strengthening systems and gloss treatments from Wella to protect your hair during lightening. This ensures your color stays vibrant, shiny, and healthy — even after multiple sessions.


Sustainable Products for Modern Color

At Craft Collective, we believe that high-performance beauty should be environmentally responsible. That’s why every balayage service includes products from R+Co and R+Co Bleu, both vegan and cruelty-free lines designed for professional results without harsh chemicals.


Our partnership with Aveda and Wella allows us to combine advanced color science with plant-powered care. We recycle whenever possible, use refillable backbar systems, and maintain a low-waste color bar that minimizes environmental impact.


Ross Township clients love knowing that when they sit in our chair, they’re supporting a salon that values both artistry and sustainability.


A Location Worth the Drive

We’re just a short drive from Ross Park Mall, located conveniently in the North Hills area. Whether you’re spending the afternoon shopping or planning a self-care day, we’re an easy stop for a luxury balayage appointment that transforms your entire look.


Many of our clients visit from nearby neighborhoods including West View, McCandless, and Shaler. They appreciate that our stylists combine high-end results with an approachable, welcoming salon atmosphere.


Maintenance Made Simple

Balayage is one of the lowest-maintenance color techniques available, but care still matters. We always send our guests home with personalized product recommendations — often from R+Co or Wella — to help preserve tone and shine.


To maintain your look, we suggest:


  • Using a sulfate-free, color-safe shampoo.

  • Scheduling a gloss or toner refresh every 6–8 weeks.

  • Visiting for a full balayage refresh once or twice a year.


Our stylists also provide at-home styling tips for longevity and protection — because great hair doesn’t stop when you leave the salon.


Why Ross Township Chooses Craft Collective

Clients across Ross Township and the greater Pittsburgh area trust Craft Collective Salon Group because of our attention to detail, education-driven culture, and modern approach to sustainable beauty.


We’re not just keeping up with trends — we’re setting them. Every stylist here believes in balancing creativity with care, giving each guest a result that feels natural, wearable, and timeless.


If you’re searching for “best balayage near Ross Park Mall,” visit Craft Collective Salon Group. You’ll find artistry, authenticity, and the kind of color that turns heads for all the right reasons.
