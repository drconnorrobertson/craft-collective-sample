Fall 2025 Hair Trends in Pittsburgh: Warmth, Dimension, and Effortless Luxury

Craft Collective Team · 2025-12-19


As the air cools and Pittsburgh’s skyline shifts to amber and gold, hair color trends follow suit. Fall 2025 is all about warmth, richness, and natural depth, tones that echo the season’s cozy textures and earthy palette. After months of summer lightening and humidity, this is the season for reset, restoration, and refinement.


At Craft Collective Salon Group in North Hills, we’re already seeing clients gravitate toward luxurious brunettes, creamy coppers, and dimensional blondes that feel soft yet polished. This is your complete guide to what’s trending this fall, from colors to treatments to the sustainable salon rituals keeping Pittsburgh clients glowing.


1. Cozy Copper & Burnished Bronze

The reign of copper continues, but this season, it’s subtler, deeper, and more sophisticated. Fall 2025 brings a burnished, earthy twist on last year’s bright apricot and strawberry tones.


Our stylists love blending bronze, cinnamon, and chestnut undertones into soft copper bases, creating warmth that complements every skin tone. The key is dimension, mixing warm lowlights or shadow roots for a believable, low-maintenance finish.


Pair this look with a glossing enhancement [link to gloss-shine-enhancements-north-hills] to boost shine and prevent fading as Pittsburgh’s humidity drops.


2. Chocolate Espresso Brunettes

If summer is for brightness, fall is for depth. This season’s brunettes are velvety and multi-tonal, with rich espresso, dark chocolate, and caramel accents that shimmer in low light.


Our colorists achieve this look through foilage or smudged lowlights, giving a subtle lift while preserving natural richness. Clients love this trend because it grows out beautifully with minimal upkeep.


Brunette tones like this pair perfectly with bond-repair treatments [link to hair-treatments-health-north-hills] that add resilience and gloss after a summer of UV exposure.


For an added layer of dimension, check out our Custom Color Formulation in North Hills guide, where every brunette is tailored to the individual’s undertone.


3. Rooted Blonde Revival

Fall blondes are evolving from summer’s bright beige to rooted warmth with golden dimension.


Think honey blonde, soft buttercream, and creamy beige, tones that feel lived-in, not overdone. A shadow root [link to root-smudge-shadow-root-north-hills] or balayage refresh extends the life of your color while making regrowth effortless.


A quick gloss service mid-season refreshes tone and adds a healthy glow as the weather cools.


4. Seasonal Reds: Auburn Meets Mulberry

Rich auburn tones are trending everywhere in Pittsburgh this fall, from the North Hills to downtown. What’s new is the depth: stylists are introducing mulberry and red-wine undertones for a more complex finish.


For fair skin, we love lighter shades of russet or ginger. For deeper skin tones, we layer burgundy or espresso roots to create contrast and radiance.


These shades photograph beautifully, perfect for autumn weddings, family portraits, or fall events.


5. The Polished Long Shag

Cuts are leaning natural this season, effortlessly styled yet intentional.


The long shag continues to dominate, now paired with subtle ’70s-inspired curtain bangs and face-framing layers. The goal is movement, not volume overload.


To maintain softness, our stylists use hydrating sprays and lightweight serums that prevent static and dryness.


If you’re looking to combine shape with shine, we recommend pairing your cut with a glossing or conditioning treatment to amplify the light-reflective finish.


6. Mushroom Blonde & Cool Neutrals

Neutral shades are trending again, and for good reason. After seasons of warmth, cool taupe and mushroom blonde tones offer a grounded, modern vibe.


We achieve this look through controlled toning sessions and semi-permanent glosses that maintain dimension without brassiness.


Clients who loved last season’s creamy beige can transition seamlessly into these cooler fall shades using our Hair Color for Every Season in North Hills approach.


7. Repairing & Resetting After Summer

Fall is the ultimate time to restore what summer took away. Chlorine, salt, and UV damage can leave hair porous and dull, so recovery is key.


At Craft Collective, we recommend a deep restorative treatment series every 4–6 weeks. These bond-repair and keratin-based formulas replenish strength, elasticity, and moisture.


You can learn more in our Hair Treatments & Health post, which outlines exactly how our stylists rebuild your hair’s structure post-summer.


8. Embracing Low-Maintenance Color

“Lived-in” is the new luxury. Fall’s biggest movement is color that looks refined without constant upkeep.


Techniques like root smudging, balayage blending, and dimensional glossing ensure your color grows out naturally.


This approach not only saves time but also supports hair health, reduces chemical stress, more shine. For detailed guidance, visit Lived-In Color & Low-Maintenance Blends in North Hills.


9. Texture Meets Shine

Fall is dry-air season, which means texture can quickly lose its luster. Clients are focusing on achieving both movement and smoothness, styling hair to reflect light naturally.


Our stylists recommend:

  • Leave-in conditioners for moisture balance.

  • Glossing treatments for reflective shine.

  • Smoothing serums for humidity resistance without heaviness.


The result? Touchably soft hair that still moves with the crisp autumn breeze.


10. Sustainable Fall Beauty

Craft Collective continues to lead Pittsburgh salons in sustainability. This season’s color trends use low-tox, cruelty-free, ammonia-light formulations that deliver premium performance while protecting the planet.


We’re proud members of Green Circle Salons, recycling foils, color tubes, and even hair clippings to reduce waste. Learn more about our values in Sustainable & Eco-Friendly Salon North Hills.


Because true beauty is conscious beauty.


11. At-Home Fall Hair Care Routine

The shift in seasons calls for a shift in products. Swap lightweight summer formulas for nourishing, protective ones.


Here’s your fall checklist:

  • Moisture-rich shampoo and conditioner

  • Heat protectant for cooler-weather styling

  • Bond repair leave-in for strength

  • Gloss-safe shampoo for longevity

  • Weekly hair mask for hydration


A personalized take-home plan ensures your salon results last well into winter.


12. Color & Cut Combinations We Love

Fall color works best when paired with complementary cuts.


  • Soft layers for balayage and dimensional brunettes.

  • Blunt bobs for copper and mushroom tones.

  • Long shags for rich brunettes or auburn hues.


Pairing the right cut and color ensures balance between texture, tone, and personality. For ideas, see Spring 2025 Hair Trends to Try and Root Smudge & Shadow Root Techniques.
