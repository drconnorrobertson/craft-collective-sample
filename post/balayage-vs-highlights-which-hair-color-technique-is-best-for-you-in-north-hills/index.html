Balayage vs Highlights: Which Hair Color Technique Is Best for You in North Hills?

Craft Collective Team · 2025-11-26


At Craft Collective Salon Group, color is an art form, one that blends technique, tone, and personality. Among the most common questions we hear from guests is: What’s the difference between balayage and highlights? Both add dimension and brightness, but they offer very different effects and maintenance levels.


Whether you’re drawn to sun-kissed, low-maintenance tones or crave bold, defined brightness, understanding these two color methods helps you choose what fits your lifestyle and style goals.


What Is Balayage?

Balayage (pronounced “bah-lay-ahge”) is a French term meaning “to sweep.” It describes the way colorists hand-paint lightener onto the hair in soft, sweeping motions to create a natural, sunlit effect.


Unlike traditional foils, balayage isn’t applied in perfectly sectioned lines. Instead, stylists freehand the placement to mimic where the sun would naturally lighten your hair, around the face, crown, and mid-lengths.


At Craft Collective Salon Group, our balayage specialists tailor every placement to your haircut, texture, and desired brightness level. The result is soft, dimensional color that grows out gracefully and looks effortless for months.


The Benefits of Balayage

– Low maintenance: Perfect for clients who prefer longer gaps between appointments.

– Natural dimension: The blend looks softer than foil highlights, with no harsh regrowth lines.

– Customizable tone: From golden beige to cool ash, balayage can be fine-tuned for every complexion.

– Healthier look: Less frequent touch-ups mean reduced exposure to lightener and heat.


Ideal Candidates for Balayage

– Clients who love a natural, beachy look

– Busy professionals who don’t want constant maintenance

– First-time color clients

– Those transitioning from darker to lighter shades


What Are Highlights?

Traditional highlights use foils to section and lighten specific strands of hair from root to tip. This technique allows precise control and maximum brightness, especially close to the scalp.


At Craft Collective, our stylists use foils strategically to create consistent, even lift and contrast. While balayage is freehand art, foiling is architectural design, clean, symmetrical, and structured.


The Benefits of Highlights

– Maximum brightness: Perfect for achieving bold, high-contrast color.

– Even lift: Foils hold heat, allowing for lighter results.

– Custom placement: Ideal for full blonding or detailed pattern work.

– Adaptable: Works beautifully for both subtle dimension and striking blonde transformations.


Ideal Candidates for Highlights

– Clients who love bright, consistent blonde

– Those wanting root-to-tip lightness

– People who plan to maintain touch-ups every 6 to 8 weeks

– Clients with fine or smooth hair that shows placement beautifully

In short: If you love low-maintenance, natural beauty, go for balayage. If you want high-impact blonde and definition, traditional highlights are your best friend.


The Hybrid Approach: Foilage

For many of our North Hills clients, the perfect answer isn’t either/or, it’s both.


Foilage combines the softness of balayage with the brightness of foils.


Your stylist paints sections by hand, then wraps select pieces in foil for added lift. This hybrid technique gives the seamless, organic flow of balayage with the brightness of highlights.


It’s a perfect choice for brunettes wanting lightness or blondes craving dimension.

Every color service begins with a detailed consultation. We evaluate:

– Your hair’s health, texture, and porosity

This allows our colorists to design a custom plan, sometimes balayage, highlights, often a creative mix of both.


At Craft Collective, personalization is everything.


Maintaining Your New Color

Whether you choose balayage, highlights, or foilayage, care at home is what keeps your color salon-fresh.


Our stylists recommend sulfate-free, color-safe shampoo and conditioner, paired with regular gloss or toner appointments every 6 to 8 weeks. You can learn more about at-home maintenance in our Product Education & Hair Care guide [link to product-education-hair-care-north-hills].


We also encourage clients to schedule quarterly deep conditioning and bond-repair treatments [link to hair-treatments-health-north-hills] to protect their investment and keep hair silky and strong.
