Sustainable Beauty & Product Philosophy | Craft Collective Salon Group North Hills Pittsburgh

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-26

At Craft Collective Salon Group, we believe that great hair shouldn’t come at the expense of the planet. Located in North Hills Pittsburgh, our salon is built on the idea that sustainability, creativity, and beauty can coexist — seamlessly. From the products we choose to the way we run our salon, every decision is guided by a commitment to environmental responsibility and clean beauty.


The modern salon experience has evolved. Clients today want more than just a haircut or color; they want to know their stylist is making mindful choices that align with their values. At Craft Collective, we’ve embraced that responsibility fully — combining artistry with ethics to create a salon experience that feels as good as it looks.


Our sustainability philosophy begins with the brands we trust. We’ve carefully partnered with leaders in eco-conscious innovation:


  • R+Co

  • R+Co Bleu

  • Wella Professionals

  • Aveda Color


Each brand shares our commitment to clean ingredients, responsible sourcing, and recyclable packaging — ensuring every product that touches your hair supports both your wellness and the planet’s.


The Meaning of Sustainable Beauty

For us, sustainable beauty is about more than eco-friendly packaging. It’s a mindset — a promise to consider impact in every aspect of our work. That includes how we formulate, how we educate, and how we engage with our clients. We believe sustainability must be practical, not performative. That’s why we’ve built systems in our North Hills salon that allow every stylist to participate in meaningful environmental care.


From reducing water usage with efficient basins to using energy-saving lighting and recycling every possible material, sustainability at Craft Collective isn’t an afterthought — it’s an operating standard. We see each small action as part of a bigger movement toward responsible beauty in the Pittsburgh community.


Our Product Partners: Innovation Meets Integrity

R+CoR+Co has revolutionized professional haircare by eliminating unnecessary additives and focusing on performance-driven clean formulations. Every product is color-safe, vegan, cruelty-free, and formulated without parabens, sulfates, mineral oils, or petrolatum. The packaging is 100% recyclable and features striking art inspired by creative culture — a perfect match for Craft Collective’s brand of artistry and authenticity.


We use R+Co products for everyday styling, texture, and shine because they deliver consistent results without buildup or residue. The formulas are lightweight yet powerful, giving our stylists creative freedom while protecting the hair’s health.


R+Co BleuR+Co Bleu is R+Co’s couture line — a luxury collection built around sustainability. Every bottle is made from 100% post-consumer recycled plastic, and every formula is certified vegan, cruelty-free, and gluten-free. The line is infused with a proprietary Blue Molecule Complex designed to repair and protect at the molecular level.


We use R+Co Bleu for clients who want indulgent results without environmental compromise. Its sustainability isn’t just a label — it’s woven into the brand’s DNA, from sourcing to production to packaging. The textures, fragrances, and finishes elevate the salon experience, making eco-conscious beauty feel like luxury, not limitation.


Wella ProfessionalsWella has long been a pioneer in sustainable color innovation. Their formulas are designed for performance and protection — providing long-lasting vibrancy while minimizing environmental impact. Wella’s recycling initiatives and clean chemistry approach have helped reduce waste across the global salon industry.


We use Wella Professionals for our color and lightening services because it combines superior pigment control with advanced bond technology. This means we can achieve brilliant blondes and rich tones while keeping the hair strong, resilient, and healthy — even after complex color work.


Aveda ColorAveda has been synonymous with plant-powered beauty for decades. Their hair color systems are 96% naturally derived, vegan, cruelty-free, and manufactured with 100% wind and solar power. Aveda’s commitment to environmental stewardship and ethical sourcing mirrors our own philosophy at Craft Collective.


We love Aveda Color for clients seeking a botanical approach to hair color — one that delivers luminous shine and long-lasting vibrancy with minimal chemical exposure. It’s ideal for sensitive scalps, conscious consumers, and anyone who wants high performance through natural innovation.


Responsible Salon Practices

We believe sustainability should extend beyond the product bottle. At Craft Collective, we’ve reimagined how a salon can operate responsibly:


  • Energy Efficiency: Our North Hills space is equipped with LED lighting and low-flow shampoo basins that conserve both energy and water.

  • Recycling Systems: Every foil, tube, and color bowl is disposed of through specialized recycling programs that ensure proper material recovery.

  • Waste Reduction: We’ve eliminated single-use plastics wherever possible, using biodegradable gloves, refillable product systems, and eco towels.

  • Air Quality: We use advanced ventilation and low-tox color systems to maintain a clean, safe environment for clients and stylists alike.

  • Ethical Sourcing: We partner exclusively with manufacturers who maintain cruelty-free and fair-trade practices.


These measures create an environment where sustainability feels seamless — integrated into every touchpoint rather than added on top.


Educating Clients for Greener Beauty

We see sustainability as a shared journey between stylist and client. Our team takes pride in teaching eco-friendly haircare habits that make a difference beyond the salon chair. We educate clients on how to extend the life of their color, reduce heat exposure, and choose products that protect both their hair and the environment.


Our stylists recommend R+Co and R+Co Bleu for at-home care because they deliver salon-quality performance without environmental compromise. We also guide clients toward refillable packaging options and recycling programs for product empties. These small habits add up to meaningful impact when practiced collectively.


The Pittsburgh Approach to Sustainable Beauty

North Hills and the greater Pittsburgh area have seen a growing awareness of environmental responsibility across industries — and beauty is no exception. As one of the few eco-conscious salons in the region, Craft Collective Salon Group has become a leader in local sustainable innovation.


Our clients often tell us they’ve been searching for a salon that aligns with their values without sacrificing quality — and they find it here. By combining advanced techniques with sustainable practices, we’ve proven that modern beauty can be ethical, effective, and elegant all at once.


Why It Matters

Choosing sustainable products and methods isn’t just a business choice — it’s a personal one. We see every guest as part of a bigger ecosystem. The products we use wash into local waterways, the energy we consume affects our community footprint, and the culture we create influences the next generation of stylists.


At Craft Collective Salon Group, we’re building a legacy of mindful beauty that’s bigger than trends. It’s about showing that environmental consciousness and exceptional artistry can thrive together — right here in North Hills Pittsburgh.


The Craft Collective Commitment

We commit to continuously improving our practices and staying at the forefront of sustainability in the salon industry. That means partnering with innovative brands, participating in green salon programs, and educating our clients and staff on best practices for eco-conscious living.


Our team regularly attends workshops on sustainable salon management, waste diversion, and clean chemistry. These ongoing efforts ensure that we’re not only following industry standards but actively raising them.


A Greener Future for Beauty

Sustainability is no longer optional — it’s the future of beauty. As the North Hills community continues to evolve, Craft Collective Salon Group will remain a local leader in clean, conscious, and creative salon practices.


From eco-friendly lighting to ethically formulated color systems, we’re proving that every detail matters. Because when beauty and responsibility come together, everyone benefits — our clients, our team, and our planet.


If you’re searching for “eco-friendly salon Pittsburgh” or “sustainable beauty North Hills PA,” Craft Collective Salon Group is your destination. Experience what it means to look good, feel good, and do good — all in one chair.
