Logan Goetz

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-21


Logan Goetz is one of Craft Collective Salon Group’s standout stylists, known for her creativity, precision, and innate ability to craft hairstyles that reflect both artistry and individuality. Her approach to hairstyling embodies what Craft Collective Salon Group is best known for intentional beauty, expert technique, and a deep understanding of how personal style can elevate confidence. Logan has built her reputation in Pittsburgh as a stylist who listens, collaborates, and delivers consistently stunning, wearable results that make her clients feel their absolute best.


At Craft Collective Salon Group, Logan’s process begins with connection. She believes every great haircut or color starts with truly understanding the client their daily routine, their vision, and their comfort level with change. Her consultations are thoughtful and grounded, giving each guest the confidence that they’re in expert hands. Whether she’s designing a dimensional brunette, brightening a lived-in blonde, or reshaping a cut to enhance texture and movement, Logan approaches every detail with focus and care.


Her technical specialties include custom color blending, hand-painted balayage, and structured yet effortless cuts that grow out beautifully. Logan’s work is defined by balance: soft transitions, clean lines, and natural texture that make the hair look luxurious without appearing overdone. She has an intuitive understanding of tone and proportion, which allows her to create results that enhance her clients’ features and feel perfectly tailored to their look.


Clients often describe Logan as warm, professional, and deeply intentional in her work. She has a calm energy that instantly puts people at ease, transforming the salon experience into something both relaxing and inspiring. Her guests appreciate how she takes the time to explain her process, recommend customized maintenance plans, and ensure their hair stays healthy and strong long after the appointment. For many, sitting in Logan’s chair feels like reconnecting with a trusted friend who happens to be a master of her craft.


Beyond her technical skill, Logan is a valued collaborator within the Craft Collective team. She participates actively in the salon’s advanced education programs, refining her technique and keeping pace with modern trends in color and cutting. Her commitment to professional growth mirrors the salon’s broader culture of continuous learning and excellence. She’s also known for her collaborative nature always ready to share insights, support her peers, and contribute to the positive, creative energy that makes Craft Collective Salon Group one of Pittsburgh’s premier salons.


Her artistry reflects her belief that the best hair is hair that feels natural to the person wearing it. Logan focuses on crafting styles that move easily, maintain their integrity, and enhance her clients’ everyday confidence. She believes beauty should feel attainable—something that makes life easier, not harder. Her precision and practicality, combined with her creative edge, are what make her one of the most requested stylists in the salon.


Logan’s design philosophy aligns with Craft Collective’s mission to provide high-end, personalized hair care that’s rooted in craftsmanship and connection. Her ability to combine artistry with authenticity makes her an essential part of the salon’s growing legacy in Pittsburgh.
