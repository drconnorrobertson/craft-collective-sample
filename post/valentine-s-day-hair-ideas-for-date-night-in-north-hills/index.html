Valentine’s Day Hair Ideas for Date Night in North Hills

Craft Collective Team · 2025-12-20


Love is in the air, and your hair deserves to be part of the celebration. Whether you’re heading to a candlelit dinner, a cozy wine bar in Pittsburgh, or a casual movie date, your hairstyle sets the tone for the evening.


At Craft Collective Salon Group in North Hills, we believe Valentine’s Day isn’t just about the perfect outfit or bouquet; it’s about how you feel. The right hairstyle can make you feel confident, radiant, and effortlessly romantic.


From soft curls that say “timeless romance” to sleek buns that exude modern elegance, here are the top Valentine’s Day hair ideas to inspire your next date-night look, plus pro styling tips and maintenance tricks for the ultimate love-day glow.


1. Soft, Romantic Curls

The ultimate Valentine’s Day classic. Soft, cascading curls never fail to evoke a sense of romance and femininity.


Our stylists love using large-barrel curls brushed through for a natural, touchable finish, think Hollywood waves, but softer and more modern.


Pro Tip: Always start with a heat protectant before curling, and finish with a shine glossing spray for that photo-ready sheen.


For longer hair, add subtle face-framing highlights or balayage dimension to catch the light during dinner or under city lights.


2. The Effortless Half-Up Twist

If you want a balance between polished and relaxed, the half-up twist is your go-to Valentine’s style.


This look gently pulls back the top section of your hair, creating lift and showcasing your features while leaving the rest down in soft waves.


Our North Hills stylists love finishing it with a romantic pearl pin or ribbon accent; it’s flirty yet sophisticated.


Perfect Pairing: Soft blush makeup and a cozy off-shoulder sweater for an effortlessly sweet date-night look.


To keep the ends smooth and frizz-free, apply a leave-in conditioner after washing.


3. Sleek, Low Bun with Side Part

Nothing says modern romance like a sleek low bun. Chic, minimal, and timeless, this hairstyle complements every face shape and outfit, especially elegant evening wear.


Our stylists create clean lines using smoothing serums and tension-controlled blowouts, finishing with a soft side part for balance.


Why It Works: It keeps the focus on your face and accessories, letting your natural beauty take center stage.


Add a touch of scent, like a spritz of floral hair mist, for an extra romantic detail.


4. The “Falling in Love” Blowout

Big, bouncy blowouts are having their moment, and Valentine’s Day is the perfect occasion to show them off.


At Craft Collective, our signature satin blowout delivers weightless movement, volume, and mirror-like shine that lasts for days.


Salon Secret: We prep each blowout with heat protectant spray and finish with a glossing treatment to lock in radiance.


Pair it with soft curls or pin one side behind the ear for a flirty, asymmetrical twist.


5. Side-Swept Glam Waves

For clients wanting something dramatic and romantic, side-swept glam waves are always a hit.


This look channels red-carpet confidence while remaining wearable for real-life date nights.


Styling Steps:

  1. Create a deep side part.

  2. Curl large sections away from your face.

  3. Pin one side back and tuck behind your ear.

  4. Finish with shine serum.


This works beautifully on medium to long hair, especially when combined with a gloss refresh for reflective highlights.


6. Loose Braided Crown

If your Valentine’s date is more outdoorsy, maybe ice skating at PPG Place or dinner in a cozy cabin, a loose braided crown blends comfort and romance effortlessly.


It keeps hair out of your face while adding soft texture and charm.


Our stylists recommend weaving a few tendrils loose for movement and finishing with a soft hold spray for flexibility.


For an extra whimsical touch, weave in a small metallic ribbon or delicate hairpiece.


7. Polished Ponytail with a Twist

A ponytail might sound simple until you see how elevated it becomes with the right technique.


The polished low ponytail is sleek at the roots, voluminous at the ends, and wrapped with a strand of hair or velvet ribbon for a romantic finish.


Craft Collective Tip: Add a bond-repair treatment before styling to keep hair silky and strong, then finish with a light gloss spray for shine that lasts all night.


8. Retro Romance: 90s-Inspired Blowout

Nostalgia meets glamour in this 90s-inspired blowout, soft volume, flipped ends, and face-framing layers that create a dreamy, vintage effect.


Our stylists achieve this look using round brushes, volumizing mousse, and strategic curl direction to lift the hair while maintaining smoothness.


Why It’s Perfect for Valentine’s Day: It complements any outfit, from silk slip dresses to modern blazers. Plus, it’s versatile enough to wear straight to brunch the next morning.


Maintain the bounce with lightweight dry shampoo and a shine serum.


9. Romantic Updos for Short Hair

Short hair deserves just as much attention on date night.


For pixie cuts or chin-length bobs, try textured pin curls, mini twists, or a slicked-back wet look that exudes confidence and edge.


Adding a touch of sparkle, like a jeweled clip or barrette, instantly elevates the vibe.


Our stylists finish every short-hair updo with glossing treatments for shine and definition.


10. Color Enhancements for Valentine’s Glow

Great hair color enhances every style, and Valentine’s Day is the perfect excuse for a mini color refresh.


Trending tones for February:

  • Rose Gold Blonde: Romantic and soft, with a hint of warmth.

  • Cinnamon Brunette: Adds depth and glow for cozy dinners.

  • Cherry Mocha: A sultry nod to winter’s red trend.


To maintain that luminous color, our stylists recommend a custom gloss service every 6 to 8 weeks and sulfate-free shampoo.


11. Haircare Prep Before Date Night

To ensure your style lasts through dinner, dancing, and maybe a little rain or snow, follow these essentials:

  • Wash hair 24 hours before styling for better grip.

  • Apply heat protectant.

  • Use a moisturizing mask once a week during winter.

  • Finish with light-hold spray and shine gloss.


Healthy hair styles better, so keep up with trims and hydration for picture-perfect results.


12. Sustainable Romance: Eco-Friendly Styling

We believe love should extend to the planet, too.


Every Valentine’s Day appointment at Craft Collective is part of our Sustainable & Eco-Friendly Salon North Hills initiative. We use low-tox color lines, biodegradable foils, and cruelty-free styling products, because beauty should never come at Earth’s expense.


Even our candles and refreshment selections are sourced locally to support the North Hills community.
