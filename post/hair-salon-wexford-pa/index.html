Hair Salon in Wexford, PA | Craft Collective Salon Group

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-23

Just north of Pittsburgh and a few minutes from Wexford Plaza, Craft Collective Salon Group has become the salon of choice for Wexford residents who want elevated, modern, and sustainable hair services. Our salon blends creativity, luxury, and technique — offering a full menu of custom color, precision cuts, extensions, and bridal styling in a warm, modern environment.


Our stylists serve guests across the Wexford, McCandless, and North Hills areas who are looking for personalized beauty with an artistic edge. Whether you’re visiting for a sunlit balayage, a precision bob, or a full transformation, you’ll find unmatched expertise and care at every appointment.


We proudly use eco-conscious, high-performance product lines including R+Co, R+Co Bleu, Wella Professionals, and Aveda Color. Each brand reflects our commitment to clean beauty and modern artistry — a philosophy that defines everything we do at Craft Collective Salon Group.


A Destination for Wexford Professionals

Wexford’s fast-paced community of professionals and families values both quality and efficiency — and that’s exactly what we deliver. Our stylists know how to craft low-maintenance, high-impact styles that move seamlessly from business to casual.


Many of our Wexford clients visit us for dimensional highlights, subtle face-framing, and refresh cuts that are easy to maintain between appointments. Our stylists specialize in creating wearable, effortless luxury — looks that reflect your lifestyle, not just a trend.


Because we’re located so close to the Wexford corridor along Route 19, clients often stop in before or after work, or on weekends as part of their self-care routine. We’ve designed the experience to be as relaxing as it is inspiring.


Balayage and Modern Color Services

Balayage remains a client favorite in Wexford, and our stylists are recognized for their ability to blend tones that complement every skin tone and season. We use advanced color systems from Wella Professionals for depth and shine, and Aveda Color for clients who prefer natural, plant-based ingredients.


Whether you want bright summer blondes, rich fall coppers, or soft brunettes with lived-in texture, our colorists create results that feel fresh yet timeless. Each session begins with a consultation to determine the right level of lift, tone, and placement for your hair type.


We also specialize in color correction for clients seeking to restore balance or improve tone from previous services. Using Wella’s bond-strengthening technology, we repair compromised hair while achieving even, natural results.


Sustainable Luxury for Wexford

At Craft Collective Salon Group, we believe modern beauty should be responsible. Our partnership with R+Co and R+Co Bleu allows us to combine editorial-grade performance with eco-friendly practices.


R+Co Bleu’s luxury line features vegan formulas and 100% recycled packaging, aligning with our mission to reduce waste while delivering high-end results. Combined with Aveda’s sustainability initiatives and Wella’s clean color technology, our salon operates at the intersection of style and stewardship.


Clients from Wexford appreciate that they can enjoy a premium salon experience while supporting sustainable practices that protect the planet.


Precision Cuts and Styling Expertise

Every haircut at Craft Collective Salon Group begins with shape, balance, and texture. Our stylists are trained to analyze face shape, bone structure, and movement to design cuts that enhance natural features. From modern shags and lobs to classic layers and sleek bobs, we deliver technical precision with an effortless finish.


We use styling products from R+Co to create soft texture and lasting hold. The line’s vegan, paraben-free formulas allow us to achieve a flawless finish without buildup — perfect for Wexford clients who need styles that last through busy days and evenings out.


Bridal and Event Styling Near Wexford Plaza

Brides across Wexford and Cranberry choose Craft Collective Salon Group for timeless, photo-ready wedding looks. Our bridal specialists collaborate with clients to design hairstyles that balance beauty and comfort — ensuring every moment feels effortless.


From soft updos to modern waves and accessory integration, each bridal look is tailored to your dress, theme, and personality. Our salon also offers on-site wedding day services throughout the North Hills area.


Men’s Grooming and Professional Styling

Our Wexford guests include executives, athletes, and creatives who value clean, consistent grooming. We provide contemporary cuts, fades, and beard trims that maintain a polished look without excessive styling time.


Our stylists use lightweight, versatile R+Co products that help clients recreate the same results at home. Every appointment includes a consultation and product recommendations tailored to hair type and lifestyle.
