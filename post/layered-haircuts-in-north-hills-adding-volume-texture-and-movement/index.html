Layered Haircuts in North Hills: Adding Volume, Texture, and Movement

Craft Collective Team · 2025-12-05


The right haircut can completely transform how your hair looks, feels, and moves, and nothing achieves that more beautifully than a layered cut. At Craft Collective Salon Group in North Hills, our stylists specialize in creating customized layered haircuts that enhance your natural texture and bring shape, balance, and body back to your hair.


Layering is an art. It’s not just about cutting pieces shorter; it’s about strategically designing how weight, texture, and volume interact to create soft, effortless movement.


The Purpose of Layers

Layered haircuts work by removing weight in targeted areas while enhancing flow and direction. The result is balanced, bouncy hair that looks great styled or air-dried.


The benefits include:


– More volume at the crown or through the mid-lengths

– Enhanced texture for wavy or curly hair

– Reduced bulk in thick or coarse hair

– Seamless shape for long styles that need life

– Soft framing around the face for a flattering finish


Our stylists customize each haircut to your face shape and texture, from light, invisible layers to bold, defined shapes.


Popular Layering Styles

1. Long Layered Haircut

Perfect for maintaining length while adding shape and dimension. Long layers work especially well with balayage or dimensional brunette techniques because the movement helps showcase your color transitions.


2. Medium Layered Haircut

Medium-length hair thrives with balanced layering that creates lift and swing. This style pairs beautifully with a Gloss & Shine Enhancement to add light reflection and definition.


3. Short Layered Cut

For bobs or shoulder-length styles, short layering adds instant body and gives structure to fine or limp hair. Pair it with a Root Smudge & Shadow Root for an edgy, modern touch.


4. Face-Framing Layers

Designed to contour and highlight your best features, these layers soften the edges around your cheeks and jawline. Combine them with Face-Framing Highlights for a glowing, dimensional finish.


5. Butterfly & Shag Layers

A modern twist on the classic 70s-inspired look, airy, voluminous, and full of personality. These are perfect for clients seeking a lived-in, effortlessly styled vibe.


The Craft Collective Approach to Layering

Each layered haircut at Craft Collective begins with an in-depth consultation. Your stylist evaluates:


– Hair density and weight distribution

– Texture and curl pattern

– Growth direction and cowlicks

– Styling preferences and tools you use at home


We then tailor your haircut to align with your lifestyle, ensuring your new layers fall naturally, whether you blow-dry, curl, or air-dry daily.


Styling and Home Care

Layered haircuts are easy to maintain with the right products and techniques. To keep your layers healthy and defined:


– Use a lightweight volumizing spray or mousse for lift

– Apply smoothing serum to ends to prevent frizz

– Use heat protection from Product Education & Hair Care before styling

– Schedule trims every 8 to 10 weeks to preserve shape

– Treat hair monthly with hydration or bond-building treatments 


For styling education, our stylists demonstrate product layering and blow-dry techniques during every appointment, ensuring your haircut looks salon-fresh every day.


Sustainable Salon Practices

Your layered haircut is performed with the same care and purpose as every Craft Collective service. As part of our Sustainable & Eco-Friendly Salon program, we recycle all hair clippings and foils through Green Circle Salons, minimizing waste while supporting global environmental projects.
