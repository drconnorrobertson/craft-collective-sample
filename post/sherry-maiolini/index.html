Sherry Maiolini

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-21


erry Maiolini is one of Craft Collective Salon Group’s most experienced and dedicated stylists, known for her impeccable attention to detail, steady professionalism, and ability to create hair that feels both timeless and naturally beautiful. With years of experience behind the chair, Sherry has become a trusted name in Pittsburgh for clients seeking consistent, high-quality results and an experience grounded in care and craftsmanship. Her work embodies the Craft Collective Salon Group ethos artistry, authenticity, and connection in every service.


At Craft Collective Salon Group, Sherry approaches each client with patience, intention, and a deep respect for individuality. She takes the time to listen carefully, ensuring she understands not just what her clients want, but what makes them feel most confident. Her thoughtful consultations set the tone for a relaxed, collaborative appointment. Whether she’s performing a color refresh, designing a structured cut, or creating a completely new look, Sherry prioritizes balance, precision, and manageability hair that looks as good at home as it does in the salon.


Her technical expertise spans a wide range of services, including dimensional color, tonal correction, and tailored haircutting. Sherry’s color work is characterized by its seamless transitions and natural depth, delivering results that are soft, flattering, and easy to maintain. She’s particularly skilled in creating looks that grow out gracefully, allowing her clients to enjoy long-lasting beauty without excessive upkeep. Her haircuts are equally meticulous, emphasizing movement, proportion, and everyday wearability.


Clients describe Sherry as calm, caring, and deeply attentive. She brings an unmatched sense of professionalism to every service, ensuring that each appointment feels comfortable, unrushed, and focused entirely on the client’s needs. Many of her guests say they feel instantly at ease in her chair a testament to her genuine kindness and steady demeanor. Sherry’s clients trust her not only for her artistry but for her honesty and commitment to helping them maintain healthy, beautiful hair over time.


As part of the Craft Collective Salon Group team, Sherry plays a vital role in upholding the salon’s high standards of excellence. She actively participates in the salon’s ongoing education and professional development programs, refining her skills and staying current with modern techniques. Her openness to learning and evolution, even after years in the industry, exemplifies the spirit of continuous growth that defines Craft Collective.


Within the team, Sherry is valued for her reliability, mentorship, and collaborative approach. She’s the kind of stylist others look to for guidance and inspiration someone who leads quietly through consistency and care. Her commitment to her clients and colleagues alike strengthens the salon’s culture of trust, artistry, and shared success.


Sherry’s personal philosophy aligns perfectly with the Craft Collective vision: great hair should enhance, not overpower. She believes in creating beauty that feels natural, balanced, and effortless. Her work celebrates subtlety the soft transition of a color blend, the clean line of a cut, the gentle movement that makes hair come alive. For her, every detail matters because every client deserves to feel confident in their own authentic look.
