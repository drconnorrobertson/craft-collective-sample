Hair Treatments & Hair Health in North Hills: Restore, Strengthen, and Shine at Craft Collective Salon Group

Craft Collective Team · 2025-11-24


Healthy hair never happens by accident; it’s the result of mindful care, professional expertise, and the right treatments tailored to your unique needs. At Craft Collective Salon Group in North Hills, we treat every head of hair as an individual canvas. Whether you need deep repair after color, hydration for frizz control, or scalp rejuvenation for growth, our salon provides targeted, science-backed solutions that bring your hair back to life.


Why Hair Health Matters

Your hair reflects your lifestyle, environment, and overall well-being. Everyday stressors, heat styling, sun exposure, water minerals, and chemical processes can strip away the natural strength and shine that make hair beautiful.


Our stylists approach hair health proactively. Through professional treatments, restorative routines, and education, we help each client maintain balance between creativity and care. Because great color and design mean nothing if the foundation, your hair’s integrity, isn’t healthy.


Professional Hair Treatments at Craft Collective

We offer a curated selection of professional-grade treatments designed to strengthen, protect, and enhance every texture and type. Each treatment is personalized after a brief consultation to ensure your service supports both immediate goals and long-term results.


Keratin Smoothing Treatments

Keratin smoothing treatments are ideal for reducing frizz, improving manageability, and adding high-gloss shine. The process infuses the hair shaft with proteins and sealing agents, restoring smoothness while protecting against humidity.


Our formula choices depend on your hair’s texture and density, ensuring that you receive a soft, natural finish, not heavy or pin-straight results. Clients love keratin treatments because they can dramatically cut styling time and help maintain a polished look for months.


Deep Conditioning & Repair Masks

These treatments penetrate the cuticle to restore elasticity, hydration, and softness. Perfect for color-treated or heat-damaged hair, deep conditioning works on a molecular level to rebuild broken bonds and smooth roughness.


We often recommend this service as an add-on to color, extensions, or after lightening processes to protect long-term health.


Bond-Building Treatments (Olaplex, K18, etc.)

Bond-building treatments are the gold standard for repairing structural damage caused by chemical or mechanical stress. These formulas work inside the cortex of the hair, reconnecting broken disulfide bonds that give hair strength and flexibility.


Our stylists incorporate these treatments during color services or as stand-alone sessions for restoration. They’re ideal for clients transitioning to lighter shades, using hot tools regularly, or recovering from past overprocessing.


Scalp Treatments & Detox

A healthy scalp equals healthy hair. Our scalp treatments remove buildup, restore hydration, and stimulate circulation to encourage natural growth. Using gentle exfoliating products and nourishing oils, we rebalance the scalp’s microbiome and soothe dryness or irritation.


This service is a client favorite for those struggling with flaking, excess oil, or sensitivity. Many pair it with a haircut or blowout for a complete refresh.


Gloss & Shine Enhancements

A gloss treatment adds instant brilliance and smoothness to the hair surface. It’s perfect for dull or faded tones and can be performed between color sessions.


Our stylists use ammonia-free glosses that lock in reflection, enhance depth, and leave hair soft to the touch, an effortless upgrade for any service.


Custom Blends & Treatments for Extensions

Extensions need special care to stay hydrated without loosening bonds. We offer tailored moisture and protein treatments that nourish both your natural hair and extension wefts, ensuring seamless longevity.


The Science Behind Strong Hair

Every strand is made of keratin proteins held together by bonds. When those bonds break, hair becomes weak, brittle, and dull. Treatments like bond-builders, keratin, and deep hydration target these molecular weaknesses, helping to rebuild structure.


Our stylists are trained to identify early signs of damage and recommend targeted regimens. Preventing breakage before it happens is far more effective than repairing it later.


Sustainable Hair Health Practices

At Craft Collective, sustainability and health go hand in hand. We minimize harsh chemicals, choose cruelty-free product lines, and recycle foils, bottles, and packaging through our eco programs. Our goal is to enhance your beauty routine without harming the environment.


We also educate clients on home care practices that extend treatment results while reducing product waste.


Home Maintenance Tips for Healthy Hair

– Use professional-grade shampoo and conditioner formulated for your hair type.

– Limit heat styling and always apply a thermal protectant.

– Sleep on a silk pillowcase to prevent friction and frizz.

– Brush gently from ends upward to avoid breakage.

– Schedule treatments seasonally, especially after color changes or weather shifts.


Following these steps ensures your hair remains strong and radiant between salon visits.


Recognizing When It’s Time for a Treatment

You may need a professional treatment if your hair feels:

– Dry, coarse, or tangly after washing

– Dull, lacking shine even after styling

– Weak or stretchy when wet

– Uneven in color absorption or tone

– Prone to split ends or frizz despite trims


Our stylists can perform a strand analysis to identify underlying causes and select the right treatment pathway.
