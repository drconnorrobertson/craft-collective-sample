Where to Find Wigs, Hair Toppers, and Non-Medical Hair Solutions in Pittsburgh

Craft Collective Team · 2026-01-11


Many clients in Pittsburgh explore wigs and hair toppers as stylish, confidence-boosting options for thinning hair, medical hair loss, or simply wanting a new look without committing to color or extensions. Modern wigs and toppers look natural, are incredibly customizable, and offer a safe way to achieve fullness without adding tension to your own hair.


This guide breaks down what types of wigs and toppers are available, how to choose the right one, and what to look for when selecting a Pittsburgh salon that specializes in non-medical hair solutions.


Understanding the Difference Between Wigs and Hair Toppers

Wigs

A wig provides full coverage. It replaces the entire appearance of the hair and is ideal for clients with significant hair loss, medical hair loss, or those who want a dramatic transformation.


Toppers

A topper covers a specific area, usually the crown or part line. It blends with your natural hair and enhances fullness without covering the entire head.


Both options come in synthetic, heat-safe synthetic, and human hair versions, each offering different price points, styling options, and longevity.


Why Clients Choose Wigs or Toppers

Clients in Pittsburgh choose wigs and toppers for many reasons:

  • Hair thinning

  • Medical treatments

  • Autoimmune conditions

  • Postpartum shedding

  • Wanting fullness without extensions

  • Trying a new color without chemical processing

  • Convenient styling

  • Low-maintenance alternatives


These solutions provide flexibility and comfort while maintaining a natural look.


How to Choose the Right Wig or Topper

When selecting a wig or topper, consider:


Base construction

A thinner base looks more natural and blends better. Lace front options offer realistic hairlines.


Density

Too much density looks bulky. Too little density exposes the base. A careful balance is key.


Color match

For toppers, the blend must match your natural color or your desired shade perfectly. For wigs, color should complement your skin tone and features.


Length and texture

Choose a style that fits your natural pattern or desired look. Textured hair clients should select styles that support curl patterns and movement.


Lifestyle

Your daily routine matters. A wig or topper should fit your comfort level, maintenance schedule, and styling habits.


Why Fit Matters Most

A properly fitted wig or topper makes all the difference. Poor-fitting systems can feel heavy, shift easily, or look unnatural. A trained salon will customize placement, cut, and blending to ensure seamless movement and comfort.


Pittsburgh Climate and Wig or Topper Maintenance

Humidity can impact synthetic fibers, creating puffiness or tangling. Cold weather causes dryness. Hard water affects shine. A good Pittsburgh salon will recommend maintenance routines that:

  • Reduce frizz

  • Preserve shine

  • Maintain moisture

  • Protect the base

  • Extend longevity


Proper care keeps your investment looking beautiful longer.


Where to Find High Quality Wigs and Toppers in Pittsburgh

Not every retailer or salon specializes in natural-looking, realistic wigs and toppers. A true specialist offers:

  • Customized fitting

  • Cut and blending

  • Color matching

  • Education on maintenance

  • Scalp comfort assessment

  • Texture matching for curly clients

  • Options for medical and non-medical needs


Avoid purchasing from places that do not offer fitting or customization. Wigs and toppers should be tailored, not generic.
