The Best Haircuts for Your Face Shape in North Hills Pittsburgh

Craft Collective Team · 2026-06-27

Finding a haircut that flatters your face shape can completely transform your look and boost your confidence. In North Hills Pittsburgh, clients turn to Craft Collective Salon Group for personalized haircuts that enhance natural features, suit lifestyle needs, and work with hair texture. Understanding your face shape is the first step in choosing a style that works for you.


Identifying Your Face Shape

There are several common face shapes, each with styles that highlight strengths and balance proportions:

  • Oval: Balanced and versatile; most styles work well

  • Round: Styles with length and layers help elongate the face

  • Square: Soft, layered cuts or waves can soften strong jawlines

  • Heart: Chin-length layers and side-swept bangs complement a pointed chin

  • Long/Oblong: Styles with volume and width create balance and frame the face


Craft Collective stylists in North Hills Pittsburgh help clients determine their face shape and recommend cuts that enhance natural beauty.


Choosing the Right Haircut for Your Lifestyle

A flattering haircut should also fit your daily routine and maintenance preferences:

  • Busy professionals may prefer low-maintenance cuts that grow out gracefully

  • Active clients may benefit from layered or textured styles that stay stylish without daily styling

  • Those who love experimenting with color may want cuts that showcase dimension and movement


By considering lifestyle, stylists ensure clients leave with a haircut that works for both style and practicality.


Hair Texture and Haircuts

Hair texture plays a key role in choosing a flattering cut:

  • Straight Hair: Sleek layers and blunt cuts add movement

  • Wavy Hair: Layered cuts enhance natural waves and reduce bulk

  • Curly Hair: Strategic layering prevents triangle shapes and promotes bounce

  • Fine Hair: Textured cuts can create the appearance of fullness

  • Thick Hair: Thinning and layering techniques can reduce weight and bulk


Craft Collective stylists in North Hills Pittsburgh tailor cuts to complement each client’s unique texture.


Trendy Cuts Popular in North Hills Pittsburgh

Some of the most requested styles include:

  • Bob Cuts: Classic or asymmetrical for a chic, modern look

  • Layered Cuts: Add movement and volume to any length

  • Shag Haircuts: Perfect for textured, effortless styles

  • Pixie Cuts: Bold, low-maintenance, and flattering for many face shapes


Stylists combine trending cuts with personalized touches for each client.


Maintenance and Styling Tips

Even the best haircut requires proper care to maintain shape and health:

  • Schedule regular trims every 6–8 weeks

  • Use styling products suitable for your hair type and texture

  • Follow professional recommendations for heat styling and treatments

  • Protect hair from environmental factors like sun, wind, and humidity


These steps help North Hills Pittsburgh clients keep their cuts looking fresh between salon visits.


Choosing the right haircut is a combination of face shape, hair texture, lifestyle, and professional guidance. North Hills Pittsburgh clients trust Craft Collective Salon Group for expert advice, precision cutting, and personalized recommendations. With the right haircut, every client can enjoy hair that enhances their features, fits their life, and looks polished every day.
