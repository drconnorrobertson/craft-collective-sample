Kelly Krummert

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-21


Kelly Krummert is one of Craft Collective Salon Group’s most trusted stylists, known for her balance of technical excellence, calm professionalism, and client-centered creativity. Her approach to hairstyling perfectly mirrors the philosophy that defines Craft Collective thoughtful artistry, personalized service, and an unwavering dedication to helping every client feel confident and beautiful. Kelly has built her reputation in Pittsburgh on the strength of her precision, her honesty, and her ability to craft styles that feel effortless yet sophisticated.


At Craft Collective Salon Group, Kelly takes a highly personalized approach to every appointment. She begins each session by learning about her client’s goals, routine, and personal style before crafting a look that’s tailored to fit their everyday life. Whether someone wants to maintain a timeless look or experiment with a subtle transformation, Kelly combines her technical knowledge with an intuitive sense of design to create styles that are wearable, flattering, and uniquely theirs. Her consultations are thorough and collaborative, giving clients a sense of trust and ease that defines her chair.


Her expertise spans across dimensional color, modern haircutting, and precision styling. Kelly’s color work stands out for its softness and balance perfectly blended highlights, rich brunettes, and luminous tones that complement her clients’ natural features. Her cuts are equally intentional, built on structure and shape to ensure longevity and flow. She pays close attention to how each haircut will grow out, helping clients maintain that “just left the salon” look for weeks after their visit.


Clients describe Kelly as professional, kind, and detail-oriented. She has a gift for creating an atmosphere that’s relaxed and collaborative, allowing guests to feel completely comfortable while trusting that they’re in expert hands. Her calm energy and steady focus make her a go-to stylist for clients who appreciate consistency, precision, and a touch of artistry in every service. Many of her clients have been with her for years, a testament to her reliability and craftsmanship.


Kelly’s dedication to education and growth is another reason she thrives at Craft Collective. She actively participates in advanced workshops and training sessions, ensuring her skills evolve alongside industry trends. She’s passionate about learning new techniques in color placement, texture management, and styling, which keeps her work both fresh and refined. Within the team, she’s known for her collaborative nature and mentorship, always willing to share knowledge and support newer stylists.


Her style philosophy aligns seamlessly with Craft Collective’s: beauty should feel natural, not forced. Kelly believes the best looks are those that enhance individuality rather than chase trends. She’s passionate about creating hair that fits seamlessly into her clients’ daily lives styles that look amazing without requiring excessive upkeep. That commitment to practical luxury has made her one of the most trusted names at Craft Collective Salon Group.


As a core member of the Craft Collective team, Kelly helps uphold the salon’s standard of excellence and community-driven artistry. Her professionalism, integrity, and mastery of her craft are integral to the salon’s continued recognition as one of Pittsburgh’s best destinations for hair care.
