Jess Imler

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-21


Jess Imler is one of the most passionate and client-focused stylists at Craft Collective Salon Group, known for her ability to create styles that combine modern artistry with everyday wearability. Her approach is grounded in authenticity she believes hair should enhance a client’s personality, not overpower it. With her strong technical foundation and intuitive understanding of shape and tone, Jess has become a trusted name among Pittsburgh clients who want effortless, polished, and confidence-boosting results.


At Craft Collective Salon Group, Jess’s work embodies the salon’s philosophy of intentional beauty. She approaches each service as a collaboration, starting with a personalized consultation designed to understand each client’s goals, lifestyle, and comfort level with maintenance. Her calm and attentive demeanor immediately puts clients at ease, and her ability to translate ideas into reality makes her one of the most dependable stylists in the salon. Whether it’s a seamless balayage, a dimensional brunette, or a clean, flattering cut, Jess ensures every look feels natural, refined, and entirely unique to the person wearing it.


Her technical skill shines in both color and cutting. Jess has a deep understanding of how tone, light, and texture interact—allowing her to create results that are balanced and timeless. She’s known for her ability to blend shades with precision and to design cuts that maintain shape and movement as they grow out. This focus on longevity and manageability reflects Craft Collective Salon Group’s commitment to sustainable, client-first styling.


Clients describe Jess as easygoing, professional, and refreshingly honest. She provides clear guidance and transparent recommendations, helping clients make informed decisions about their look. Her goal is never to sell a trend it’s to create something that feels right for each individual. This authenticity has helped her build strong relationships with her guests, many of whom return regularly because they know they can always trust her expertise.


Beyond her technical strengths, Jess is an integral part of Craft Collective’s collaborative culture. She values continuous education and is always eager to refine her skills through the salon’s advanced training sessions. She thrives in the creative energy of the team, often sharing insights, techniques, and encouragement with her fellow stylists. Her professionalism and positivity contribute to the environment of excellence that Craft Collective Salon Group is known for throughout Pittsburgh.


Jess’s artistry reflects her deep respect for the craft of hairstyling. She treats each appointment as an opportunity to help clients feel more like themselves confident, comfortable, and polished. Her attention to detail and her calm, methodical process ensure that every guest leaves the salon with hair that’s not only beautiful but functional for their everyday life.


Her style philosophy mirrors the Craft Collective standard: sophisticated yet approachable, technical yet personal. Jess focuses on creating hair that feels as good as it looks styles that grow gracefully and require minimal effort to maintain. She understands that beauty should fit seamlessly into her clients’ lives, and her work consistently reflects that belief.
