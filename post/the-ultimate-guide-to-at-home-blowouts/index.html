The Ultimate Guide to At-Home Blowouts

Craft Collective Team · 2025-11-13

There’s nothing quite like the confidence of a fresh blowout—the smoothness, the shine, the volume, and the bounce that lasts for days. At Craft Collective Salon Group, stylists have mastered the blowout as both an art and a science. But what if you could recreate that professional finish at home between appointments? With the right products, tools, and techniques, you can. This detailed guide breaks down the process, step by step, so your at-home blowouts look as flawless as the ones crafted at our North Hills and Wexford locations.


Understanding the Craft Collective Blowout Philosophy

A professional blowout isn’t just about drying hair—it’s about shaping it. Every stroke of the brush, every degree of heat, and every sectioning pattern has purpose. Craft Collective stylists believe a proper blowout should achieve three goals:


  1. Longevity: Lasts up to five days with minimal touch-ups.

  2. Health: Protects the cuticle from heat and friction.

  3. Polish: Looks effortless but refined, reflecting salon-quality craftsmanship.


By following these principles, you’ll transform your home styling routine into a professional-level ritual.


Preparation: Set Yourself Up for Success

A great blowout starts before you even turn on the dryer. Preparation determines how smooth, shiny, and long-lasting your result will be.


Step 1: Choose the Right Shampoo and Conditioner

Begin with a lightweight, smoothing formula that removes buildup without stripping moisture. Craft Collective stylists recommend:


  • Aveda Smooth Infusion Anti-Frizz Shampoo and Conditioner for all hair types.

  • R+Co Bleu Primary Color Shampoo for colour-treated hair (see Article 2 for glossing care).

  • Wella ColorMotion+ Shampoo for shine and resilience.


Always rinse thoroughly with cool water to seal the cuticle.


Step 2: Detangle with Care

Use a wide-tooth comb or detangling brush while hair is damp, not soaking wet. Start at the ends and work upward to prevent breakage. Stylists suggest applying leave-in conditioner at this stage for added slip and protection.


Step 3: Apply Heat Protectant

This step is non-negotiable. A good heat protectant forms a micro-shield that prevents moisture loss and thermal damage. Top choices at Craft Collective include:


  • R+Co Bleu Heat Defense Serum for medium to coarse textures.

  • Aveda Botanical Repair Leave-In for fine or fragile hair.


Apply evenly, comb through, and section your hair before drying.


The Tools That Make a Difference

Not all dryers and brushes are created equal. Investing in quality tools pays off through smoother, faster blowouts and healthier hair.


  • Hair Dryer: Use one with ionic technology and multiple heat/speed settings. The Dyson Supersonic and GHD Helios are salon favourites.

  • Brush: Choose a round ceramic brush for volume and shine. Boar-bristle brushes smooth frizz, while mixed bristles provide tension control.

  • Clips: Sectioning clips keep your work clean and efficient.

  • Nozzle Attachment: Concentrates airflow for precision and polish.

  • Finishing Spray: Lightweight, flexible-hold formulas prevent flyaways.


Craft Collective stylists emphasise tool maintenance—clean brush barrels and filters regularly to ensure even airflow and hygiene.


Sectioning: The Secret to Professional Control

Divide hair into four to six sections depending on thickness. Clip everything except the bottom layer. Work from the nape upward, drying each section fully before moving on. Precision sectioning prevents overheating and ensures every strand receives even tension.

For clients with layers (see Article 12), stylists recommend splitting crown and face-framing areas separately to maintain lift and shape.


Technique: The Craft Collective Method


Step 1: Rough Dry

Before styling, rough-dry the hair about 70–80% with fingers or a paddle brush. This removes excess moisture and speeds up the process. Avoid scrunching or twisting—smooth airflow from roots to ends to maintain cuticle alignment.


Step 2: Brush and Roll

Take a two-inch section. Position the round brush beneath the strand, pull taut, and direct the nozzle downward along the hair shaft. Always follow the brush with the airflow—never let the heat linger in one place.


Step 3: Cool to Set

After each pass, press the dryer’s cool shot button to lock in shape and shine. This mimics the sealing stage stylists use at the salon, giving your blowout professional longevity.


Step 4: Root Lift and Volume

To create lift, focus on the crown area. Use a smaller round brush, roll sections upward, and hold for a few seconds before releasing. Spritz a volumizing spray at the roots—R+Co Bleu Thickening Spray is a Craft Collective go-to.


Step 5: Finishing Touches

Once dry, let hair cool for five minutes before styling further. Add a few drops of Aveda Nutriplenish Oil to the ends for shine. Mist lightly with Wella EIMI Stay Firm Spray for flexible hold.


Blowout Styles for Every Hair Type


Straight Hair

Focus on smoothing and shine. Keep airflow angled downward and brush tension firm. Finish with light oil for a glassy surface.


Wavy Hair

Enhance natural bends. Alternate brush direction for body and motion. Scrunch mid-lengths slightly while cooling for soft texture.


Curly Hair

Use a diffuser to preserve definition, or stretch curls with a large round brush for a voluminous blowout. Apply curl cream beforehand for moisture memory (see Article 10).


Coarse or Textured Hair

Work in smaller sections. Apply a heat protectant plus smoothing serum. Consider a low-heat pass with a flat iron afterward for polished results.


Extending the Life of Your Blowout

Your effort deserves longevity. Follow these stylist-approved tips to extend results for up to five days.


Day 1

Avoid moisture exposure. Let hair cool fully before touching. Sleep with hair wrapped loosely in a silk scarf or bonnet.


Day 2–3

Refresh roots using a lightweight dry shampoo. Flip hair upside down and blast cool air to revive volume.


Day 4–5

Re-curl ends or touch up with a round brush and dryer on low heat. Mist with gloss spray for renewed shine.


If you work out, use a sweatband to absorb moisture near the hairline and allow hair to cool before re-styling.


Common Blowout Mistakes

  1. Skipping Heat Protectant: Leads to dryness and breakage.

  2. Using Too Much Product: Causes buildup and dullness.

  3. Over-Drying: Removes natural moisture, creating frizz.

  4. Wrong Brush Size: Too small over-curls, too large removes lift.

  5. Ignoring Cool Shot: Shortens blowout lifespan significantly.


Craft Collective stylists recommend mastering technique slowly rather than rushing—patience yields polish.


Professional Add-Ons for Next-Level Results

  • Gloss Refresh (see Article 2): Enhances shine and longevity.

  • Keratin Express (see Article 7): Reduces frizz for faster at-home blowouts.

  • Moisture Infusion Treatment (see Article 13): Prepares hair for humidity changes.


Combining professional maintenance with proper at-home care delivers consistently smooth, camera-ready results.


Eco-Friendly Blowouts

Sustainability is core to every Craft Collective service—even blowouts. The salon minimizes its carbon footprint by using energy-efficient dryers, biodegradable towels, and refillable styling products. Clients can extend this approach at home:


  • Air-dry partially before heat styling.

  • Unplug dryers after use to reduce standby energy.

  • Choose vegan and refillable haircare formulas.

  • Recycle empty aerosol cans and product packaging properly.


Luxury and responsibility can—and should—coexist.

Final Reflection

A perfect blowout isn’t about perfection—it’s about confidence. When your hair moves effortlessly and feels healthy, you carry that energy into everything you do. By combining Craft Collective’s techniques with mindful product use, you can recreate professional results from the comfort of home.
