Your Holiday Hair Guide 2025: Celebrate the Season in Style | Craft Collective Salon Group

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-26

The holidays are here—a season of celebration, connection, and endless events. Whether it’s a winter gala in downtown Pittsburgh or a cozy family gathering in Wexford, your hair should feel as joyful as the season itself. At Craft Collective Salon Group, we make holiday beauty effortless through clean, luxurious styling that shines.


1. Classic Blowouts That Last

A smooth, long-lasting blowout is the foundation of any great holiday look. Our stylists use R+Co Bleu Optical Illusion Oil and Wella EIMI Perfect Me to create soft volume and humidity resistance that holds for days.


For inspiration, explore The Ultimate Blowout and Styling Experience in Wexford.


2. Glossing Before the Gathering

A Shinefinity gloss or Aveda Botanical Repair glaze is the fastest way to revive color before the festivities. This treatment enhances shine, smooths frizz, and deepens tone—perfect for those last-minute touch-ups.


Learn how glossing enhances tone in Glossing and Shine Revival Guide for Wexford Clients.


3. Elegant, Effortless Updos

For holiday dinners and New Year celebrations, polished low buns, modern French twists, and soft waves are trending in 2025. Our stylists create shape and texture that photographs beautifully while feeling light and comfortable.


To add sparkle, we finish each look with a mist of R+Co Bleu Reflective Shine Spray—vegan, lightweight, and sustainably packaged.


4. Healthy Hair Through the Holidays

Between travel, cold air, and heat styling, it’s easy to lose hydration during the season. We recommend alternating between Aveda Nutriplenish Leave-In and Wella FusionPlex Mask for soft, strong strands throughout December.


Read about long-term hair health in Winter Hair Revival: How to Keep Your Hair Healthy and Glossy All Season.


5. Give the Gift of Beauty

Looking for a meaningful, easy holiday gift? Craft Collective Salon Group gift cards are available online and in-salon—redeemable for any cut, color, or treatment. It’s the perfect way to give luxury and sustainability in one thoughtful package.


Pair a gift card with a refillable R+Co Bleu product or Aveda travel set for a complete present that supports eco-conscious beauty.


Celebrate the Season at Craft Collective

From party blowouts to New Year’s transformations, we’re here to make your holiday season shine.
