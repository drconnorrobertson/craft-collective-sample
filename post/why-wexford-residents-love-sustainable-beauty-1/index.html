Why Wexford Residents Love Sustainable Beauty | Craft Collective Salon Group

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-20


In Wexford, beauty isn’t just about style — it’s about values. As sustainability becomes part of daily life across Pittsburgh’s North Hills, more clients are choosing eco-friendly salons that deliver both luxury and responsibility.


At Craft Collective Salon Group, we’re proud to lead that change. Our commitment to sustainability runs deeper than products — it’s a full-circle philosophy built into every service, every tool, and every stylist’s approach.


Here’s why Wexford residents are embracing sustainable beauty and how our salon is redefining what it means to look good and do good.


1. Conscious Clients, Conscious Choices

Wexford has become a hub for educated, mindful consumers who care about quality and impact. Clients are reading ingredient lists, asking about sourcing, and supporting brands that prioritize wellness and the environment.


Our stylists share that same mindset — which is why we’ve partnered with the cleanest, most ethical brands in the industry.


Explore how our philosophy began on the Sustainable Beauty Philosophy page.


2. The Power of Clean Ingredients

Our shelves feature only cruelty-free, vegan, and planet-friendly brands — including:

  • R+Co: High-performance, sulfate-free formulas with recyclable packaging.

  • R+Co Bleu: Luxury eco-conscious line using post-consumer recycled materials.

  • Wella Professionals: Glossing and color care systems with reduced ammonia and optimized water use.

  • Aveda: 100% vegan, plant-powered, and carbon-neutral.


Each product we choose supports professional performance and sustainability.

For insight on integrating these products into your daily care, read The Wexford Guide to Dimensional Blonde.


3. Low-Waste Salon Operations

Every detail at Craft Collective is designed with environmental mindfulness in mind:

  • Biodegradable towels and gloves

  • LED lighting and energy-efficient dryers

  • Refillable product systems that cut plastic waste by 70%

  • Responsible water filtration for color and rinse stations


This means you can enjoy a luxury salon experience while minimizing your carbon footprint.

Learn more about our operational practices in Why North Hills Residents Are Choosing Eco-Friendly Haircare.


4. Sustainable Color Services

Eco-beauty doesn’t stop at shampoo. Our stylists use Aveda and Wella professional color systems formulated for low-impact processing, reduced waste, and maximum shine.


Every lightening and glossing service is paired with bond-building treatments to maintain hair health.For tone care, revisit The Ultimate Guide to Hair Glossing in Pittsburgh’s North Hills.


5. Refillable Beauty Culture

We encourage clients to refill their R+Co and Aveda products in-salon — saving money and reducing waste. Over a year, one refill system can prevent up to 30 plastic bottles from entering circulation.


This small change, multiplied across hundreds of Wexford guests, has a measurable impact.

See how this community mindset is spreading across McCandless in The McCandless Guide to Low-Maintenance Hair Color.


6. The Intersection of Luxury and Responsibility

At Craft Collective, sustainability doesn’t mean sacrificing style or performance. We deliver editorial-quality hair artistry with eco-luxury systems that honor both beauty and the environment.


That’s why Wexford clients describe our salon as “elevated yet grounded” — a space where artistry, comfort, and conscience meet.


7. How You Can Join the Movement

Sustainability starts with awareness. You can help by:

  • Choosing cruelty-free, vegan products

  • Refilling your salon bottles

  • Supporting local salons that value ethical practices

  • Scheduling regular treatments to maintain hair health sustainably


Every choice, no matter how small, contributes to a cleaner future for our community.


8. Book a Sustainable Appointment in Wexford

If you’re ready to experience beauty with purpose, schedule your visit at Craft Collective Salon Group Wexford today.


From glossing to precision cuts, every detail of your appointment supports both your personal style and environmental well being.


Book online at craftcollectivesalongroup.com — the North Hills’ trusted destination for sustainable luxury.
