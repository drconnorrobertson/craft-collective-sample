Hair Treatments & Health in North Hills: Restoring Strength, Shine, and Balance to Every Strand

Craft Collective Team · 2025-12-04


Healthy hair is the foundation of every beautiful color and cut. At Craft Collective Salon Group in North Hills, we believe that hair care should be as customized as your color formula. Our Hair Treatments & Health services are designed to rebuild, rehydrate, and rebalance every strand, whether your hair is color-treated, over-processed, or simply in need of renewal.


From bond-building systems to moisture masks and keratin smoothing treatments, our stylists craft targeted repair plans that strengthen hair from the inside out, helping every guest maintain vibrant, glossy, and resilient hair year-round.


Why Hair Health Matters

Even the best color won’t shine without a healthy foundation. Everyday stressors, heat, brushing, hard water, color services, and environmental exposure can weaken keratin bonds and strip away the cuticle’s natural lipids.


The result: dullness, frizz, and brittleness. Our treatment philosophy focuses on restoring structure, moisture, and balance, ensuring your hair not only looks better but feels stronger over time.


Our Treatment Philosophy

Each treatment is completely tailored through consultation and analysis. We pair diagnostic insight with professional-grade repair systems to address:


– Chemical and environmental damage

– Color fade or porosity issues

– Weak or brittle strands

– Excess dryness and lack of elasticity

– Dullness or loss of shine


Every session begins with a strand and scalp assessment, followed by the right combination of strengthening, hydrating, or smoothing treatments, all designed to complement your Custom Hair Color Formulation and ongoing maintenance plan.


Our Signature Treatment Options

1. Bond-Building Treatment

Our professional-grade bond builders repair the inner structure of the hair, reversing damage caused by lightening, heat styling, or environmental stress. They’re especially recommended after blonde services (see blonde maintenance guide) or root smudge applications (learn more).


Benefits:


– Reconnects broken disulfide bonds

– Reduces breakage and split ends

– Adds elasticity and smoothness

– Prepares hair for future color services


2. Deep Hydration Mask

For dry, coarse, or curly textures, hydration masks replenish essential lipids and moisture. These are ideal following Gloss & Shine Enhancements (details here) or as a seasonal refresher for dullness and frizz.


Benefits:


– Restores softness and manageability

– Improves color longevity

– Reduces static and frizz

– Leaves hair touchably smooth


3. Keratin Smoothing Treatment

Keratin services infuse the hair with proteins and amino acids to reduce frizz, smooth texture, and enhance shine. It’s not a straightening service; it’s a restorative smoothing system that delivers sleek, humidity-resistant results.


Benefits:


– Lasts up to 3 months

– Greatly reduces drying time

– Protects color from humidity and oxidation

– Safe for color-treated and lightened hair


4. Scalp Health & Detox Treatment

Healthy growth begins at the scalp. Our exfoliating and balancing scalp treatments remove buildup, boost circulation, and create the optimal environment for new growth.


Benefits:


– Purifies follicles and removes residue

– Calms irritation and dryness

– Balances oil production

– Enhances overall hair vitality


How Often Should You Schedule Treatments?

– Bond-Building: Every 4 to 6 weeks for color clients

– Deep Hydration: Weekly at home, in-salon monthly

– Keratin Smoothing: Every 3 to 4 months

– Scalp Health: Every 6 to 8 weeks


Your stylist will recommend a rotation that complements your color cycle and home-care habits, ensuring long-term strength and softness.


Home Maintenance for Healthy Hair

Between professional treatments, your home routine plays a vital role in keeping your hair healthy. Use products recommended by our stylists through the Product Education & Hair Care series to extend the life of your salon results.


– Use sulfate-free shampoos and conditioners

– Apply a weekly deep-conditioning mask

– Protect hair before heat styling

– Sleep on a silk pillowcase to prevent friction

– Schedule regular trims every 8 to 10 weeks


Sustainable Hair Health

All treatments are performed using clean, cruelty-free, and eco-responsible formulas as part of our Sustainable & Eco-Friendly Salon initiative. We recycle all product packaging, foils, and gloves through Green Circle Salons to reduce waste at every stage.
