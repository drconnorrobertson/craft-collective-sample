Kim Hughes

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-21


Kim Hughes is one of the most trusted and technically accomplished stylists at Craft Collective Salon Group, admired for her precision, creativity, and genuine care for every client she works with. She represents the best of what the salon stands for artistry rooted in skill, beauty driven by connection, and a salon experience defined by calm professionalism and warmth. With years of experience and a natural eye for design, Kim has built a loyal Pittsburgh clientele who value her consistency, her craftsmanship, and her ability to create timeless, easy-to-maintain styles that make everyday life feel a little more beautiful.


At Craft Collective Salon Group, Kim’s approach is deliberate, collaborative, and highly personalized. She begins each appointment with a thoughtful consultation, ensuring that every service reflects the client’s personality, lifestyle, and long-term goals. Her ability to translate a vision into reality whether through dimensional color, precise cutting, or subtle styling adjustments makes her one of the salon’s most dependable and creative stylists. She focuses not just on trends, but on creating results that feel fresh, natural, and completely wearable.


Kim’s technical strength lies in her mastery of color placement and her understanding of shape and proportion. She’s especially known for her seamless blonding, balanced highlights, and dimensional brunettes that grow out effortlessly. Her cuts are equally intentional, blending structure and softness for a result that moves naturally and maintains its form over time. She pays careful attention to how the hair will behave after the appointment an essential part of her process that reflects Craft Collective Salon Group’s dedication to sustainable, long-lasting beauty.


Clients often describe Kim as calm, kind, and quietly confident. She creates an atmosphere that feels welcoming and grounded, giving guests the space to relax and enjoy the experience. Her attention to detail and gentle communication style help clients feel understood and reassured throughout the service. Many say that time in Kim’s chair feels restorative a blend of artistry and ease that defines the Craft Collective experience.


Kim’s commitment to excellence extends beyond her work behind the chair. She actively participates in the salon’s advanced education and training sessions, always looking for ways to evolve her craft. She’s passionate about learning new techniques in color theory, cutting design, and styling, and she brings that fresh perspective to every appointment. Her curiosity and discipline help keep Craft Collective at the forefront of Pittsburgh’s salon community.


Within the team, Kim is respected as both a mentor and collaborator. She exemplifies the salon’s culture of mutual growth, professionalism, and support. Her consistency and reliability set a high bar, and her willingness to share her knowledge contributes to the creative synergy that keeps Craft Collective thriving.


Kim’s design philosophy centers on creating beauty that feels natural and enduring. She believes that a great haircut or color should make life easier not more complicated. Her goal is for clients to leave feeling effortlessly confident, knowing their hair will look great with minimal effort day after day. That combination of artistry and practicality is what keeps her clients coming back again and again.
