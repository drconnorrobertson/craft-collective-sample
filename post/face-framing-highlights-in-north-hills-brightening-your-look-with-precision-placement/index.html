Face-Framing Highlights in North Hills: Brightening Your Look with Precision Placement

Craft Collective Team · 2025-12-01



Sometimes, the smallest color change makes the biggest impact. At Craft Collective Salon Group in North Hills, face-framing highlights are one of our signature services, adding brightness, definition, and a youthful glow without needing a full color transformation.


This subtle technique focuses light around your face, enhancing your eyes, skin tone, and natural bone structure. Whether you prefer sun-kissed ribbons or soft contouring, face-framing highlights are an instant refresh that elevates your entire look.


What Are Face-Framing Highlights?

Face-framing highlights, also called “money piece” highlights, are strategic color placements applied near the front hairline and around your part. They’re designed to:


– Brighten your face

– Add visual depth and contrast

– Accentuate your haircut and layering

– Create a polished, modern finish


They can be bold and high-contrast or subtle and blended, depending on your base color and tone preference.


Why Clients Love This Look

Face-framing highlights are perfect for clients who want noticeable change without the commitment of full color. The benefits include:


– Quick refresh: Adds life to your color in one short session.

– Low maintenance: Grows out gracefully between appointments.

– Customizable tone: Works with blondes, brunettes, and redheads.

– Seasonal versatility: Adds brightness in summer or depth in fall.


They also complement almost every haircut, from blunt bobs to long layers, because they naturally draw the eye upward.


How We Create the Perfect Frame

At Craft Collective, every highlight placement is customized using our custom color formulation process.


  1. Consultation: We determine undertone, face shape, and existing color.

  2. Placement Design: Light is strategically painted to frame your cheekbones and jawline.

  3. Balayage or Foil Technique: Depending on your desired contrast, we use freehand painting for softness or foils for lift.

  4. Glossing Finish: We refine tone with a gloss and shine enhancement for natural glow and reflectivity.

  5. Bond Care: Every lightening session includes bond-repair treatment to protect strength and shine.


The result? Seamless, radiant brightness that looks intentional but never overdone.


Best Color Pairings

– Brunettes: Caramel or honey ribbons for warmth and movement.

– Blondes: Platinum, champagne, or golden tones for radiance.

– Redheads: Copper-gold or strawberry accents for extra vibrance.


We often combine this technique with root smudge blending [link to root-smudge-shadow-root-north-hills] for the perfect grow-out and dimension.


Seasonal Inspiration

– Spring: Soft beige highlights to complement renewal and light textures.

– Summer: Golden pieces that glow in sunlight and add beachy contrast.

– Fall: Butterscotch or amber ribbons for cozy warmth.

– Winter: Icy or neutral tones for crisp, polished shine.


For a full seasonal approach, visit Hair Color for Every Season in North Hills.


Maintenance Tips

Face-framing highlights are easy to maintain with proper care:


– Use sulfate-free shampoo and conditioner.

– Schedule a gloss refresh every 6 to 8 weeks.

– Limit hot-tool temperatures to preserve tone.

– Protect from UV rays and mineral buildup.

– Add a deep-conditioning treatment monthly for hydration.


Your stylist will provide a tailored at-home care plan after your service.


Sustainable Highlighting

Every highlight service follows our Sustainable & Eco-Friendly Salon philosophy. We use cruelty-free, low-tox color lines and recycle all foils and tubes through Green Circle Salons.


Your brightness doesn’t come at the planet’s expense.
