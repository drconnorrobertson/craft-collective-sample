Custom Hair Color Formulation in North Hills: Precision Blending for Perfect Results

Craft Collective Team · 2025-12-04


No two heads of hair are alike, so no two formulas should be either. At Craft Collective Salon Group in North Hills, our Custom Hair Color Formulation process ensures that every shade, tone, and highlight is mixed uniquely for you.


From dimensional brunettes to luminous blondes and radiant reds, every color we apply is designed to enhance your natural complexion, texture, and lifestyle, resulting in a finish that feels luxurious, long-lasting, and authentically yours.


What Is Custom Hair Color Formulation?

Custom formulation means your stylist handcrafts a color blend that matches your specific hair type, pigment level, and tone preferences. It’s a process rooted in artistry and chemistry, balancing undertones, depth, and reflectivity to achieve a seamless, salon-exclusive result.


Rather than choosing a pre-mixed color from a box, your stylist analyzes the base pigments in your hair, then mixes professional-grade shades to achieve a one-of-a-kind result that flatters your features and enhances your natural beauty.


Why Custom Color Matters

Even the most beautiful highlight or balayage can lose its impact if the underlying tone isn’t balanced correctly. Custom formulation ensures:


– A natural, multidimensional finish

– Perfect tone balance (no brassiness or ashiness)

– Color longevity and shine retention

– Seamless blending between root, mid, and end tones

– Skin-tone harmony for a flattering overall look


Every formula at Craft Collective is stored in your personal color record, allowing our stylists to recreate or adjust your tone perfectly at each visit.


The Formulation Process

1. In-Depth Consultation

We begin with a detailed conversation about your goals, color history, and maintenance preferences.


2. Tone & Undertone Analysis

Your stylist examines your natural level, undertones, and prior pigments to determine the ideal hue and saturation.


3. Custom Mixing

Using advanced professional color systems, we hand-mix precise ratios of base, pigment, and gloss to achieve the perfect balance.


4. Application & Technique Pairing

The formula is paired with the right technique, whether it’s balayage, foiling, root smudge, or gloss enhancement, for optimal dimension.


5. Finish & Seal

The service concludes with a Gloss & Shine Enhancement (read more here) and a Hair Treatments & Health ritual (see details) for lasting softness and radiance.


Perfect for Every Color Type

Blondes benefit from custom formulations that neutralize yellow while maintaining reflective brightness. Brunettes achieve the perfect balance between richness and light movement. Redheads maintain saturation and vibrancy without fading.


Your stylist adjusts pigment ratios, developer strengths, and gloss tone to create a color that looks natural and polished, even as it grows out.


How Custom Formulation Enhances Other Services

Every signature service at Craft Collective begins with custom formulation as the foundation:


– Root Smudge & Shadow Root: seamless tone transitions

– Balayage vs Highlights: tailored contrast

– Gloss & Shine Enhancements: tone perfection and reflection

– Golden & Caramel Highlights: warmth without brass

– Blonde Maintenance: ongoing tone preservation


This precision ensures every result feels intentional, cohesive, and true to your desired look.


Sustainable Formulation Practices

As part of our Sustainable & Eco-Friendly Salon initiative, Craft Collective uses professional-grade, cruelty-free color systems that minimize waste and maximize performance.


All foils, gloves, and color packaging are recycled through Green Circle Salons, ensuring our artistry remains environmentally responsible.


Maintaining Your Custom Color at Home

To preserve the perfect tone between visits:


– Wash with professional, color-safe products from Product Education & Hair Care.

– Avoid daily shampooing; 2 to 3 times per week is ideal.

– Protect your hair with heat protectant before styling.

– Use a silk pillowcase to reduce color fade and friction.

– Schedule a gloss refresh every 8 to 10 weeks to renew shine and vibrance.


Your stylist will design a home-care plan personalized to your color type and maintenance preferences.
