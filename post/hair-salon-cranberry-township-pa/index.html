Hair Salon in Cranberry Township, PA | Craft Collective Salon Group

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-23

Located just south of Cranberry Commons and an easy drive from Route 19, Craft Collective Salon Group is where Cranberry Township residents go for modern, sustainable, and luxurious hair design. Our salon serves the greater North Hills Pittsburgh region, offering elevated color, precision cuts, extensions, and bridal styling — all grounded in artistry and sustainability.


Cranberry has quickly become one of Western Pennsylvania’s most dynamic communities. Its residents value style, sophistication, and service — the same values that define every appointment at Craft Collective Salon Group. From professionals and students to families and creatives, we’ve become the trusted salon for guests who expect the very best.


Our stylists combine advanced technical education with personalized consultation, creating results that look beautiful not just in the salon, but every day after.


A Modern Salon for Cranberry Township Clients

Our guests from Cranberry Township appreciate that every detail of our salon experience is intentional — from the design of our space to the formulas in our products. We use only high-performance, eco-conscious lines including R+Co, R+Co Bleu, Wella Professionals, and Aveda Color.


Each brand aligns with our commitment to clean beauty and innovation. Together, they allow us to create hair that’s glossy, soft, and healthy — with formulations that respect both your scalp and the environment.


Our stylists tailor every service to your goals, hair type, and maintenance needs, ensuring you leave with confidence and long-lasting results.


Balayage and Color Expertise

For Cranberry Township clients seeking dimensional color, our balayage and highlighting services offer luminous, customized results. Our colorists are trained in hand-painting techniques that create natural, lived-in brightness without harsh lines or frequent touch-ups.


We use bond-reinforcing systems from Wella Professionals to protect the hair during lightening, while Aveda Color provides plant-powered vibrancy for guests who prefer a more natural approach.


Whether you’re interested in warm honey blondes, rich brunettes, or modern copper shades, our stylists help you find a tone that complements your skin tone and personality.

Color correction and glossing services are also available to refresh or rebalance tone between visits — ideal for maintaining shine and depth through Cranberry’s changing seasons.


Sustainable Luxury for Cranberry

Craft Collective Salon Group believes beauty and responsibility go hand in hand. For our Cranberry clients, that means a salon experience designed around sustainability.

R+Co Bleu leads our sustainable lineup, featuring 100% recycled packaging and formulations that combine luxury with clean chemistry. R+Co products are all vegan, cruelty-free, and free of parabens, sulfates, and mineral oils.


In addition to responsible product sourcing, our salon operates with energy-efficient lighting, waste reduction systems, and eco-friendly towels and gloves.


We’ve found that Cranberry clients appreciate this blend of performance and purpose — beauty that feels as good ethically as it looks aesthetically.


Precision Cuts and Styling

Our stylists approach every haircut with a blend of artistry and geometry. Each design starts with a consultation to understand your goals, daily routine, and preferred level of maintenance. From sleek long layers to textured bobs, we customize every detail for proportion and flow.


We use styling products from R+Co that allow flexibility and hold without residue — ideal for humid Pittsburgh summers and crisp winter air alike.


Guests often book a blowout or style refresh before dinners at Cranberry Commons, events at UPMC Lemieux Sports Complex, or date nights downtown. Whether casual or polished, every style is crafted to last and move naturally.


Bridal and Event Hair in Cranberry Township

Cranberry Township brides trust Craft Collective Salon Group for stunning, timeless wedding looks. Our bridal stylists work one-on-one with each bride to design a look that aligns with their dress, vision, and venue — from soft, romantic waves to intricate updos.


We provide both in-salon and on-site bridal services throughout Cranberry and North Hills Pittsburgh. Our pre-event consultations ensure your style complements every detail of your big day.


For special events, photo sessions, and galas, our styling team creates polished, camera-ready looks that enhance confidence without feeling overdone.
