Modern Men’s Haircuts in North Hills: Precision, Style, and Simplicity | Craft Collective Salon Group

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-26

At Craft Collective Salon Group, we believe men’s grooming should feel as effortless as it looks. Our stylists in Wexford, Allison Park, and Ross Township specialize in precision cutting and clean styling that transitions seamlessly from office to weekend.


The Modern Men’s Aesthetic

2025’s men’s haircut trends focus on soft structure and versatility. Popular North Hills requests include:

  • Textured Crew Cuts: low-maintenance with controlled volume.

  • Tailored Fades: classic, professional, and easy to style daily.

  • Grown-Out Layers: natural shape with subtle movement.


Each style is personalized based on growth pattern, density, and face shape—creating hair that always looks good, even on non-styling days.


Sustainable Grooming Products

We use high-performance, eco-conscious styling essentials from:

  • R+Co Bleu Continental Glossing Wax – smooth hold and subtle shine.

  • Aveda Men Pure-Formance Grooming Clay – strong matte texture with zero residue.

  • Wella EIMI Mistify Me Light – humidity protection with weightless control.


See how sustainability fits into men’s grooming in The North Hills Guide to Sustainable Hair Trends in 2025.


Designed for Busy Professionals

We understand time matters. That’s why our men’s services include express consultations, efficient precision cuts, and styling education to keep your look sharp between visits.

Our goal: a haircut that grows out gracefully and matches your work-life balance.

Explore more professional styles in Professional Haircuts for Wexford Executives.


Sustainable Luxury, Simplified

We believe even everyday grooming can support a cleaner planet. From biodegradable towels to refillable products, every men’s service at Craft Collective reflects eco-luxury in action.
