Bridal Hair in North Hills: Personalized, Picture-Perfect Styles for Your Wedding Day

Craft Collective Team · 2025-12-08


Your wedding day is one of the most memorable moments of your life, and your hair should reflect that magic. At Craft Collective Salon Group in North Hills, we specialize in bridal hairstyling that feels timeless, elegant, and uniquely yours.


Every bride deserves a look that complements her dress, enhances her features, and lasts beautifully from first photos to the final dance.


The Craft Collective Bridal Experience

Our bridal experience is designed for peace of mind and perfection. From the initial consultation to the final pin, we collaborate with you every step of the way to ensure your vision comes to life flawlessly.


Our process includes:

– In-depth bridal consultation and trial session

– Hair health prep and customized treatments

– Day-of timeline coordination for seamless flow

– Optional on-site or in-salon bridal party services

– Collaboration with makeup artists and photographers for cohesive styling


We approach every wedding with the same philosophy that defines all Craft Collective services, artistry, precision, and an unwavering attention to detail.


Bridal Hair Styles for Every Bride

1. Classic Elegance

Low buns, sleek chignons, and timeless waves. Perfect for brides seeking sophisticated simplicity that photographs beautifully in any setting.


Pair it with: Gloss & Shine Enhancements for luminous reflection under natural light.


2. Romantic Boho

Soft braids, flowing curls, and textured updos that feel airy, modern, and effortlessly feminine.


Best for: Outdoor ceremonies or garden venues.


Styling support: Use our Hair Treatments & Health menu pre-wedding for hydration and elasticity.


3. Glamorous Hollywood Waves

Polished, sculpted curls with deep side parts and mirror-like shine. A favorite for formal and black-tie weddings.


Ideal for: Long hair or Extensions (coming soon in your next pillar).


4. Half-Up, Half-Down Styles

A versatile option that combines structure and movement. Adds balance for brides who want softness but need lasting hold.


Perfect with: Dimensional Brunette Techniques for visual depth.


5. Textured Ponytails & Modern Updos

Contemporary and photo-ready, these styles pair perfectly with minimalist gowns and statement accessories.


Pre-style service: Blowouts & Styling the day before for maximum polish.


Bridal Party & Group Styling

We welcome bridal parties of all sizes and provide fully coordinated services for bridesmaids, mothers, and guests. Packages can include:

– Coordinated looks tailored to each member’s dress and features

– In-salon private suite reservations for the full morning experience

– Champagne or refreshment service

– Timeline management to ensure a smooth, relaxed day


We also offer on-site bridal styling throughout the North Hills and greater Pittsburgh area, bringing the Craft Collective experience directly to your venue.


Preparing for Your Bridal Trial

Your trial is where the magic begins. We recommend scheduling your trial 1 to 2 months before the wedding, bringing inspiration photos, your veil or accessories, and wearing a top that mimics your dress neckline.


During the trial, your stylist will:

– Test different styles for your hair type and desired look

– Adjust for comfort, volume, and longevity

– Take photos and notes to perfectly recreate the look on your wedding day


Sustainable Bridal Beauty

Every bridal service aligns with our Sustainable & Eco-Friendly Salon initiative. We use clean, cruelty-free, and biodegradable styling products, ensuring your wedding beauty routine supports the planet as well as your look.
