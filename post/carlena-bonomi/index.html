Carlena Bonomi

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-21


Carlena Bonomi brings a thoughtful, refined artistry to Craft Collective Salon Group, where her passion for creating effortless, wearable beauty has made her one of Pittsburgh’s most sought-after stylists. Her approach combines classic technique with a modern eye for detail, resulting in styles that are timeless yet fresh. Clients come to Carlena not just for her technical precision, but for the sense of calm confidence she brings to every appointment. Her chair is a place where collaboration, creativity, and care come together seamlessly.


At Craft Collective Salon Group, Carlena represents everything the salon stands for connection, craftsmanship, and authenticity. She believes that great hair starts with great communication, taking the time to understand what each client envisions before crafting a look that aligns with their lifestyle, face shape, and personal style. Whether she’s designing soft, layered cuts, customizing color to add depth and dimension, or refining texture to achieve a polished finish, Carlena’s focus is always on creating results that enhance her clients’ natural beauty.


Her work is rooted in technical expertise and continuous growth. Carlena participates regularly in Craft Collective’s advanced education programs, keeping her skills sharp and her perspective current. She’s known for mastering techniques that range from precise, structured cuts to hand-painted balayage, and she has a special gift for blending tones that look organic and effortlessly lived-in. Her ability to balance creativity with restraint ensures that every look feels intentional rather than overstyled.


Clients often describe Carlena as genuine, kind, and intuitive. She has a talent for reading her clients’ needs and turning even the simplest appointment into a personalized experience. Whether a guest wants a subtle refresh or a bold transformation, she approaches every service with equal attention and artistry. Her calm presence and careful communication help clients feel at ease, fostering the kind of long-term relationships that Craft Collective Salon Group is known for.


What sets Carlena apart is her holistic view of beauty she understands that hair is not just a statement, but an extension of how someone feels day-to-day. She educates her clients about maintaining their look, from at-home styling tips to product recommendations that protect the health of their hair. This commitment to long-term results reflects Craft Collective’s broader mission to elevate the standard of salon experiences in Pittsburgh through authenticity and care.


Within the salon team, Carlena contributes to a culture of excellence and collaboration. She shares insights, supports newer stylists, and consistently strives to elevate her craft. Her professionalism and artistry make her an integral part of the collective energy that defines the salon’s success. Every appointment with Carlena is both a creative exchange and a reaffirmation of why Craft Collective Salon Group remains one of the best hair salons in Pittsburgh.


Her style philosophy is rooted in simplicity and balance beauty that looks as good on the go as it does in the mirror. She believes that great hair doesn’t have to be complicated; it just has to be crafted with skill and purpose. That mindset allows her to create versatile, flattering looks that stand the test of time.
