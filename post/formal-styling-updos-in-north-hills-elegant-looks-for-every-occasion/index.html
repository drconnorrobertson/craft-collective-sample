Formal Styling & Updos in North Hills: Elegant Looks for Every Occasion

Craft Collective Team · 2025-12-08


A perfect hairstyle completes every special moment. At Craft Collective Salon Group in North Hills, our formal styling and updo services combine timeless craftsmanship with modern artistry, ensuring your look feels as memorable as the event itself.


Whether you’re preparing for a wedding, prom, photo shoot, or elegant gala, our stylists design looks that balance structure, softness, and staying power.


The Craft Collective Approach to Event Styling

We believe every updo tells a story. Our process begins with understanding your outfit, neckline, accessories, and personality, ensuring your hair complements your entire ensemble, not just the occasion.


Our stylists specialize in:


– Wedding & bridal styling

– Bridesmaid and mother-of-the-bride looks

– Prom, gala, and black-tie events

– Editorial & photo-ready styling

– Sleek ponytails, buns, and textured chignons


Each look is custom-engineered for your hair type, desired finish, and event timeline.


Types of Updos and Formal Styles

1. Classic Chignon

The timeless twist that never fails to impress. Low at the nape or structured high on the crown, the chignon adds refinement to any occasion.


Perfect for: Weddings, corporate galas, or evenings out.


Pairs beautifully with: a Gloss & Shine Enhancement for luminous reflection.


2. Romantic Low Bun

Softly pinned curls and waves create movement and femininity without stiffness.


Ideal for: Outdoor ceremonies or garden events.


Maintenance tip: Lock in shape with humidity-resistant finishing spray.


3. Braided Updo

Intricate yet effortless, braids add texture and boho-chic charm to any look.


Works well with: Balayage or Dimensional Brunette Techniques for highlighted definition.


4. Hollywood Waves

A glamorous, red-carpet finish with sculpted curls and high shine.


Best paired with: a Keratin Smoothing Treatment before the event to ensure frizz-free silkiness.


5. Modern Ponytail

Sleek, elevated, and striking. High or mid-height ponytails can be structured or soft, making them ideal for minimalists who still want sophistication.


Bridal & Group Styling

Our bridal styling services are fully customizable, including trial appointments to test shape, hold, and accessories ahead of the big day. We welcome bridal parties for coordinated styles, offering private scheduling and refreshments for a seamless, enjoyable experience.


We also collaborate directly with photographers and makeup artists to ensure a cohesive vision from start to finish.


Sustainable Styling Practices

All event services adhere to our Sustainable & Eco-Friendly Salon standards. From aerosol recycling to non-toxic finishing products, every style is created responsibly without compromising performance.


Our stylists use cruelty-free, paraben-free formulations that provide hold, flexibility, and shine without buildup, ideal for both your hair and the planet.


Maintaining Your Updo or Style

To help your look last through every hug, dance, and photo:


– Avoid touching or brushing once set.

– Keep a small finishing spray on hand for flyaways.

– Sleep with a silk scarf or bonnet if extending overnight.

– Schedule a Hydration or Repair Treatment after the event to rebalance moisture and maintain hair health.
