Summer Hair Care in Pittsburgh: Protecting from Sun, Chlorine, and Heat the Smart Way

Craft Collective Team · 2025-12-18


Summer in Pittsburgh is all about sunshine, outdoor festivals, and pool days, but your hair might not be celebrating quite as much. Between the sun’s UV rays, chlorine from swimming pools, humidity, and heat styling, summer can be one of the toughest seasons for maintaining healthy, beautiful hair.


At Craft Collective Salon Group, our stylists help clients prepare and protect their hair from the unique challenges of a Pittsburgh summer. Whether you’re rocking fresh color, textured waves, or a low-maintenance cut, keeping your hair nourished and protected will make every day a good hair day, even when the weather says otherwise.


Let’s dive into everything you need to know to protect your hair this summer and keep it looking salon-fresh from June through September.


Understanding Summer Hair Stress

Summer weather introduces a few major hair aggressors:

  • UV Rays fade color and break down keratin, leading to dryness and brittleness.

  • Chlorine and Salt Water strip away natural oils and roughen the cuticle.

  • Humidity causes frizz and disrupts styling.

  • Sweat and Oil Build-Up can make hair feel limp or greasy.

  • Heat Styling adds more damage if done without protection.


The combination can leave even healthy hair feeling dull, tangled, and frizzy. But with the right routine and a few salon secrets, you can keep your strands strong and shiny all season.


1. Shield Your Hair from the Sun

Just like your skin, your hair needs SPF protection too. UV rays cause oxidative damage to the hair shaft, leading to color fading and moisture loss, especially in highlighted or chemically-treated hair.


Salon Tip: Use a UV-Protectant Leave-In

Our stylists recommend a leave-in conditioner with UV filters, which creates a barrier against sun exposure. This is especially important for blondes and redheads whose tones are more susceptible to fading.


For clients who spend lots of time outdoors, we often suggest lightweight sprays or creams that protect without weighing down the hair.


You can learn more about product recommendations in our upcoming Top 10 Professional Hair Products We Love blog.


Bonus Tip: When you know you’ll be outdoors for long stretches, try a loose braid or bun to minimize exposure. A chic wide-brimmed hat adds both style and protection.


2. Pre-Pool Prep: Protect Before You Dive In

Chlorine and salt water are notorious for stripping color and drying out hair. They can even cause discoloration, especially in blonde shades, when minerals bind to your hair shaft.


Before Swimming:

  • Rinse your hair with fresh water; hair saturated with clean water absorbs less chlorine or salt.

  • Apply a protective leave-in conditioner or oil to create a barrier layer.

  • For frequent swimmers, our stylists recommend a weekly clarifying shampoo to remove buildup, followed by a hydrating mask.


After swimming, always wash with a sulfate-free shampoo and use a nourishing conditioner to restore balance.


If you’re seeing dullness or brassiness post-pool, a gloss refresh (as we cover in Gloss & Shine Enhancements in North Hills) can bring back vibrancy in one salon visit.


3. Keep Color Vibrant with Smart Maintenance

Color-treated hair needs extra love in the summer. UV rays and mineral-heavy water can cause premature fading and shift your tone toward unwanted warmth.


At Craft Collective, we recommend scheduling mid-summer glossing or toning sessions to refresh your color and seal in shine. Clients with blondes, brunettes, or copper tones all benefit from gloss treatments that neutralize brass and enhance reflection.


Pro Tip: Pair your summer gloss with a bond-repair treatment to strengthen your hair’s internal structure and prevent UV-induced brittleness.


4. Hydration is Non-Negotiable

Between air conditioning indoors and the sun outside, your hair loses hydration quickly during summer. The result? Rough texture, frizz, and dullness.


Hydration Musts for Summer Hair Care Pittsburgh Clients:

  • Use a deep-conditioning mask once a week.

  • Switch to a moisture-rich shampoo and conditioner.

  • Incorporate a leave-in conditioner for ongoing softness.


We also recommend our in-salon moisture infusion treatments, which deliver concentrated hydration that lasts weeks.


Clients love pairing this with a Balayage Refresh & Maintenance session [link to balayage-refresh-maintenance-north-hills] to restore gloss and manageability after outdoor exposure.


5. Style Smart: Heat with Care

When it’s already hot outside, excessive heat styling can push your hair past its breaking point. Instead of daily blow-drying or curling, embrace your natural texture.


If you do use heat tools, remember: always apply a heat protectant.


These products create a thermal shield, preventing moisture loss and cuticle damage. For best results:

  • Keep tools below 350°F.

  • Air-dry halfway before blow-drying.

  • Use ionic or ceramic tools for even heating.


We also love recommending lightweight texturizing sprays and sea-salt mists for that effortless “summer undone” look (as featured in Spring 2025 Hair Trends to Try).


6. Control Humidity & Frizz

Pittsburgh’s summer humidity is legendary, and it can ruin a great blowout in minutes.


Salon-Proven Fixes:

  • Use anti-frizz serums or humidity-resistant sprays.

  • Avoid over-moisturizing with heavy oils that can make hair limp.

  • Opt for smoothing treatments or a keratin service for long-lasting frizz control.


Our stylists often use lightweight serums on dry hair to tame flyaways without creating buildup. For clients with textured or curly hair, we tailor routines using moisture-balancing products that reduce frizz while maintaining definition.


7. Scalp Health = Hair Health

Summer heat means more sweat, which can lead to scalp buildup, irritation, or oil imbalance.

Keep your scalp fresh by exfoliating gently once a week with a scalp scrub or clarifying rinse. This removes dead skin and product residue, allowing your follicles to breathe and grow healthy strands.


Pair that with regular trims to remove dry ends and maintain shape, learn more in How Often Should You Get a Trim? 


8. Travel-Friendly Hair Care Tips

Summer travel can disrupt your routine, but you don’t have to sacrifice hair health while on the go.


Pack mini versions of your favorite essentials:

  • UV leave-in spray

  • Color-safe shampoo & conditioner

  • Deep-conditioning mask

  • Lightweight serum or oil


If you’re heading to humid destinations, prioritize anti-frizz products and wide-tooth combs for detangling.


Our upcoming guide, “Humidity Hair Solutions in North Hills,” will share even more localized tips for managing frizz during Pittsburgh’s muggy months.


9. Sustainable Summer Beauty

Our Sustainable & Eco-Friendly Salon commitment means every product we use and recommend aligns with responsible beauty practices.


We use cruelty-free, low-tox formulas that protect both your hair and the environment, because clean beauty isn’t just a trend; it’s our philosophy.


Even our in-salon water systems are designed to minimize waste, ensuring every wash and rinse supports sustainability.


10. Post-Summer Recovery Plan

Once summer ends, it’s essential to reset. Sun exposure and chlorine can leave your strands parched and your color uneven.


Our stylists recommend scheduling a fall hair revival service:


  • Clarifying treatment to remove buildup

  • Deep conditioning to restore elasticity

  • Trim to remove split ends

  • Toner or gloss to rebalance tone


This prepares your hair for the cooler months ahead. You can read more in Fall 2025 Hair Trends in Pittsburgh when we roll out the next guide in this series.
