Why North Hills Clients Love the Craft Collective Experience | Craft Collective Salon Group

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-26

The North Hills community values authenticity, artistry, and experience—and that’s exactly what defines Craft Collective Salon Group. Our mission has always been simple: to bring world-class haircare to Pittsburgh’s North Hills, combining sustainable beauty with unmatched service and craft.


More Than a Salon

From the moment you walk into any Craft Collective location—whether in Wexford, Allison Park, or Ross Township—you’ll notice the difference. The atmosphere is calm, the products are clean, and every stylist is trained to understand the unique texture, tone, and structure of your hair.


Each service is a collaboration between client and artist—one that focuses on enhancing your individuality, not masking it.


Sustainable Beauty, Elevated

Our clients love that every appointment aligns with eco-conscious principles. We’ve built our reputation on clean beauty systems that perform without compromise:

  • R+Co Bleu for sustainable, high-performance styling.

  • Aveda for plant-powered nourishment and color integrity.

  • Wella Professionals for bond-safe innovation and brilliance.


Our salons use refillable color systems, biodegradable materials, and energy-efficient tools—all while maintaining the luxury experience clients expect.


Explore our full philosophy in Sustainable Beauty in Wexford: Clean, Conscious, and Confident.


Craftsmanship That Feels Personal

Every stylist at Craft Collective Salon Group goes through advanced education on precision cutting, modern color theory, and clean product chemistry. That means whether you’re getting a simple gloss or a full transformation, your result will always look and feel tailored.


We specialize in services that highlight your natural features—creating dimensional color, airy blowouts, and timeless haircuts that grow gracefully.


See our artistry in practice with Balayage and Dimensional Color in Sewickley: A Local Favorite.


Why Clients Keep Coming Back

Our North Hills clients describe Craft Collective as a blend of spa-like calm and creative energy. They come for the results, but stay for the experience—the sense that every detail has been considered, from sustainability to shine.


Consistency, comfort, and care are our hallmarks. It’s why first-time visitors quickly become loyal clients.


Your North Hills Salon Destination

Whether you live in Wexford, Allison Park, or Ross Township, you’ll find a Craft Collective salon ready to serve you with intention and artistry. Every stylist here shares the same passion: helping you feel your best while making beauty more sustainable.
