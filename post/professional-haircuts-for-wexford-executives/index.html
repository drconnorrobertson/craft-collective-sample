Professional Haircuts for Wexford Executives | Craft Collective Salon Group

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-27

In Wexford, image and confidence go hand in hand—and nothing defines your professional presence more than a great haircut. At Craft Collective Salon Group, our stylists understand how to create refined, low-maintenance cuts that balance style, structure, and sustainability. Each haircut is crafted to match your personal brand, work routine, and natural hair texture.


The Executive Standard

Professionals in Wexford expect more than just a trim—they want a style that fits their lifestyle. Whether you’re leading a board meeting, hosting clients, or enjoying a night out, your hair should look effortless yet intentional. Our stylists tailor every haircut to your face shape, growth pattern, and styling habits, ensuring long-lasting precision that grows in beautifully.


See how we design shapes for everyday wear in Allison Park’s Go-To Salon for Natural, Effortless Styles.


Men’s Executive Cuts

For our male professionals, we specialize in structured, clean styles that maintain their form between appointments. Popular requests include:

  • Modern Crew: Textured length on top with soft, natural fades.

  • Classic Side-Part: Clean lines and a timeless silhouette.

  • Tailored Crop: Refined, contemporary finish with low effort.


We use R+Co Bleu Continental Glossing Wax and Wella EIMI Texture Touch for flexible control that lasts from the office to after-hours events.


For more inspiration, explore Modern Men’s Cuts Popular in North Hills.


Women’s Executive Haircuts

Wexford’s professional women prefer cuts that balance elegance with manageability. Our stylists specialize in:

  • Blunt Bobs: Clean geometry with sleek, professional lines.

  • Soft Layered Lobs: Movement without maintenance.

  • Polished Long Layers: Volume and shine with flexible styling options.


Each cut is designed to enhance your natural texture, with Aveda Botanical Repair Leave-In Treatment for strength and R+Co Bleu Essential Hair Tonic to protect against heat and humidity.


Precision Meets Sustainability

Our eco-luxury salon model ensures every haircut supports a greener world. From energy-efficient dryers to biodegradable capes, each detail is designed with purpose. Even our styling lines—R+Co Bleu, Aveda, and Wella Professionals—reflect a shared commitment to sustainability.


To learn more about how we balance sustainability and sophistication, visit Sewickley’s Luxury Salon Experience: Sustainable and Sophisticated.


The Finishing Touch

Every haircut at Craft Collective Salon Group Wexford ends with a personalized blowout, adding shine and dimension that lasts for days. Using Wella Shinefinity gloss and R+Co Bleu Optical Illusion Oil, we finish your service with a radiant, professional glow.

For more shine-enhancing care, check out Glossing and Shine Revival Guide for Wexford Clients.


At-Home Maintenance

To extend your style between visits:

  • Schedule a trim every 6–8 weeks.

  • Use a silk pillowcase to preserve shape.

  • Apply leave-in hydration weekly.

  • Avoid overwashing—opt for dry shampoo when needed.


For healthy texture year-round, our stylists recommend alternating between Aveda Nutriplenish and R+Co Bleu’s Daily Conditioner.

Polished, professional, and sustainable—your next haircut should do more than just look great. Book your appointment at Craft Collective Salon Group Wexford and experience how precision and sustainability redefine professional style.
