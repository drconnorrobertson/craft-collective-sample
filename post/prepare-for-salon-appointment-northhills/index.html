How to Prepare for a Salon Appointment in the North Hills

Craft Collective Team · 2026-02-22

A useful salon appointment starts with a clear brief: what you want to change, what has already been done to your hair, and what you can realistically maintain afterward. This checklist helps you prepare for a North Hills visit without guessing which service or preparation routine is right for you.

Updated November 21, 2026. Follow any instructions your stylist gives for the service you have booked. A haircut, extension consultation and color correction can require different preparation; this guide does not replace that conversation.

Confirm the service, stylist and studio

Check the appointment confirmation for the service name, stylist, studio and start time. If you are unsure whether to choose a haircut, a color service or a consultation, ask before booking the closest-sounding option. Describe the result you want and the work already on your hair so the team can help identify an appropriate starting appointment.

The North Hills studio page lists its location at 2014D Babcock Blvd, Pittsburgh. If you intended to visit Canonsburg, check the Canonsburg location information and confirm scheduling with the salon. Use the location on your confirmation rather than assuming every stylist or service is available at both studios.

Prepare a short hair-history note

Write down the color, lightening, smoothing or extension services you remember, with approximate dates where possible. Include at-home color and work performed by other salons. If you know the product or service name, bring that information; if you do not know it, say so rather than trying to supply a precise answer.

Separate the history from the goal. “I used an at-home dark color last winter” is history. “I want a lighter result with fewer maintenance visits” is a goal. Both help the consultation, but neither establishes which service is suitable or what can be achieved in one visit. Let the stylist assess that before making a plan.

Choose photos and explain what you like

Bring a few reference photos rather than a large folder of unrelated looks. For each photo, identify the feature you want to discuss: the length, face-framing shape, contrast, placement or overall tone. A photo of a result you do not want can be useful when you explain the specific difference.

Include a recent photo of your own hair if you are speaking to the team before the appointment. Tell the stylist how you usually wear it and how much time you want to spend styling. Reference images are conversation tools, not a promise that different hair histories and starting points will produce the same result.

Ask about washing and product use

Ask the salon whether your booked service calls for freshly washed hair, dry hair, your usual styling routine or another preparation step. Mention products or styling you expect to use before the visit and ask whether any change is needed. Avoid applying a universal “wash within 24 hours” rule to every appointment.

If you have already received instructions, follow those rather than combining several online checklists. When a detail is unclear, contact the salon before making a last-minute change. The useful outcome is a shared understanding of how to arrive for your particular service.

Discuss time, budget and maintenance before agreeing to the plan

Tell the team about a firm departure time or an upcoming event when arranging the appointment. Ask for the expected appointment duration and whether the proposed change may involve a consultation or more than one visit. Do not assume the booking label alone explains every step or its timing.

Use the salon pricing information as a starting point, then confirm the proposed service, inclusions and estimated charge with the team. Ask what follow-up appointments or at-home routine the stylist recommends, and explain what frequency and budget you can maintain. Agree on changes to the plan before additional work begins.

Before you leave home

  • Check the date, start time, studio address and stylist on the confirmation.
  • Have your reference photos and short service-history note available.
  • Follow the preparation instructions given for your booked service.
  • Allow for your journey and ask the salon what to do if you expect to be late.
  • If your plans have changed, use the salon's published contact or booking route to discuss the appointment; check your confirmation for the applicable policy.
