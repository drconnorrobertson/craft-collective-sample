Pittsburgh Hair Color Cost: How to Compare Quotes

Craft Collective Salon Group · Updated October 1, 2026

Color prices are difficult to compare without knowing what an appointment includes. A starting price may exclude a haircut or finish, while another salon bundles those steps. The useful question is what the complete plan will cost on your starting hair.

Confirm Craft Collective pricing and availability with your selected stylist through the salon or booking system. This guide does not publish an unverified citywide average or promise one price for every color result.

What affects a color estimate?

  • Your current color and previous salon or at-home work.
  • The amount of hair being colored or lightened.
  • Length and density, which affect application work.
  • The goal: subtle refresh, solid coverage or substantial change.
  • Additional steps such as toning, root blending, cutting and finishing.
  • Whether testing, correction or later visits are needed.

Compare the same appointment

Ask for the full arrival-to-finish plan and write down what is included. Partial and full highlights describe different coverage. A root touch-up is not a replacement for a blonding appointment.

Use the color booking guide if you are unsure which name fits your goal. Describe the result first, then let the stylist confirm the service.

Make the estimate clear

  • Is toner or gloss included when needed?
  • Is the haircut or blow-dry included?
  • Is this a starting price, personalized estimate or hourly rate?
  • What could increase the cost, and will that be discussed first?
  • If several visits are needed, what is each visit intended to achieve?

Compare upkeep as well as the first visit

Ask what follow-up appointments the look may need. Regrowth, tone and brightness can be maintained differently. A low-contrast result and complete coverage can have different return plans even when the first appointment costs are similar.

Share your comfortable budget and how often you can attend. Use the maintenance calendar guide to think about the ongoing cost.

Consider evidence alongside the estimate

Browse actual salon work and client feedback about color and extensions. Ask to see work with a similar starting point. Your consultation establishes the individual plan.

Confirm your North Hills or Canonsburg studio and stylist. If you want to move from permanent at-home dye to a much lighter shade, contact the salon about a consultation rather than treating a routine color starting price as your budget.

Plan your salon visit

Choose North Hills at 2014D Babcock Blvd or Canonsburg at 115 W Pike St. Canonsburg is by appointment. Confirm your stylist, location and service before booking.
