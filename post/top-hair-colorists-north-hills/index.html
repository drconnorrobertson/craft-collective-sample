Top Hair Colorists in North Hills: Meet the Experts Behind Craft Collective | Craft Collective Salon Group

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-26

In the heart of Pittsburgh’s North Hills, Craft Collective Salon Group is home to some of the region’s most talented colorists—artists who blend chemistry, technique, and creativity to produce exceptional, sustainable results.


Why Craft Collective Leads in Color Expertise

Each stylist at Craft Collective undergoes continuous education through Wella Professionals, Aveda, and R+Co Bleu. This ensures our team stays at the forefront of tone technology, bond repair systems, and precision placement.


Our colorists specialize in:

  • Balayage and lived-in blondes

  • Glossing and tone correction

  • Dimensional brunettes and rich reds

  • Gray blending and root melting


Learn how we stay ahead of color innovation in 2025 Color Correction Trends: How Craft Collective Perfects Every Shade.


The Science of Shine

We use clean, pH-balanced formulas that maintain the health of the cuticle while enhancing reflection. Every gloss and toner is customized for your unique pigment balance and undertone.


See our shine philosophy in Glossing and Shine Revival Guide for Wexford Clients.


Personalized Consultation Process

Our colorists don’t just paint—they plan. Each appointment begins with an in-depth discussion of your vision, maintenance schedule, and goals. From subtle refinement to total transformation, we create color that fits your real life.


For professionals seeking polish and longevity, read Professional Haircuts for Wexford Executives.


Sustainability as the Standard

Every color service at Craft Collective reflects our sustainability pledge:

  • Biodegradable foils and gloves

  • Energy-efficient equipment

  • Refillable color systems

  • Low-impact rinse protocols


Our mission is to redefine what luxury looks like—clean, responsible, and beautiful.

Discover the broader movement in The North Hills Guide to Sustainable Hair Trends in 2025.


Why Clients Trust Us

Clients call our colorists approachable, creative, and meticulous. They trust us not just for results but for consistency, transparency, and care. That’s what turns a color appointment into a lasting relationship.
