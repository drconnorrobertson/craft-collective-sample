Essential Hair Care Tips for Summer in North Hills Pittsburgh

Craft Collective Team · 2026-07-09

Summer in North Hills Pittsburgh brings sunshine, outdoor fun, and, unfortunately, extra challenges for your hair. Sun exposure, humidity, chlorine, and saltwater can all affect hair health, leaving it dry, brittle, or faded in color. Craft Collective Salon Group provides expert guidance to help clients maintain vibrant, healthy hair all summer long.


How Summer Affects Your Hair

Summer conditions can cause:


  • Fading or brassiness in color-treated hair

  • Dryness and split ends from sun and heat exposure

  • Frizz due to humidity and sweat

  • Damage from swimming in chlorinated pools or saltwater


Awareness of these effects allows clients to adjust their routines to protect hair and scalp.


Top Summer Hair Care Tips

North Hills Pittsburgh clients can keep hair healthy in the summer with these tips:


  • Hydrate Hair Regularly: Use deep conditioning treatments and leave-in conditioners to combat dryness

  • Protect from Sun Damage: Wear hats or use UV-protectant sprays

  • Rinse Hair After Swimming: Chlorine and salt can strip natural oils; a quick rinse helps prevent damage

  • Limit Heat Styling: Embrace air-dried styles or heatless waves to minimize breakage


These habits help maintain shine, softness, and overall hair health during hot summer months.


Professional Summer Treatments

Craft Collective Salon Group offers treatments specifically for summer hair care:


  • Moisture-Rich Deep Conditioning Masks: Restore hydration lost to sun and swimming

  • Bond-Building Treatments: Strengthen hair damaged by heat or chemicals

  • Gloss and Shine Treatments: Revive color-treated hair and protect against fading

  • Scalp Treatments: Keep the scalp hydrated and balanced despite sweat and sun exposure


Professional care ensures hair remains strong, vibrant, and manageable throughout the season.


Color Protection Tips for Summer

Color-treated hair needs extra attention in the summer:


  • Use color-safe, sulfate-free shampoos

  • Schedule gloss or toner treatments to maintain vibrancy

  • Avoid prolonged sun exposure or use protective hair products

  • Hydrate strands to prevent dryness that can dull color


These steps help North Hills Pittsburgh clients maintain bright, healthy hair even in harsh summer conditions.


Why Clients Choose Craft Collective Salon Group

North Hills Pittsburgh residents trust Craft Collective for personalized summer hair care advice, professional treatments, and tailored product recommendations. Every appointment ensures hair stays healthy, protected, and beautiful during the season.


Summer doesn’t have to compromise hair health. With guidance from Craft Collective Salon Group, North Hills Pittsburgh clients can protect their hair from sun, chlorine, and humidity, keeping it vibrant, hydrated, and manageable. Professional care combined with at-home strategies ensures summer hair looks its best, all season long.
