How Often Should You Wash Your Hair in Pittsburgh

Craft Collective Team · 2026-01-21


Many people wonder how often they should wash their hair, especially with Pittsburgh’s mix of humidity, rain, cold winters, and hard water. Washing frequency affects your scalp health, hydration levels, color longevity, and how your style holds throughout the week. There is no universal rule because your hair type, lifestyle, and environment all play a role. This guide breaks down how to determine the right wash schedule based on your needs as a Pittsburgh resident.


Why Washing Frequency Matters

How often you wash influences:

  • Scalp health

  • Hydration

  • Oil balance

  • Frizz levels

  • Color longevity

  • Product buildup

  • Curl definition


Finding the right routine helps maintain healthy hair and prevents over-cleansing or under-cleansing.


How Pittsburgh's Climate Affects Washing

Humidity

Makes hair feel frizzy faster, which may tempt people to overwash.


Cold air

Causes dryness and flaking, making gentle washing essential.


Hard water

Creates mineral buildup, leading people to feel like their hair is dirty even when it is not.


Seasonal shifts

Your wash schedule may change with the weather.


How Often Should You Wash Based on Hair Type

Straight hair

Every two to three days

Straight hair shows oil quickly because it lacks curl structure to slow sebum movement.


Wavy hair

Every three to four days

Wavy hair needs balance. Too much washing dries it out, too little leads to limpness.


Curly hair

Every five to seven days

Curls thrive with natural oils. Overwashing leads to dryness and frizz.


Coily hair

Every seven to ten days

Coily textures need moisture retention and benefit from fewer wash days.


Color-treated hair

Every three to five days, depending on porosity

Frequent washing fades the tone more quickly.


Thick or dense hair

It can go longer between washes due to oil distribution patterns.


Fine hair

It may require more frequent washing because oil is visible sooner.


Signs You Are Washing Too Often

  1. Your hair feels dry

  2. Ends look frizzy

  3. Color fades quickly

  4. Your scalp feels tight or itchy

  5. Your hair loses shine

  6. Your curls lack definition


Overwashing can strip protective oils and create more frizz.


Signs You Are Not Washing Enough

  1. Your scalp feels greasy

  2. You experience itching or flaking

  3. Your roots feel heavy

  4. Your curls clump unevenly

  5. Your hair smells musty

  6. Your styling products stop working


Underwashing can cause buildup and scalp congestion.


How to Adjust Washing Frequency for Pittsburgh Conditions

In summer

Choose lightweight products and wash every two to four days, depending on hair type.


In winter

Extend washes to preserve hydration. Focus on moisturizing shampoos and conditioners.


During rainy seasons

Use anti-humidity stylers and refresh instead of overwashing.


When dealing with hard water

Use a shower filter

Use a chelating shampoo one to two times a month

Schedule salon detox treatments


How to Refresh Hair Between Washes

Dry shampoo

Absorbs oil for straight and wavy hair.


Curl refreshing spray

Reactivates curl pattern without a full wash.


Leave-in conditioners

Help soften frizz and add moisture.


Light restyling

Using a blow dryer on low or diffusing curls adds movement.


Scalp tonics

Reduce irritation and extend time between washes.


Why Water Temperature Matters

Hot water

Opens the cuticle and dries out the hair.


Warm or cool water

Keeps hair shinier and helps retain moisture.


Cool rinses

Seal the cuticle and add natural shine.
