Sustainable Beauty for the Ross Township Community

Craft Collective Salon Group is proud to lead the way in sustainable salon practices in the North Hills area. We believe great beauty should never come at the expense of the planet. From our product selection to our waste management systems, sustainability is integrated into everything we do.


Our partnership with R+Co and R+Co Bleu reflects our commitment to clean, cruelty-free beauty. R+Co’s vegan formulas are packaged in recyclable materials and made without sulfates, parabens, or mineral oils. Our stylists are also trained in water conservation and waste reduction. By using refillable backbar systems and eco-conscious color lines like Aveda, we’re reducing single-use plastics and supporting a cleaner future for Pittsburgh’s beauty industry.
