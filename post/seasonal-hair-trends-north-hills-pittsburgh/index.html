Seasonal Hair Trends in North Hills Pittsburgh | Craft Collective Salon Group

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-26

Each season brings a new energy — new colors, new textures, and new ways to express yourself. At Craft Collective Salon Group in North Hills Pittsburgh, we see seasonal shifts as opportunities for creativity. Whether you want to embrace rich winter tones or sun-kissed summer blondes, our stylists are trained to craft looks that complement both the weather and your lifestyle.


Our approach to seasonal hair trends is rooted in customization. We never chase fads blindly; instead, we interpret them through the lens of timeless beauty and wearable design. Using professional products from R+Co, R+Co Bleu, Wella Professionals, and Aveda Color, we translate global inspiration into North Hills-ready styles that feel modern, natural, and effortless.


Winter: Depth, Gloss, and Protection

Winter in Pittsburgh means dry air, cold wind, and artificial heat — all of which take a toll on the hair. That’s why our winter trend focus is on hydration and shine.


Rich espresso brunettes, deep cherry reds, and creamy dark blondes dominate this season. We enhance these tones using Wella Professionals glosses that reflect light beautifully, restoring vibrancy lost to the winter elements.


Hydration becomes the hero step in every appointment. We rely on nourishing masks and treatments from R+Co Bleu to reinforce strength and lock in moisture. Pairing these with precision trims keeps split ends at bay and ensures a sleek, healthy finish.


Styling trends for winter lean toward polished and protective — think sleek ponytails, low chignons, and softly waved lobs. Clients love our recommendations for lightweight oils and anti-static sprays from R+Co, which help maintain smoothness despite indoor dryness.


Spring: Renewal, Volume, and Movement

Spring in North Hills signals change — lighter fabrics, fresh color palettes, and hair that feels alive again. We see an annual rise in requests for dimensional blonding and soft layers that move naturally in the breeze.


Balayage remains the star of spring, but our stylists personalize it by blending pastel tones or subtle money pieces that frame the face. Using Aveda Color and Wella Professionals hybrid glosses, we create luminous, multi-tonal color that transitions perfectly into summer.


Cuts tend to become lighter and airier. The modern shag and butterfly cut lead spring trends, emphasizing movement and texture. We finish these looks with volumizing sprays and flexible mousses from R+Co that add lift without stiffness.


Summer: Bright, Effortless, and Sun-Protected

When the Pittsburgh humidity hits, effortless beauty becomes the goal. Summer 2025 hair trends in North Hills focus on low-maintenance color and air-dried texture. Clients are leaning into lived-in highlights, golden hues, and beachy movement.


Our stylists specialize in “heatless styling” — techniques that embrace natural texture. We teach clients how to prep with lightweight leave-ins and curl-enhancing creams from R+Co Bleu for frizz-free results.


Color formulas in summer emphasize tone control. We incorporate bond-building agents from Wella Professionals to protect against UV exposure and pool chemicals. For blondes, toning glosses every 4–6 weeks maintain vibrancy and prevent brassiness.


Protective styles also trend heavily — braided crowns, claw-clip twists, and loose buns keep hair stylish while minimizing heat and friction. These looks pair beautifully with dewy summer makeup and lightweight fabrics.


Fall: Warmth, Transition, and Texture

As the leaves turn, so does the palette. Fall is the season of copper, caramel, and chestnut. In Pittsburgh’s golden light, these shades glow naturally, offering warmth and depth after summer’s brightness.


At Craft Collective, our fall services focus on color transitions — taking blondes darker, toning out residual warmth, or refreshing red tones that may have faded in the sun. Aveda Color is our go-to for these warm, reflective hues because of its plant-based richness and softness.


Fall also welcomes texture back into styling. Clients love soft blowouts with minimal structure — undone waves, curtain bangs, and shoulder-length shapes that frame the face. R+Co dry texture sprays and soft hold hairsprays are staples in our styling arsenal this time of year.


Adapting Trends to Real Life

Not every seasonal trend fits every client — and that’s exactly why customization defines the Craft Collective experience. Our stylists take inspiration from runway looks, editorial shoots, and social media, then adapt them to each guest’s texture, color level, and lifestyle.


A client who travels frequently may need a style that air-dries beautifully; another who attends formal events might prefer a glossy, sculpted look. By balancing artistic vision with real-world practicality, we ensure every seasonal update feels authentic, not forced.


This is also where education plays a key role. We teach clients how to maintain their seasonal look at home with proper care routines — alternating moisturizing and strengthening products, adjusting wash frequency, and protecting hair from UV or dryness depending on the time of year.


Product Philosophy Behind Every Trend

Seasonal styling is only as strong as the health behind it. That’s why we trust four powerhouse brands:


  • R+Co for creativity, performance, and texture versatility.

  • R+Co Bleu for luxury, sustainability, and high-definition shine.

  • Wella Professionals for precision color science and bond repair.

  • Aveda Color for natural, plant-based, sustainable color.


By integrating these systems into every service, we maintain color longevity and scalp health across all seasons — something many Pittsburgh salons overlook.


Year-Round Maintenance Plans

To keep hair vibrant year-round, we recommend booking seasonal maintenance appointments every 8–12 weeks. These sessions allow for gloss refreshes, trims, and product adjustments based on environmental changes.


Our stylists personalize each maintenance plan to your needs:


  • Winter → hydration + shine boosters

  • Spring → brightening + bond repair

  • Summer → tone protection + UV defense

  • Fall → nourishment + color refresh


This rhythm ensures your hair stays healthy, stylish, and perfectly balanced no matter what season you’re in.


Why Seasonal Trends Matter for North Hills Clients

Living in Western Pennsylvania means dealing with four very distinct seasons. The humidity, dryness, and temperature swings here can dramatically affect hair texture and tone. Craft Collective’s seasonal approach isn’t about chasing fashion — it’s about protecting and enhancing your hair through environmental shifts unique to our region.


Our North Hills location allows us to understand what local clients really need — practical beauty that feels elevated but sustainable. From bridal looks to back-to-school cuts to holiday blowouts, we create styles that move with you through the year.


The Craft Collective Promise

Our stylists are constantly training, researching, and testing to stay ahead of trends — not just globally, but locally. Every look we create is built on our pillars of education, sustainability, and artistry.


Seasonal trends will always come and go, but healthy, personalized beauty lasts. That’s why at Craft Collective Salon Group, we don’t just follow trends — we craft them into something timeless, wearable, and uniquely you.
