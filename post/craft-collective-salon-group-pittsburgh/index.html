Craft Collective Salon Group: Redefining Beauty in Pittsburgh’s North Hills | Craft Collective Salon Group

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-26

In the heart of Pittsburgh’s North Hills, Craft Collective Salon Group is redefining what it means to experience modern beauty. More than a salon, it’s a collective of passionate artists united by a simple mission—to create effortless, sustainable, and timeless results for every client.


A Vision Rooted in Craft and Care

Craft Collective Salon Group was founded on the belief that beauty should be both luxurious and responsible. Every haircut, color, and treatment is guided by craftsmanship and care for the planet—proof that artistry and ethics can exist in perfect harmony.


From the first consultation to the final blowout, our stylists approach each service with precision, creativity, and attention to detail.


Explore this approach in How Craft Collective Is Redefining the Modern Salon Experience.


Sustainability in Every Strand

Clean beauty is more than a value—it’s our standard.We partner exclusively with brands that share our environmental mission:

  • Aveda – plant-powered color with up to 96% naturally derived ingredients.

  • Wella Professionals – advanced glossing and tone science for brilliant, lasting shine.

  • R+Co Bleu – vegan, cruelty-free styling that supports ocean conservation.


Every aspect of the salon—from energy-efficient tools to biodegradable towels—is designed with sustainability in mind.


Learn more about our green philosophy in The North Hills Guide to Sustainable Hair Trends in 2025.


Our Expertise

Our stylists are trained in:

  • Balayage and Dimensional Color for natural, sunlit tones.

  • Precision Haircuts and Layering that complement every texture.

  • Glossing and Repair Treatments for radiant, healthy hair.

  • Bridal and Event Styling that combines durability with elegance.


See our artistry in Best Balayage in Pittsburgh: Craft Collective Salon Group Leads the Way.


Meet the Artists

Our team represents the heart of Craft Collective. Each stylist brings years of experience, continued education, and a shared passion for connection and creativity. Together, they form a culture of collaboration where learning never stops and every guest feels like part of the family.


Get to know our team in Meet the Artists: Inside Craft Collective’s Stylist Team.

Transformations That Inspire


We believe hair has the power to transform not only how you look—but how you feel. Every service is a story of confidence, self-expression, and renewal.

Explore these journeys in From Everyday to Effortless: A Real Client Transformation.


Why North Hills Chooses Craft Collective

Our clients consistently describe Craft Collective as a space that feels both professional and personal—where expertise meets empathy, and every detail matters.


They return because we don’t just do hair; we build relationships rooted in trust, education, and genuine care.
