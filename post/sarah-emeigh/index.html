Sarah Emeigh

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-21


Sarah Emeigh is one of Craft Collective Salon Group’s most naturally gifted stylists, known for her precision, creativity, and calm confidence behind the chair. Her approach reflects everything Craft Collective stands for beauty that’s intentional, personal, and designed to last. With a keen eye for balance and an unwavering commitment to her craft, Sarah has become one of Pittsburgh’s most trusted names for clients seeking hair that feels both refined and effortless.


At Craft Collective Salon Group, Sarah approaches every service with purpose and professionalism. She believes hair is an extension of self-expression, and her mission is to help each client discover a look that feels authentic, manageable, and elevated. She begins every appointment with a thoughtful consultation, listening closely to her client’s goals and assessing their hair texture, tone, and lifestyle. This collaborative process ensures that every color, cut, and finish is fully customized.


Sarah specializes in dimensional coloring, soft blonding, and precision haircuts that move beautifully and grow out seamlessly. Her attention to tone and texture gives her color work a naturally luminous quality, while her cutting techniques emphasize shape and structure without feeling overdone. Whether she’s crafting a subtle enhancement or a full transformation, Sarah delivers results that are polished, balanced, and effortlessly wearable.


Her clients often describe her as patient, professional, and detail-oriented. She has a calm presence that makes each appointment relaxing and enjoyable, and her ability to communicate clearly ensures her guests always feel comfortable and informed. Sarah takes pride in making her clients feel confident and cared for, turning every salon visit into a moment of self-renewal rather than just a service.


Beyond her technical skill, Sarah is passionate about continued growth and education. She regularly participates in Craft Collective’s advanced training programs, learning new techniques and exploring innovative approaches to modern hair design. Her willingness to keep evolving ensures her clients benefit from the latest trends, while her grounded technique ensures those results remain timeless.


Within the Craft Collective team, Sarah is known for her professionalism and quiet leadership. She’s a team player who contributes to the salon’s positive culture of learning, collaboration, and mutual respect. Her thoughtful approach and high standards help elevate those around her, reinforcing the salon’s commitment to excellence in every detail.


Sarah’s personal philosophy centers on the idea that great hair should make life easier, not harder. She focuses on creating looks that her clients can recreate at home styles that look beautiful with minimal effort. This emphasis on sustainable, long-lasting beauty aligns perfectly with Craft Collective Salon Group’s reputation for modern, mindful hair care.


Her artistry and dedication are key reasons Craft Collective continues to be recognized as one of the best hair salons in Pittsburgh. Sarah embodies the balance of craft and care that makes the salon unique approachable luxury grounded in genuine connection.
