Tape-In Hair Extensions in North Hills: Effortless Fullness with Invisible Blending

Craft Collective Team · 2025-12-09


For clients who want instant transformation without the long appointment, tape-in hair extensions offer the perfect balance of convenience, natural appearance, and comfort. At Craft Collective Salon Group in North Hills, we specialize in creating seamless, low-maintenance tape-in looks that enhance your natural texture while maintaining hair health and manageability.


Whether you’re looking for extra fullness, added length, or a color enhancement without chemical processing, our stylists custom-match and apply each extension to blend invisibly into your natural hair.


What Are Tape-In Extensions?

Tape-in extensions are thin, pre-taped wefts of 100% human hair that are carefully sandwiched around small sections of your natural hair. They sit flat against the scalp, making them ideal for fine to medium textures that need volume without bulk.


These extensions are gentle, lightweight, and completely reusable, giving you both luxury and practicality in one service.


Benefits include:


– Fast installation (usually under 2 hours)

– Virtually invisible attachment points

– Lightweight comfort with zero pulling or strain

– Ideal for color blending and highlights

– Reusable for 2 to 3 applications with proper care


Why Clients Love Tape-Ins

Tape-ins are the go-to choice for clients who want a natural enhancement without a high-maintenance routine. They move freely, lie flat, and look completely seamless, whether you’re wearing your hair up or down.


At Craft Collective, we install and style each weft with precise sectioning and tension control, ensuring comfort and durability from the first wash to the last wear.


Perfect for:


– Clients seeking subtle volume or modest length

– Busy professionals needing low-maintenance upkeep

– Brides and event guests who want a full, polished finish

– Color blending without chemical processing (especially when paired with Balayage or Gloss Enhancements)


The Craft Collective Application Process

Every tape-in service begins with a consultation to evaluate your hair’s condition, density, and goals. We color-match your extensions for a perfect tone blend, ensuring no visible seams or harsh lines.


Step-by-Step Process

  1. Prep & Cleanse: We clarify your hair to remove oils and residue for better adhesion.

  2. Sectioning: Hair is divided into even rows to ensure balanced placement.

  3. Installation: Pre-taped wefts are applied using a sandwich technique, layering each piece with precision.

  4. Cut & Blend: Extensions are shaped to integrate seamlessly with your natural haircut.

  5. Finish & Style: We apply a smoothing serum and complete your look with a professional blowout.


Maintenance & Longevity

Tape-in extensions last about 6 to 8 weeks per installation, depending on hair growth and care. The hair itself can be reused for up to 3 to 4 installations with proper maintenance.


Maintenance visits include:


– Re-taping and repositioning wefts to maintain even weight distribution

– Scalp cleansing and reapplication

– Optional Gloss & Shine Enhancements for tone refresh


Extension Care at Home

To extend the life of your tape-ins:


– Wait 48 hours before washing after installation

– Use sulfate-free shampoo and avoid oils near the bonds

– Brush daily with a loop or extension-safe brush

– Sleep with hair in a low ponytail or braid

– Avoid high heat directly on the tape area


Our stylists will recommend professional care products from our Product Education & Hair Care collection for gentle cleansing and moisture balance.


Color & Styling Flexibility

One of the greatest advantages of tape-in extensions is versatility. They can be used to:


– Add highlights or lowlights without bleach

– Create dimension and depth in color services

– Enhance fullness in Blowouts & Styling 

– Support Formal Updos with hidden reinforcement


We often recommend a Hair Treatments & Health service before installation to ensure your natural hair is strong and properly prepped for bonding.


Sustainable Extensions

Our Sustainable & Eco-Friendly Salon commitment includes partnering with ethical extension suppliers and using low-waste packaging. We recycle all used tape and foils through Green Circle Salons, giving you luxury without environmental guilt.
