Meet the Stylists | Craft Collective Salon Group North Hills Pittsburgh

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-26

At Craft Collective Salon Group, the art of great hair begins with the right team. Nestled in the heart of North Hills Pittsburgh, our salon is home to a group of stylists who blend artistry, education, and passion into every service they perform. The name Collective represents our philosophy — a community of experts, each bringing their own strengths and perspectives to create an unmatched salon experience.


When you walk into Craft Collective, you’re not just booking an appointment; you’re joining a culture of creativity, integrity, and care. Every stylist here is trained in both advanced technical skill and personalized client consultation, ensuring that your look is as unique as you are.


The Collective Philosophy: Collaboration Over Competition

Our name isn’t just a brand — it’s a belief system. We built Craft Collective Salon Group around the idea that great hair is a team effort. Collaboration fuels everything we do. From sharing education techniques to consulting together on color transformations, our stylists work side by side to deliver the best possible outcome for every guest.


Unlike traditional salons where stylists work in silos, we operate as one creative ecosystem. You’ll see stylists assisting one another, exchanging ideas, and celebrating each client’s transformation as a collective achievement. This approach keeps our culture positive and our craft constantly evolving.


It’s also what keeps our clients coming back — because they know they’re not just booking a stylist, they’re benefiting from the knowledge of an entire team.


Education at the Core

The beauty industry evolves daily, and so do we. Every stylist at Craft Collective Salon Group commits to continuous education in both artistry and science. From advanced cutting workshops to global color seminars with Wella Professionals, Aveda, R+Co, and R+Co Bleu, we never stop learning.


Our team regularly participates in hands-on training led by top educators in balayage, precision cutting, color correction, and sustainable beauty practices. We stay at the forefront of industry innovation so we can bring the latest trends and techniques directly to our North Hills clientele.


This commitment means that every stylist — whether junior or senior — has a strong technical foundation paired with a deep understanding of modern design. It’s why our results stay consistent, refined, and elevated.


Experience and Expertise

Every stylist at Craft Collective Salon Group specializes in at least one area of expertise, from advanced blonding to textured cutting to event styling. We encourage each team member to develop their own creative niche, so clients can match with a stylist who truly aligns with their vision.


Our salon culture thrives on mentorship. Senior stylists share their experience with emerging talent, ensuring every guest receives both innovation and mastery. New stylists learn not only technique but also the Craft Collective approach to client care — one rooted in respect, authenticity, and communication.


Whether you’re visiting for a precision haircut, multidimensional balayage, or a full bridal transformation, our team approaches every service with intention and artistry.


Local Roots, Modern Vision

Being based in North Hills Pittsburgh means we understand our community — professionals, students, parents, and creatives who all want to look and feel their best without pretense. Our team has grown with the local area, building a loyal client base that values quality, consistency, and connection.


We bring global inspiration home, translating runway and editorial trends into everyday wearable beauty. From the sophistication of downtown Pittsburgh to the casual charm of Wexford and Ross Township, we design hair that fits seamlessly into your lifestyle while reflecting who you are.


Each stylist takes pride in serving not just as a hair professional, but as a trusted part of the North Hills community.


The Products We Trust

Our stylists don’t just style hair — they curate entire experiences. Product selection is a key part of that process.


We proudly use and recommend:


  • R+Co — known for creative, performance-driven formulas that support every styling need, from texture to shine.

  • R+Co Bleu — the luxury extension of R+Co, combining sustainability with editorial-level results.

  • Wella Professionals — trusted worldwide for advanced color systems that prioritize hair health.

  • Aveda Color — plant-powered and cruelty-free color that delivers vibrant, natural brilliance.


Each product line reflects our commitment to clean beauty and exceptional performance. By blending artistry with science, we ensure your results last longer and your hair stays healthier between appointments.


What Defines a Craft Collective Stylist

Being part of Craft Collective Salon Group means embracing a certain standard — not just of skill, but of mindset. Our stylists share common traits that set our salon apart:


  • Creativity: A passion for exploring new techniques and trends.

  • Empathy: Listening deeply to understand every client’s goals.

  • Precision: Attention to detail in every snip, blend, and color melt.

  • Sustainability: A respect for the environment and clean salon practices.

  • Community: A belief in collaboration and growth — for both stylists and clients.


These values shape every service we provide. It’s why our clients describe their experience as both professional and personal — where expert craftsmanship meets genuine care.


Behind Every Great Style Is a Great Consultation

The foundation of every service at Craft Collective Salon Group is the consultation. Our stylists take time to discuss your goals, hair history, and maintenance preferences before picking up a single tool.


We use visual references, touch-based texture assessments, and digital color swatches to build clear expectations. This approach eliminates surprises and ensures your final look feels perfectly “you.”


Our consultations also extend to at-home care. We teach clients how to maintain their look with R+Co, R+Co Bleu, Wella, or Aveda products tailored to their texture, tone, and lifestyle.


A Culture of Care

We see every client as a relationship, not a transaction. Our stylists remember your formula, your preferences, your milestones — even your coffee order. This attention to detail is what makes our guests feel truly known and valued.


We’ve built a salon culture that celebrates authenticity. Our stylists bring their full personalities to work, which makes every appointment warm, conversational, and human. You’ll notice the difference the moment you walk through our doors — it’s not just about hair, it’s about connection.


Why Our Team Excels in Pittsburgh’s Beauty Scene

North Hills has become a destination for elevated beauty experiences, and Craft Collective Salon Group stands at the forefront of that movement. Our stylists are trusted not just for their artistry but for their consistency and professionalism.


We’ve developed a reputation as the salon where clients can relax, knowing they’re in skilled hands. From the newest stylist to the most experienced colorist, everyone at Craft Collective holds themselves to a shared standard of excellence — one that keeps us ranked among Pittsburgh’s best salons year after year.


Join the Collective

Whether you’re a stylist seeking a creative, supportive salon home or a client looking for your forever hair team, Craft Collective Salon Group is built for you. We believe that beauty grows through collaboration, and our team continues to expand with individuals who share our values of artistry, growth, and respect.


If you’re looking for a new stylist in North Hills Pittsburgh, visit Craft Collective Salon Group and experience a team that works together to make every appointment extraordinary.
