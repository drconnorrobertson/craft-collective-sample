Top 3 Blowout Styles for North Hills Events | Craft Collective Salon Group

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-25

When it comes to making a statement at a North Hills event — whether it’s a gala, wedding, or work celebration — a flawless blowout never goes out of style. At Craft Collective Salon Group, our McCandless and Ross Township stylists specialize in long-lasting blowouts designed to hold up to Pittsburgh’s humidity and long evenings.


Here are three styles our guests love for formal and everyday occasions alike.


1. The Classic Volume Blowout

Nothing says timeless elegance like full, smooth volume with soft body. Our stylists use R+Co Bleu’s Essential Hair Tonic for light hydration before blow-drying, followed by Wella’s EIMI Root Shoot mousse to add lift at the crown.


We finish with a round brush technique and a touch of R+Co’s “Outer Space” hairspray for a flexible hold that lasts from morning meetings to midnight dinners.


For parents or professionals who need something easy to maintain, this is the same salon-perfect finish featured in Why McCandless Loves Low-Maintenance Styles.


2. The Polished Wave Blowout

This is the go-to style for formal events like weddings, photoshoots, and holiday gatherings.

We start with Aveda’s Nutriplenish Leave-In Conditioner to smooth texture and protect from heat, then create soft waves using alternating curl directions for movement.


Our stylists customize wave intensity based on your face shape — tighter curls for glam looks or looser waves for effortless chic.


See similar finishes in Wedding Hair Trials: What to Expect Before the Big Day.


3. The Sleek Power Finish

For corporate events and professional gatherings, the Sleek Power Blowout delivers shine, polish, and control. It’s perfect for humid days or when you need a smooth, glass-like finish that reflects confidence.


We rely on Wella Professionals’ Ultimate Repair Miracle Hair Rescue for pre-blowout strength and R+Co Bleu’s “De Luxe Reparative Mask” for elasticity. A final touch of shine spray creates a photo-ready glow under any lighting.


For maintenance and frizz control, explore Best Hydrating Products for Dry Scalp — the hydration support behind this smooth finish.


Event-Ready Hair in McCandless and Ross Township

Whether you’re attending a fundraiser, family wedding, or corporate dinner, our North Hills team ensures your blowout lasts and feels light. Each service includes customized product pairing, heat protection, and finishing techniques to suit your personal look.
