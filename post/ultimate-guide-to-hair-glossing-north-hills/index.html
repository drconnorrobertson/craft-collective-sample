The Ultimate Guide to Hair Glossing in Pittsburgh’s North Hills | Craft Collective Salon Group

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-25

Hair glossing is the secret weapon behind that luminous, salon-perfect finish. Whether your goal is extra shine, color correction, or a seasonal tone refresh, a professional gloss service can completely transform the way your hair reflects light.


At Craft Collective Salon Group near Ross Park Mall, our stylists perform hundreds of gloss treatments each season — each customized to your hair color, texture, and lifestyle. Here’s your complete guide to understanding why glossing is the North Hills client favorite.


1. What Is a Hair Gloss?

A gloss is a semi-permanent conditioning treatment designed to enhance tone, smooth the cuticle, and add a reflective finish. Unlike traditional color, glossing doesn’t penetrate deeply — it coats and seals for instant radiance.


Glossing can:

  • Refresh faded highlights or balayage

  • Neutralize brassiness or warmth

  • Boost shine for natural hair

  • Smooth frizz and add softness


We use Wella Shinefinity and Aveda Glossing Treatments for their clean, ammonia-free formulas and incredible light reflection.


2. Who Benefits from Glossing?

Almost everyone. Glossing is ideal for clients who want vibrant, healthy-looking hair between major color appointments.


It’s especially popular among Ross Township brunettes looking to enhance dimension, and blondes who want to keep their highlights bright.


See examples of tone control in Balayage for Brunettes in Ross Township.


3. The Process: Quick, Gentle, and Transformative

Our glossing appointments take about 30 minutes. Your stylist will:

  1. Analyze your existing tone and texture.

  2. Apply a custom Wella or Aveda gloss mixture from root to end.

  3. Process it for 15–20 minutes.

  4. Rinse, condition, and finish with R+Co Bleu’s Optical Illusion Oil.


It’s the perfect add-on after a haircut or color, and it delivers results that last four to six weeks.


4. How Glossing Differs from Toner

Toners adjust tone (usually right after coloring), while glosses enhance shine and condition — though some products combine both.


For example, Wella Shinefinity adds soft tone correction and a glassy finish simultaneously. It’s less corrective than toner but more nourishing for overall health.


Learn more about maintaining tone and strength in How to Choose the Right Shampoo for Colored Hair.


5. Maintaining Your Gloss at Home

To extend the life of your gloss, use sulfate-free, pH-balanced shampoos and heat protection.Our stylists recommend:

  • R+Co “Gemstone” Shampoo and Conditioner

  • Aveda “Color Conserve”

  • Wella “Color Motion+” for bond care and brilliance


Avoid clarifying shampoos unless directed by your stylist — they can fade the gloss finish.Learn additional shine care in The Secret to Long-Lasting Shine (Without Heavy Oils).


6. Glossing for Every Season

  • Spring/Summer: Add warmth and gold for sunkissed brightness.

  • Fall/Winter: Cool down tone with mushroom or chestnut hues.

  • Holiday Season: Layer a clear gloss over your existing color for mirror-like reflection before events.


Our stylists plan gloss appointments strategically around seasonal weather and event calendars in Ross Township and McCandless.


See examples of seasonal tone shifts in How to Transition from Summer to Fall Color.


7. Sustainability in Every Service

Every gloss service at Craft Collective supports our commitment to sustainable beauty. We use refillable color tubes, recyclable packaging, and energy-efficient dryers.


Our partnership with R+Co, R+Co Bleu, Wella, and Aveda ensures each gloss is vegan, cruelty-free, and planet-conscious.


Learn more about our eco standards on the Sustainable Beauty Philosophy page.
