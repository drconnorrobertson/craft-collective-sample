Top Haircut Trends in North Hills Pittsburgh for Every Style and Lifestyle

Craft Collective Team · 2026-06-05

Finding a haircut that fits your lifestyle and personality is more than just a routine appointment, it’s about confidence, practicality, and self-expression. In North Hills Pittsburgh, clients are increasingly seeking salons that understand both modern trends and everyday needs. Craft Collective Salon Group has become a go-to destination for people looking for haircuts that are stylish, versatile, and tailored to each individual.


Why Your Haircut Should Fit Your Lifestyle

Every haircut tells a story, but it should also work for the life you live. Busy professionals in North Hills Pittsburgh want styles that look polished but don’t require hours of daily styling.


Parents need haircuts that are manageable for kids and themselves. Students often want something trendy yet easy to maintain. The best salons take all of these factors into account before picking up scissors.


At Craft Collective Salon Group, stylists consult closely with clients to understand their routines, styling habits, and goals. That way, every cut balances creativity with practicality, so it not only looks great in the salon but also grows out beautifully over time.


Current Haircut Trends in North Hills Pittsburgh

North Hills clients are embracing haircuts that are modern, versatile, and low-maintenance.


Some of the most popular styles include:

  • Textured Layers: Layers create movement and body, perfect for both straight and wavy hair. They add dimension without heavy styling.

  • Blunt Cuts: A sharp, clean line makes hair look thick and healthy. This works well for clients seeking a bold, defined look.

  • Face-Framing Layers: Strategically placed layers around the face accentuate features and soften angles, flattering all face shapes.

  • Shag Styles: Shags are making a comeback with a modern twist, offering effortless volume and edge.

  • Bobs and Lobs: Classic bob and long bob cuts remain popular for their versatility and sophistication.


Each trend can be adapted to suit different hair textures and lifestyles. North Hills clients often request a combination of techniques to ensure their haircut is truly personalized.


How Craft Collective Salon Group Personalizes Every Cut

Not all haircuts are created equal. The team at Craft Collective Salon Group begins every appointment with a detailed consultation. Stylists consider:


  • Hair type and texture

  • Face shape and facial features

  • Lifestyle and daily routine

  • Maintenance preferences


This process ensures that the haircut is tailored specifically to the client. Personalized cuts are easier to style, grow out naturally, and complement hair color choices, making them a long-term investment in appearance and confidence.


Why Regular Haircuts Matter in North Hills Pittsburgh

Even the best haircut loses its shape over time. Regular trims and maintenance appointments keep hair healthy and looking polished. In North Hills Pittsburgh, seasonal changes can also affect hair health, making consistent salon visits crucial.


Craft Collective encourages clients to schedule touch-ups based on their hair type and style, helping prevent split ends and maintain the haircut’s original shape.


Choosing the Right Salon in North Hills Pittsburgh

When selecting a salon, clients should look for more than skill alone. The right salon provides:

  • A professional yet welcoming environment

  • Stylists who listen and provide personalized guidance

  • Ongoing education and technique updates to stay ahead of trends

  • Consistency and quality with every visit


Craft Collective Salon Group embodies these qualities, which is why so many North Hills Pittsburgh residents trust the team with their hair.


Haircuts are more than just a service, they are an investment in how you feel every day.
