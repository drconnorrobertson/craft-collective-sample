Glossing & Shine Treatments in North Hills: Tone Refresh, Reflective Finish, and Color Enhancement

Craft Collective Team · 2025-12-13


Hair color is most beautiful when it shines, and achieving that reflective, healthy glow often comes down to one essential service: glossing.


At Craft Collective Salon Group in North Hills, our Glossing & Shine Treatments enhance tone, reduce frizz, smooth texture, and add incredible luminosity to your hair. These treatments are designed for blondes, brunettes, redheads, lived-in color clients, and anyone who wants their hair to look healthier, richer, and more expensive.


Whether you’re maintaining your balayage, toning down brass, adding richness back to brunette hair, or boosting shine on natural hair, a gloss is the fastest way to transform your look.


What Is a Hair Gloss?

A gloss is a demi-permanent treatment that:


– Enhances shine

– Adjusts or refreshes tone

– Reduces frizz

– Seals the cuticle

– Softens texture

– Adds a “camera-ready” finish

– Helps color last longer


Unlike permanent color, glosses contain no lift, making them gentle, conditioning, and ideal for all hair types, including extension hair.


Benefits of Glossing Treatments

Glosses create the kind of mirror-like shine and soft texture usually only seen in professional photos.


You’ll notice:


– Smoother hair

– Softer finish

– More consistent tone

– Less dryness

– A healthier appearance

– Better blending with extensions

– More definition in curls or waves

– Improved light reflection


Color looks richer. Blondes look brighter. Brunettes look deeper. And hair feels fresher instantly.


Glosses for Blondes: Brightening Without Damage

Blonde hair naturally loses tone and becomes brassy over time. Glossing restores:


– Cool, icy tones

– Creamy beige softness

– Neutral champagne blends

– Sun-kissed gold hues


Glosses are essential for blondes who want to protect their color between highlight appointments.

Glosses for Brunettes: Richness, Depth & Tone Control

Brunettes often struggle with:


– Warmth creeping in

– Dullness

– Flat color

– Uneven tone through mids and ends


A gloss brings richness back to your brunette by adding:


– Chocolate depth

– Caramel glow

– Smoky or cool undertones

– Espresso richness

– Multi-dimensional warmth


It restores the expensive-looking shine brunettes are known for.


Cross-link: Dimensional Brunette Techniques


Glossing for Extension Clients

Glossing is one of the most important services for hand-tied and tape-in extension wearers.

Benefits include:


– Matching the tone of natural hair with extension hair

– Adding shine and softness to extension ends

– Reducing dryness in extension wefts

– Smoothing texture for seamless blending

– Preventing color shift during wear


Many clients combine their extension move-up with a gloss for maximum impact.


Cross-link: Extension Care & Maintenance


Glossing for Natural Hair (No Color Added)

Even clients with uncolored hair benefit from a clear gloss.Clear glosses:


– Make hair shinier

– Reduce frizz

– Seal the cuticle

– Add a “polished” look

– Increase softness and slip

– Enhance curls and waves


It’s like laminating your hair, without altering your natural color.


How Long Does a Gloss Last?

On average, glosses last 4 to 6 weeks, depending on:


– Washing frequency

– Heat styling

– Sun exposure

– Water minerals

– Hair porosity

– Extension wear

– Hair-care products


Your stylist will recommend a maintenance schedule unique to your tone and hair type.


Cross-link: Product Education & Hair Care


When Should You Get a Gloss?

We recommend glossing if you:


– Want to refresh tone after highlights

– Notice brassiness or warmth

– Want your brunette to look richer

– Need to shine before an event

– Wear extensions

– Want your hair to look healthier instantly

– Prefer low-maintenance color

– Need to soften or calm the color that has grown out


Glossing is the #1 in-between service for all color clients.


Pairing Glossing With Other Treatments

Glosses pair exceptionally well with:


Hydration & Moisture Treatments

Glosses seal the cuticle, and moisture treatments ensure hydration is locked in.


Repair & Strengthening Treatments

Strengthen internally, gloss externally for full-spectrum hair restoration.


Scalp Health Treatments

Healthy roots + reflective lengths = complete beauty.


Sustainable Glossing Practices

In alignment with our Sustainable & Eco-Friendly Salon, our glosses include:


– Low-tox, ammonia-free formulas

– Vegan, cruelty-free ingredients

– Recyclable packaging

– Reduced chemical waste

– Eco-safe toning pigments


You get beautiful shine while supporting a healthier planet.
