Inside the Chair: How Craft Collective Stylists Create Signature Color Experiences | Craft Collective Salon Group

Craft Collective Team · 2025-10-26

What sets Craft Collective Salon Group apart isn’t just the result—it’s the process. Every color transformation starts with collaboration between stylist and client, guided by artistry, education, and sustainability.


Step 1: The Consultation

We begin every color service with a dialogue. Our stylists listen to your lifestyle, maintenance preferences, and tone goals. This ensures that your final color complements both your look and your routine.


Read more about our consultation approach in Professional Haircuts for Wexford Executives.


Step 2: Custom Formulation

Each stylist hand-mixes color formulas using Wella Professionals Shinefinity, Aveda Full Spectrum, and R+Co Bleu Toning Systems.We adjust pigment levels to match undertones and lighting conditions, ensuring multidimensional shine that lasts.


See how this technical artistry defines our reputation in Top Hair Colorists in North Hills: Meet the Experts Behind Craft Collective.


Step 3: Sustainable Application

Our salon practices use biodegradable foils, refillable glossing bottles, and eco-friendly water systems to reduce waste and preserve the environment.


Learn how sustainability informs every service in The North Hills Guide to Sustainable Hair Trends in 2025.


Step 4: Finishing with Intent

Every appointment concludes with a precision blowout using R+Co Bleu Optical Illusion Oil for gloss and Aveda Control Force for soft hold. Stylists teach simple at-home maintenance tips so you can preserve that salon-fresh look with ease.


Why Our Stylists Stand Out

Each member of our team is trained in color theory, chemistry, and long-term maintenance—ensuring your shade stays rich and balanced between visits.


Learn about the education culture that supports this standard in Inside Craft Collective: The Art of Teamwork and Education.


Experience Signature Color

At Craft Collective Salon Group, color isn’t applied—it’s designed.Book your personalized color experience today and discover why our stylists are known across Pittsburgh for artistry that lasts.
