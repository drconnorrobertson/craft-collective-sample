Sustainable & Eco-Friendly Hair Salon in North Hills: Conscious Beauty with Craft Collective Salon Group

Craft Collective Team · 2025-11-25


At Craft Collective Salon Group, beauty isn’t just about looking good; it’s about feeling good and doing good. As North Hills’ leading sustainable salon, we believe that every decision, from the products we use to the waste we manage, should support both your personal well-being and the planet we share.


Our eco-friendly philosophy guides everything we do. We’ve reimagined the salon experience to prove that you don’t have to sacrifice luxury to make ethical, sustainable choices.


Why Sustainability Matters in Modern Beauty

The beauty industry generates millions of pounds of waste each year, and color tubes, foils, plastics, and hair clippings often end up in landfills. We decided to change that story.


At Craft Collective, we view sustainability as a long-term commitment, not a marketing trend. Every bottle, brush, and bulb has been chosen with care to reduce our carbon footprint and inspire a new standard for North Hills salons.


Our mission is simple: create stunning results with conscious intention.


Eco-Friendly Practices That Define Craft Collective

Green Circle Salon Partnership

We proudly partner with Green Circle Salons, a national organization dedicated to recycling and repurposing up to 95% of salon waste. This includes hair clippings (used in oil-spill clean-up mats), foils, color tubes, plastics, and even gloves.


By joining this program, we’ve diverted thousands of pounds of waste from local landfills while educating both clients and stylists on responsible disposal.


Non-Toxic, Cruelty-Free Product Lines

We carefully select color and styling lines that are free of parabens, ammonia, and harsh sulfates. Our favorite brands use low-impact manufacturing and biodegradable packaging.

We believe healthy hair and healthy living go hand in hand, no compromise needed.


Energy-Efficient Operations

Our North Hills location is designed for minimal energy use. We’ve installed LED lighting, energy-saving dryers, and smart thermostats to ensure the salon operates efficiently without sacrificing comfort.


Every small improvement contributes to a cleaner, more sustainable future.


Water Conservation

Salon services rely heavily on water, so we’ve integrated EcoHeads and other conservation systems to reduce water waste during washing and rinsing. These innovative tools also purify and soften the water for a better experience that’s gentler on hair and the environment.


Refillable & Recyclable Packaging

We encourage clients to refill professional hair products and return used bottles for recycling. Our retail shelves feature brands that prioritize closed-loop systems, meaning packaging is repurposed rather than discarded.


Locally Sourced & Community-Driven

Sustainability also means supporting the local economy. We partner with Pittsburgh-area artisans and vendors to supply sustainable décor, locally made candles, and wellness goods. Every collaboration keeps resources circulating within the North Hills community.


The Client Experience: Beauty Without Waste

From the moment you walk in, you’ll notice that Craft Collective feels different, with fresh air, clean design, natural light, and a welcoming energy rooted in mindfulness.


Our stylists are trained to use products efficiently, minimize chemical runoff, and prioritize the long-term health of your hair and scalp. We believe that sustainability isn’t only environmental, it’s emotional, too. It’s about caring for yourself in a space built on respect and intention.


Sustainable Color & Treatment Options

We’ve tailored every color, lighting, and treatment process to balance performance with planet-friendly practices.


– Low-ammonia color systems reduce fumes and environmental toxicity.

– Recyclable foils and color tubes are collected after every service.

– Bond-building treatments reduce breakage, meaning fewer corrective appointments and less product waste.

– Eco-safe rinses limit chemical runoff into local water systems.


You’ll leave with hair that looks and feels healthy, knowing your beauty choices made a difference.


Building a Culture of Responsibility

Our sustainability initiative extends beyond the salon floor. Each team member receives continuous education on green practices and participates in community volunteer projects that align with our values.


We also host client education days to share tips on:

– Reducing single-use plastic at home

– Choosing clean beauty products

– Repurposing hair care containers

– Supporting local businesses committed to sustainability


When clients and stylists work together, small efforts become a meaningful impact.


How Sustainable Beauty Benefits You

Choosing an eco-friendly salon offers more than environmental satisfaction; it improves the health of your hair and scalp. Cleaner formulas mean fewer irritants, and sustainable practices often lead to more thoughtful service.


The air feels fresher, the water is softer, and your hair shines brighter. Sustainability simply looks better on everyone.


Our Long-Term Commitment

Sustainability isn’t static; it evolves. Craft Collective Salon Group continues to evaluate suppliers, invest in greener technologies, and lead by example within Pittsburgh’s salon community.


We aim to inspire both stylists and clients to choose consciously, one haircut or color at a time.


Book Your Eco-Friendly Salon Experience

If you’re searching for a sustainable hair salon in North Hills where artistry and environmental care meet, Craft Collective Salon Group welcomes you. Join us in redefining beauty as a force for good.
