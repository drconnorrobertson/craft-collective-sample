Wella Professionals & R+Co

On the artist teams

Two of our stylists sit on brand artist teams — the brands’ own rosters of working hairdressers. Kim Hughes is on the R+Co artist team; Sherry Maiolini is on the Wella Professionals artist team. It is how the techniques we use reach the floor here first.

R+Co Artist Team

Kim
Hughes

Stylist at Craft Collective

Seasoned artist elevating her craft since 2002. 20+ years behind the chair. Several New York Fashion Weeks for Zac Posen, Christopher Palu, Lujin Zhang, Korto Momolu. Has her Masters in Color, Cutting, and Styling from The Wella Studio. Wella Ambassador for Pittsburgh Fashion Week. Pittsburgh native passionate about education.
