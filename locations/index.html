  • The studio address on your reservation.

  • The complete estimate, appointment duration and preparation instructions.

Choosing between the studios

Start with where your selected artist works and the appointment you need. Check the route from your actual starting point, then allow time for the complete service. If you are unsure about availability or where to book, call 724-514-7231 before selecting a service.

For a substantial color change, new extensions or an unfamiliar booking label, explain your starting hair and goal first. The team can help identify which consultation or reservation fits.

Plan the service and budget

Browse the service menu, our artists and our work. The Craft pricing guide separates published amounts from services requiring an individual quote. Use the first-visit checklist when preparing for a new artist.
