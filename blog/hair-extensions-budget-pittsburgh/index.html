Hair Extensions in Pittsburgh: Plan the Full Budget

Craft Collective Salon Group · Updated October 1, 2026

The first installation is only one part of a hair-extension budget. When comparing Pittsburgh salons, ask about the hair itself, fitting, installation, blending, maintenance and eventual removal. A low starting price is not a useful comparison if the services included are different.

Craft Collective offers hair extensions. Your quote should follow a conversation about the result, your starting hair, method suitability and the upkeep you can commit to. There is no single price that fits every head of hair.

Separate the quote into understandable parts

  • Hair: length, amount, texture and color match.
  • Initial service: fitting, installation and any blending cut or finish.
  • Color work: whether matching your natural hair or the extension hair is a separate service.
  • Maintenance: the expected return schedule and what each visit includes.
  • Removal or replacement: who performs it and what is charged.
  • Home care: products or tools required for the selected method.

Ask which items are optional and which are required. If a package is offered, ask what happens when you need a different amount of hair or extra service time. Get the estimate for your proposed plan rather than a number based on an inspiration photo alone.

Volume and length can require different plans

Adding fullness to the existing length may involve a different amount of hair from creating a much longer result. Your natural density and haircut affect blending. Shorter layers, texture and the way you wear your hair can also influence placement.

Explain whether you want everyday fullness, extra length or a look for an event. Bring photographs with a similar starting texture and show how you normally wear your hair up. The stylist can then discuss a realistic plan and whether the method suits your hair.

Budget for maintenance before you install

Ask how often the chosen method needs professional maintenance and what conditions might bring that visit forward. The answer depends on the method, your natural growth and how the extensions are cared for. Do not assume every method has the same schedule.

Compare the cost across the period you intend to wear the extensions. Include installation, planned maintenance, any replacement hair and removal. This gives you a more useful number than the first appointment alone.

Questions that protect your decision

  • Is my hair suitable for this method and this amount of added hair?
  • Can I see work with a similar texture and goal?
  • What discomfort, slipping or tangling should prompt me to contact you?
  • What products, brushing and sleep routine should I follow?
  • Who should remove the extensions, and how do I book that service?

Compare proof alongside the price

Look at the salon gallery and read color and extension client feedback. A review can describe one client’s experience; it cannot guarantee your result or establish that every method is suitable for you.

At consultation, agree on the expected appearance, the upkeep and the full estimate. If you are not ready for ongoing maintenance, ask about alternatives before committing to installation.
