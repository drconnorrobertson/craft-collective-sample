Hair Extensions in Pittsburgh:
Everything You Need to Know

March 28, 2026 · By Derek Piekarski · Craft Collective Salon Group

Why Pittsburgh Clients Are Choosing Hair Extensions

Hair extensions have evolved dramatically in the last decade. They are no longer just for adding length. Modern extensions add volume, cover thinning areas, and allow for creative color without chemically treating your natural hair. At Craft Collective Salon Group, our extension specialists work with clients across Pittsburgh, from North Hills to Cranberry Township to South Hills, helping them achieve fuller, more versatile hair.

Types of Hair Extensions We Offer

There is no one-size-fits-all extension method. The right choice depends on your hair type, density, lifestyle, and goals. Here are the primary methods available at Craft Collective:

Hand-Tied Weft Extensions

Hand-tied wefts are sewn onto a beaded row installed in your hair. They lay flat against the head and distribute weight evenly, making them comfortable for all-day wear. This method works well for clients who want volume and length without visible attachment points.

Tape-In Extensions

Tape-in extensions use thin, adhesive-bonded wefts that sandwich small sections of your natural hair. They are quick to install, easy to maintain, and work especially well for fine or thin hair. Most tape-in clients come in for reinstallation every 6 to 8 weeks.

Keratin Bond Extensions

Individual keratin bonds offer the most natural movement and versatility. Each extension strand is bonded to a small section of natural hair using a keratin adhesive. This method is ideal for clients who want extensions that can be styled in updos, ponytails, and other versatile styles.

Why a Consultation Is Required

At Craft Collective, we require an in-person consultation before any extension service. During your consultation, our extension specialist will assess your natural hair health, density, and scalp condition. We will discuss your goals, recommend the best method, and provide accurate pricing based on the amount of hair you will need.

This step is essential because poorly matched or incorrectly installed extensions can damage your natural hair. We take extension work seriously and only proceed when we are confident the result will be beautiful and sustainable.

How Much Do Hair Extensions Cost in Pittsburgh?

Extension pricing varies significantly based on the method, length, and volume desired. Initial installation typically includes the cost of the hair itself plus the installation service. Maintenance appointments are needed every 6 to 10 weeks depending on the method. We provide a detailed cost breakdown during your consultation so there are no surprises.

Caring for Your Extensions

Proper aftercare is critical for extending the life of your extensions and protecting your natural hair. Our team provides detailed care instructions at your installation appointment. Key tips include using sulfate-free shampoo, brushing gently with an extension-safe brush, and sleeping with your hair in a loose braid. For more hair care guidance, visit our hair care tips page.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Which extension method is best for me?

It depends on your natural density, how you wear your hair and how much maintenance you want. Hand-tied wefts suit most medium-to-thick hair, tape-ins sit flattest on finer hair, and individual bonds give the most placement flexibility. A consultation is where this gets decided.

How much do extensions cost in Pittsburgh?

Two costs: the hair, and the labour to install it. Both scale with how much hair you need. Every extension client at Craft Collective starts with a consultation so we can colour-match and quote exactly. Call 724-514-7231.

How long do extensions take to install?

Two to four hours for a first full install, depending on method and how many rows you need. Move-up appointments afterwards are shorter.

Will extensions damage my hair?

Not if they are sized to your density and moved up on schedule. Damage comes from too much weight, rows set too tight, or leaving an install in past its move-up date.
