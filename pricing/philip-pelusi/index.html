Philip Pelusi Pricing: Published Menu & Cost Guide

Craft Collective Salon Group · October 24, 2026

Searching for Philip Pelusi prices near Pittsburgh area? Use the published examples below to identify the right questions for your appointment. The exact service label, artist level and included finish matter as much as the number beside the service.

This guide is published by Craft Collective Salon Group. Source reviewed October 24, 2026: Philip Pelusi official menu or business booking page. Amounts describe that source, not a guaranteed quote. We have not independently ranked the salon or established that Craft costs less.

Pricing structure: Published ranges; confirm the individual salon. “Quote not established here” means the reviewed page did not establish a numerical quote for that item; it is not a claim that no price exists elsewhere. A plus sign or “from” amount is a starting figure, not a ceiling.

Treat the group menu as a starting point

The official services page publishes ranges, but the location and designer still need to be confirmed. Ask whether those figures apply to your intended branch and present goal. Do not substitute a retail-product price from the shopping site for a salon-service estimate.

Request the complete appointment total

Send the salon a current photo, your recent color or treatment history and a description of the result you want. Say whether you are maintaining the current look or asking for a substantial change. Ask which artist and reservation fit that starting point, then request a complete estimate.

  • Is the listed amount for my chosen stylist and appointment location?
  • Does the quote include consultation, toning, cutting and the finish I want?
  • What additional time, product or preparation could change the estimate?
  • Which follow-up services should I budget for after this visit?

Compare with a Craft Collective appointment

Craft lists selected skincare, brow and lash prices on its service menu. Its reviewed hair menu does not establish fixed dollar quotes for most color, cut or extension appointments. Craft’s pricing guide separates published amounts from services needing an individual estimate.

Use the same goal and inclusions when requesting a Craft quote. North Hills has online booking; Canonsburg is by appointment and should be confirmed by phone at 724-514-7231. A missing public number cannot be treated as zero or as evidence that one salon is less expensive.

Check the repeat-visit budget

Ask about maintenance after the initial result, including which smaller services might be needed between larger visits. For extensions, separate hair purchase, installation, adjustments and removal. Compare totals over the same period and keep optional purchases separate. Confirm deposits and cancellation terms before reserving; a deposit credited toward the service is not an extra service charge.
