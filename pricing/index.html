How this directory treats prices

Each competitor page links the official menu or business booking page reviewed October 24, 2026. Starting figures, ranges, artist levels and exclusions remain attached to the published service. Conflicting amounts are identified rather than silently choosing the lower number. A quote not established on the reviewed page is not described as a free service or as proof that no pricing exists elsewhere.

The service tables are selected examples, not a complete market survey. They do not establish an average Pittsburgh rate, a cheapest provider or a price advantage for Craft. A color package with finishing and a base lightening charge have different scope. Use the linked sources and ask the salon to confirm what applies to your own appointment.

Turn the research into a booking decision

Choose the relevant service and artist first, then compare the full appointment and expected maintenance over the same period. Ask about toner, cutting, finishing, added hair and any separate provider you want to see. Confirm the final studio address and policies before reserving.
