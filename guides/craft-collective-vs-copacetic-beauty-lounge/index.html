What Copacetic highlights

Copacetic’s website describes a woman-owned North Hills salon, hourly color pricing, extension consultations, and hair-loss services including toppers and wigs. It also advertises same-day appointments, subject to availability. See Copacetic’s official website and its color service page.

Craft Collective offers haircuts, balayage, highlights, blonding, extensions and smoothing services. Explore the service menu and individual stylist profiles. Our locations are 2014D Babcock Blvd in the North Hills and 115 W Pike St in Canonsburg. Canonsburg is by appointment; confirm where your stylist works.

Compare the total color estimate

An hourly price and a service-menu price cannot be compared fairly without knowing the time and inclusions. Ask for an estimated total for the same goal at each salon. Explain your previous color, density, length and the change you want. Then ask what could change that estimate during consultation.

  • Are application and processing time both included in the estimate?
  • Are toner, finishing and any haircut included?
  • Does the plan involve one appointment or several?
  • What follow-up services should you budget for?

Extensions: ask about the whole cycle

Bring your desired length and fullness to the consultation, then discuss the hair purchase, installation, maintenance appointments and eventual removal. Confirm the method and who will maintain it. Ask to see work on hair with similar density to yours. A method name or introductory estimate does not establish the full ongoing commitment.

Our extension budget guide provides a checklist to use when comparing quotes. Craft’s extension page explains how to begin with a consultation.

Hair-loss needs deserve a specific conversation

Copacetic advertises hair-loss services. If toppers, wigs or a private consultation are central to your search, ask it directly about those offerings. Do not assume another salon offers the same services because it provides extensions. Ask Craft about your particular goal before booking.

Questions before choosing

Is Craft Collective cheaper than Copacetic Beauty Lounge?

We have not established that. Ask both salons for the total estimate for the same service plan, including finishing and expected maintenance. An advertised starting price is not necessarily your final quote.

Can I keep my current color when changing salons?

Describe the tone and placement you want to preserve and share your recent color history. Your new stylist can assess which reservation fits that goal. See our switching-salons checklist.

Which salon should I choose?

If toppers, wigs or a dedicated hair-loss consultation are your priority, check Copacetic’s advertised offerings. If you are comparing color or extensions, use the same goal and full-budget checklist at both salons.
