Salon Toner vs. Purple Shampoo: What to Book or Ask About

Craft Collective Salon Group · October 24, 2026

When blonde hair starts looking warmer than you want, buying a purple shampoo and booking a salon toner are different decisions. Start by describing what changed. Is the shade too yellow, the lightness different from your goal, or are some sections a different tone from others?

A home-maintenance product and a salon color plan

Redken describes purple shampoo as a pigmented cleanser used to counter yellow warmth. A salon toning appointment allows a colorist to assess and adjust the color plan. These roles overlap in tone maintenance, but they are not identical services. Read Redken’s explanation of gloss, toner and purple shampoo.

Describe the change before choosing a product

Tone-maintenance decisions
What you noticeWhat to ask
Yellow warmth on lighter piecesWould a recommended pigmented shampoo help maintain the planned tone?
Uneven warmth or different tones in sectionsWhich areas need assessment rather than the same product everywhere?
Hair looks less bright than the goalIs the concern tone, lightness or both?
Color no longer matches the referenceWould a gloss or toning visit fit, or is a different service needed?

What to bring to a salon conversation

Take photographs in similar daylight rather than comparing a warm bathroom picture with a professionally lit reference. Tell the stylist when the color was done, which areas changed first and what you have used since. Include pigmented shampoos, masks and any at-home color so the current appearance can be understood.

Point out what you liked immediately after the appointment. If the placement and lightness still work, say so; you may be asking to preserve those features while adjusting tone. If you want brighter pieces or a different root appearance, explain that separately.

Use home care according to the actual product

Ask your stylist which product, frequency and directions apply to your hair and follow the manufacturer’s instructions. Do not assume more frequent use will create a better result. If you dislike the tone after using a pigmented product, explain what you used before adding more products or booking a corrective appointment.

Purple shampoo is a maintenance discussion, not a reason to promise the same result as a custom salon service. Ask whether the recommendation is meant to maintain your current shade, change its appearance slightly or bridge the time until an assessment.

Choose the right reservation

Tell the salon whether you want only a tone refresh or also a haircut, root work or lightening. Confirm what is included in a gloss or toner estimate and whether a finish is separate. At Craft, call if the booking label is unclear rather than choosing a smaller appointment for a larger color goal.
