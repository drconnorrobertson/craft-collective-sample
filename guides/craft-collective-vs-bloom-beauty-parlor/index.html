Craft Collective vs. BLOOM Beauty Parlor in Pittsburgh

Published by Craft Collective Salon Group · Sources checked October 1, 2026

Looking for an alternative to BLOOM Beauty Parlor, or deciding whether to travel between East Liberty and the North Hills for your hair appointment? Consider both the result and the routine. A salon you can return to comfortably may be a better fit for frequent maintenance.

This comparison is published by Craft Collective Salon Group. We have not tested or independently rated the other salon. Details come from the linked official pages checked October 1, 2026; menus, prices, staff and availability can change.

Which BLOOM does this page cover?

This page refers to BLOOM Beauty Parlor at 214 N. Highland Avenue in East Liberty. Its website emphasizes sustainability, cruelty-free practices and an inclusive setting, with online booking and text help choosing a service. See BLOOM Beauty Parlor’s official website. Other businesses also use the name Bloom; confirm the address before reserving.

Craft Collective offers haircuts, balayage, highlights, blonding, extensions and smoothing services. Explore the service menu and individual stylist profiles. Our locations are 2014D Babcock Blvd in the North Hills and 115 W Pike St in Canonsburg. Canonsburg is by appointment; confirm where your stylist works.

Neighborhood convenience and upkeep

Think beyond the first appointment when choosing a location. Ask how often your color, haircut or extensions may need salon maintenance, then consider the travel that schedule creates. Check the salon’s current hours and any parking or access details directly before your visit. We have not measured route times between these salons.

  • Can you return for the recommended maintenance appointments?
  • Does the chosen stylist work at the location you intend to visit?
  • How much appointment time can you reserve alongside travel?
  • Would a smaller refresh fit your schedule better than a major change?

Ask about the salon practices you value

If environmental practices or product preferences shape your choice, ask each salon what those practices mean for the service you want. Discuss the products involved, any preferences you have and what the salon can accommodate. We do not claim Craft has the same sustainability certifications or practices as BLOOM.

Compare color plans with the same brief

Give each stylist the same starting information: your recent hair history, desired tone, budget and maintenance preference. Ask what is included in the estimate and whether the plan needs several visits. A consultation lets you compare proposed work instead of assuming that similarly named services are identical.

When to explore Craft as an alternative

Craft may belong on your shortlist if our North Hills or Canonsburg location works for you and you like the work in our gallery. Review individual stylist profiles and confirm the appointment plan. Choose the combination of work, setting and maintenance that fits your priorities.
