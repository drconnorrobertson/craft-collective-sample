South Hills Salon Alternatives & Comparisons

Craft Collective Salon Group · October 24, 2026

A South Hills salon search can include Mt. Lebanon, McMurray and Canonsburg, but those are different appointment destinations. Use the services you need and the places you routinely travel to make a shortlist before comparing estimates.

This guide is published by Craft Collective Salon Group. It brings together source-linked comparisons and does not independently rank local salons.

Start with the relevant local comparisons

  • Geno Levi Salon vs. Craft Collective — McMurray and Canonsburg
  • La Pomponnée vs. Craft Collective — McMurray and Mt. Lebanon
  • Salon Vivace vs. Craft Collective — Mt. Lebanon

Match the service to the reason for your search

For recurring color, compare the first visit and the maintenance plan. For extensions, separate the hair purchase and installation from future adjustments and removal. For a haircut, compare the actual stylist’s work and whether the finish you want is included. If you want spa, barbering or event services, verify each category directly rather than assuming all salons offer the same combination.

Craft’s Canonsburg appointment option

Craft Collective’s studios are North Hills at 2014D Babcock Boulevard, Pittsburgh and Canonsburg at 115 W Pike Street. North Hills has online booking; call 724-514-7231 for Canonsburg appointments or help choosing a service. Confirm the studio where your stylist works.

Canonsburg is by appointment only. Call before traveling, selecting a complicated service or arranging several appointments around the visit. Explain whether you are maintaining an existing look or changing direction, then confirm which stylist, service block and preparation instructions fit the plan. North Hills online availability should not be treated as the Canonsburg schedule.

A useful brief for each salon

Send a current photograph, one or two references, your recent hair history and a comfortable budget. Explain how you wear your hair most days and how often you can return. Give each salon the same information so the proposed work can be compared fairly.

If one salon proposes several visits and another proposes a smaller single appointment, ask which result each plan is intended to achieve. A difference in scope can explain a price difference. Use the quote worksheet to record those details before deciding.
