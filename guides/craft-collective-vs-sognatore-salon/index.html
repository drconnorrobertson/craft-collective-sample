Included finishing can change the comparison

Sognatore’s separate color-and-cut and color-and-blow-dry listings show why menu totals need context. If you want a haircut at the same visit, compare the version that includes it. If you only want to maintain your current color, ask whether a cut is needed at that visit. For Craft, request the corresponding complete appointment rather than comparing a bare color service with a finished package elsewhere.

Balayage and extensions need different questions

For balayage, identify how much brightness you want and where it should start. Ask how toning and any root work are handled. For extensions, Sognatore names Great Lengths; confirm the available method and ongoing care with it. Ask Craft which methods are suitable and available for your own density, length and routine. Two salons advertising extensions may propose different attachment systems and maintenance commitments.

How Craft may fit your shortlist

Explore Sognatore when its advertised services, artist portfolio or appointment packaging suit your plan. Add Craft when a North Hills or Canonsburg visit works for you and an individual stylist’s work matches your goal. A service list alone does not establish a quality or price winner.

Craft Collective’s studios are North Hills at 2014D Babcock Boulevard, Pittsburgh and Canonsburg at 115 W Pike Street. North Hills has online booking; call 724-514-7231 for Canonsburg appointments or help choosing a service. Confirm the studio where your stylist works.

Make the final consultation specific

Write down what you want to preserve as well as what you want to change. A new salon can maintain a shade or shape you already enjoy; changing stylists does not require a complete transformation. Ask to see relevant work from the artist you would book.

  • Which service and stylist match my starting hair and desired finish?
  • What is included in the total estimate, and what could change it?
  • What can be achieved at the first appointment, and what may need another visit?
  • What follow-up appointments and home routine should I expect?
