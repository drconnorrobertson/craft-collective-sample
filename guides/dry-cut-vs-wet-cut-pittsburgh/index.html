Dry Cut vs. Wet Cut: Pittsburgh Haircut Consultation Guide

Craft Collective Salon Group · October 24, 2026

A dry haircut and a wet haircut describe how the hair is prepared during cutting. They are not automatic measures of quality, price or suitability for every curl pattern. The best starting point is the shape you want and how you normally wear your hair.

How the approaches differ

L’Oréal’s professional hair publication describes dry cutting as a way to see the hair’s movement and usual fall, while wet cutting can help establish a defined outline. It also discusses combining the methods. See the wet-versus-dry cutting explanation.

If you wear your natural curls or waves

Show the stylist what your hair looks like with your normal routine, including the areas that sit shorter or wider. Explain whether you also straighten it sometimes. Ask to see examples on comparable texture and clarify how the cut will be judged in the finish you wear most often.

Do not change your arrival routine based on a generic article. One stylist may want clean, naturally dried hair; another may plan to wash it before cutting. Ask the person you are booking for specific preparation instructions, including products, detangling and whether to avoid tying it back tightly.

If you want a precise short outline

Bring side and back views of the reference, especially for a bob or a cropped shape. Agree on where the perimeter should sit and how the neckline and pieces around the ears should look. Discuss how the shape behaves when you air-dry, tuck it behind your ears or wear a different part.

Ask the stylist to explain the role of wet and dry work in that plan. A combined approach can be part of one appointment. Avoid booking a method solely because it sounds more specialized if the reservation does not fit the shape or finish you want.

Check the quote and the finish

Confirm whether the estimate includes consultation, shampoo, cutting and the final style. A dry-cut label alone does not tell you whether washing is included, and a standard haircut label does not tell you whether the stylist will refine the shape dry. Ask what to reserve and how long to allow for the complete visit.
