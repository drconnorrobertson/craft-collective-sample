Switching Hair Salons in Pittsburgh: A Practical Checklist

Published by Craft Collective Salon Group · Sources checked October 1, 2026

Changing salons can be a practical choice when your schedule, location, budget or hair goals change. Before booking a new Pittsburgh stylist, write down what you want to keep and what you want to change. That gives the first consultation a clearer starting point.

Bring the history behind your current hair

  • When was your most recent cut and color, and what was done?
  • Have you used at-home dye, lightener, henna or direct color?
  • Have you had smoothing treatments or other chemical services?
  • Do you currently wear extensions, and what method and maintenance do they require?
  • What styling tools and products do you regularly use?

If you know your previous formula or extension details, bring them. If you do not, say so. Avoid guessing product names or concealing at-home color; the stylist needs accurate information to plan the work.

Separate a new stylist from a complete transformation

You can change salons and keep the shape or shade you already like. Describe the parts that work: the length around your face, your root tone or how your hair behaves between washes. Then name the specific issue you want help with. A targeted adjustment may require a different reservation from a full transformation.

Ask for the plan and estimate before booking

  • Which service should I reserve for this starting point and goal?
  • What is included in the estimated total?
  • How long should I allow for the complete appointment?
  • What may need to happen over more than one visit?
  • What follow-up visits and home routine should I expect?

Confirm the stylist, address and current policies. If you need help choosing a Craft appointment, call 724-514-7231 rather than selecting an unfamiliar service solely by its name.

Use comparisons to make a shortlist

Our Pittsburgh salon alternatives guide links to researched comparisons. Use those pages to identify questions and location differences, then check availability directly. A published comparison does not replace a conversation with the person who will perform your service.

Prepare for the first visit

Bring daylight photographs of your current hair and a few examples of your goal. Explain how you normally wear your hair and how much time you spend styling it. After consultation, agree on the service and estimate before proceeding. See our first-visit checklist and inspiration-photo guide for more preparation ideas.
