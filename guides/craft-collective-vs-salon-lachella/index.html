Where Craft fits

Craft Collective has studios at 2014D Babcock Boulevard in Pittsburgh’s North Hills and 115 W Pike Street in Canonsburg. Our service menu includes color, balayage, highlights, haircuts, extensions and smoothing services. Confirm your stylist’s location and the services included in your individual estimate.

Everyday color or a special occasion?

For routine color, ask for the total including any toning, cut and finish. For an event, ask whether the reservation includes a trial and how many people can be accommodated. Salon Lachella advertises bridal styling; confirm the current arrangements with it. At Craft, discuss the event date, location, party size and desired finish before assuming a standard styling reservation covers the plan.

Compare the full plan

Give both salons the same brief: a daylight photo of your current hair, recent color and chemical history, inspiration images, your budget and how often you can return. Ask which parts of the inspiration are realistic at the first visit and what may need several appointments.

  • Which stylist has examples close to my starting hair and goal?
  • Does the estimate include toner, a haircut and blow-dry, where needed?
  • What maintenance visits and home care should I expect?
  • What are the cancellation and rescheduling terms for this reservation?

Which salon should you choose?

Salon Lachella may fit a Cranberry-based routine or an event plan that matches its current offering. Consider Craft when you prefer our North Hills or Canonsburg studio and have confirmed the stylist, appointment length and arrangements.

We have not established that Craft is less expensive or has faster availability. Compare actual estimates and appointment options for your specific service. If you are keeping your current shade, tell your new stylist what you like about it as well as what you want to change.
