Cranberry Hair Salon Alternatives & Comparisons

Craft Collective Salon Group · October 24, 2026

Comparing hair salons near Cranberry? Use your appointment goal to narrow the field. This guide brings together options for a Cranberry appointment, nearby Wexford options, or a North Hills salon. It is published by Craft Collective and is not an independent ranking.

Choose your appointment before your technique

Write down the result you want in plain language: brighter around the face, less visible root contrast, more fullness, a shorter shape or easier day-to-day styling. A stylist can then explain the appointment that matches it. Two salons may use different names for a similar result, or include different services in the same label.

Compare location and a realistic schedule

Craft Collective has studios at 2014D Babcock Boulevard in Pittsburgh’s North Hills and 115 W Pike Street in Canonsburg. Our service menu includes color, balayage, highlights, haircuts, extensions and smoothing services. Confirm your stylist’s location and the services included in your individual estimate.

Craft’s North Hills studio is on Babcock Boulevard; it is not a Cranberry storefront. Check the route from your actual starting point and allow for the whole appointment. Confirm the studio before paying a deposit or arranging transportation. Appointment availability changes, so contact the salon rather than relying on a published article for open times.

Bring a brief that makes comparisons useful

  • Your starting hair and recent color or chemical history.
  • The result you want and the parts of your current look to preserve.
  • Your comfortable budget and maximum appointment length.
  • How frequently you want to return and how you style at home.

Ask each salon for the full estimate using that same information. Include toner, finishing and follow-up visits in the discussion. Use our price comparison checklist to keep the scope consistent.

Look at individual work

Review examples from the person you may book with. For curls, compare texture and how the hair is worn; for color, compare the starting shade and placement; for extensions, compare density and blending. If a particular service is essential, confirm it directly rather than inferring it from an overall salon description.
