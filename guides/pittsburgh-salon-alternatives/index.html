Compare the scope, then the estimate

Send each salon the same current hair photos, recent service history and target look. Ask for the initial visit and the maintenance plan over the same period. Include toning, cutting and finishing where needed. For extensions, confirm the hair purchase, installation, adjustments and removal.

Use the interactive quote worksheet or read the estimate checklist. A lower price is only meaningful when you understand what work it covers.
